# Source precedence: most official, then most recent

When Sources disagree, the Knowledge Base takes them in this order: game data (as extracted by Toolbox) > the IdleOn wiki, if edited after the relevant patch > dated community guides (Reddit, Steam, the community progression guide) > anything else; within a tier, the newer wins. Every entry records the patch it was verified against, so superseded mechanics (e.g. class-change level requirements that moved) surface as stale rather than as confident advice. This exists because AI research tends to return outdated stats and older mechanics under the same names.

**Amendment (2026-10-17, M11 D-2):** this order settles facts only. Judgment entries (Priority Lists, Builds, the Meta Path) are Contested whenever two or more Sources dated after the current major patch disagree, whatever their tier or date, because recency changes the game, not the quality of an opinion.

**Amendment (2026-10-19, M26 FR-2b, owner Q10):** "game data" also covers a read of the game client itself, the web build's script on legendsofidleon.com, as a tier 1 Source. Toolbox stays the preferred tier 1 citation; a client read is for what Toolbox does not extract or gets wrong. Such a Source must be checkable: its `url` is the client file, `excerptSha256` is the hash of the excerpt we stored (`docs/research/game-client/excerpts/<sha256>.txt`, at most 2 KB, a citation and never a copy of the client), `clientBuild` gives the file's sha256 and Last-Modified date, and `gameVersion` is the version that build was matched to in `docs/research/game-client/builds.json`. A build not yet matched to a game version is cited at tier 4 and backs display only. `kb check` verifies the excerpt hash, its size and the build row. The first recorded build (N.js, sha256 `42da3130…`, Last-Modified 2026-10-17) was matched to 2.3.540 by content on 2026-10-19 (M26 V43): the client ships no version literal.
