Forge live checks

Three items Claude can't do alone. About 5 minutes of your attention; part 2 then runs on its own for roughly 75 minutes in the background.

Never paste tokens or codes into chat. The only thing you type into Claude is the short phrase at the end of each part.

Part 1 · ~2 min

Toolbox mirror token

Lets the weekly sync keep our backup copy of IdleOn Toolbox up to date. It can only touch that one mirror repo.

Part 2 · ~3 min of you, then ~75 min on its own

Character-switch recording + token-expiry check

One local script does both. It records your raw save each time it changes (for the "Focus follows the game" feature), then keeps your original login token until it expires and checks that the game's database refuses it 1, 5 and 15 minutes later (the safety margin the Suggestions feature relies on). Read-only, your own account, nothing leaves your Mac.

Part 3

Nothing else

Part 2 covers both of the "by hand" checks the specs asked for. Results stay in fixtures/private/live-checks/ (gitignored) and only the outcome is recorded on the specs.
