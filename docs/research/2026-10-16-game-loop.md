# Game loop and systems for Worlds 1–3 on the current patch (2026-10-16)

Ticket: [#2](https://github.com/YassSoliman/idleon-forge/issues/2), part of map [#1](https://github.com/YassSoliman/idleon-forge/issues/1). Researched and **verified on 2026-10-16** against patch **"Lumi Menagerie" (Steam, 2026-10-16)**. The last patch the wiki has numbered is **v1.22 "Jelly Operator" (2026-09-19)**. Terms follow [`GLOSSARY.md`](../../GLOSSARY.md), and conflicts are settled by [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-source-precedence.md): game data (via Toolbox) beats the wiki edited after the patch, which beats dated guides.

## Answer

- **A Play Session is a stockpile-drain loop followed by a re-park.** On login, each character claims its AFK gains (resources, coins, drops, EXP and cards). The player then spends what built up while AFK, in roughly this order:
  1. Anvil production, then the forge.
  2. Stamps, Upgrade Vault and bribes, paid in coins.
  3. Alchemy: the cauldron bar, liquids and vials.
  4. Post Office orders.
  5. Arcade balls and the Owl and Poppy clickers.
  6. W3 jobs: construction builds, cogs and flags, refinery ranks, library books, traps, worship charge and the equinox bar.
  7. Daily and weekly claims.

  Last comes the **AFK Setup**: every character back on its best map, skill or station, with the right talent preset, cards, obols and food. Toolbox's alert engine already encodes almost exactly this checklist ([§5](#5-toolbox-already-encodes-the-session-checklist)).
- **The main work in a session is putting things back before they hit their cap.** Many W1–3 stockpiles stop accruing at a cap, so AFK time past it is wasted:
  - anvil production (capped by carry capacity)
  - liquid cauldrons
  - uncollected arcade balls
  - cogs waiting on the shelf (each one doubles the cost of the next)
  - forge slots
  - refinery fuel in storage
  - full inventories
  - a prayer curse that caps AFK at 10 hours

  A good AFK Setup is mostly "nothing will cap before the next session".
- **Seven systems are per-character, and an AFK Setup has to cover each character on them:** talents and presets, card loadout, obols, active bubbles, anvil production, post-office box points, and worship skull/charge. Everything else (stamps, statues once gold, bubbles, vials, vault, construction, refinery, printer and others) is account-wide.
- **Class gates on the current patch:**
  - First class: **Level 10** (Promotheus, game data and wiki).
  - Subclass (Speccius, W2): an item quest of **100 Kebab Sticks and 50 Crabby Cakey**, with **no level gate listed**.
  - Elite class: Eliteus, whose quest is the Greenie Gang.
  - Master class: Masterius in W6.
  - **I found no patch from 2021 to 2026 that changed the first-class level.** If it changed, the change was only on Discord or in-game. The ticket's premise that it changed is therefore **unconfirmed**.
  - What *did* change: the 2nd talent preset unlocks at Lv 15 (it used to be 30), and master-class quests got easier (Royal Guardian orbs went from 600 to 250, Death Bringer from 92 to 25 monsters).
- **The game is far past where older guides stop.**
  - Version numbering **restarted at v1.00 with World 7** (2025-11-06), so a guide citing "v2.xx" predates W7.
  - There are now 7 worlds, 20 skills (Spelunking and Research are new), **11 character slots** (since 2026-05-29) and 4 master classes.
- **Recent changes that make older W1–3 advice wrong:**
  - W1–3 monster respawn was "severely lowered" (2026-03-29).
  - W1–3 card drop rates were buffed "tremendously" (2026-03-13).
  - The **Upgrade Vault** (2025-02, an early-game coin sink) arrived, and the anvil can be used from anywhere.
  - Auto-loot is instant, and Drop Rarity and Drop Chance merged into a single Drop Rate stat (2026-03-06).
  - **Statues drop from crystal mobs, not skilling** (2025-11-15).
  - **Construction costs only refinery salts**, not materials (2026-02-20).
  - Salt Lick costs only salt (2026-06-06).
  - Greenstacks are permanent (2026-06-01).
  - Charge Syphon was reworked and Time Candies no longer give worship charge (2026-02-20).
  - Lord of the Hunt no longer gates critters (2025-07-06).
  - Companions were renamed Pets (2026-02-20), and the free pet is now daily (2026-08-25).
- **Daily resets matter for the Next Action engine and are mostly use-it-or-lose-it.** Each one needs its own Toolbox-derived check:
  - Daily: merit tasks per world, the Picnic Stowaway quest, boss keys from Dog Bone, Djonnut and Bellows (each banks up to 3 days), colosseum tickets, the shop restock, 5 minigame plays, Post Office orders, vial attempts, Liquid Shop prices, the W2 boss-raid reset, Dr Defecaus and Baba Yaga, the 150 boss gem fights, and the free pet.
  - Weekly: Killroy (skulls reset), Weekly Battles trophies, the Bonus Ballot vote, dungeon boosted runs (12 free), Shimmer and Rando islands, and the gem-shop resets.
- **Some systems have gates that break the per-world mental model.** These need explicit "not yet" handling in the Knowledge Base:
  - Sigils sit in W2 alchemy but **need the W4 Lab** to level.
  - Equinox dreams added in 2026 need **W7 Research G8**.
  - The Killroy room 2 unlocks at W4.
  - Stamp gilding comes from W4 Rift 26.
- **Some unknowns need in-game confirmation before they go in the Knowledge Base:**
  - the maximum claimable AFK hours
  - the clock time of the daily reset (it seems tied to the account or save via `ShopRestock`)
  - the subclass and elite level gates
  - whether character-slot unlock levels are cumulative
  - whether manual stat points exist (the wiki says stats come from talents and systems)
  - what "Buy Upgrades ≥ 25" in the class quest refers to
- **For later decision tickets: reuse Toolbox's alert definitions as the seed of the Knowledge Base's "stockpile ready" rules.**
  - `utility/dashboard/alertMeta.js`, `account.js` and `characters.js` list about 60 per-account and per-character conditions with thresholds, already maintained within days of each patch.
  - They are GPL-3.0, which is compatible under ADR 0001.
  - They also gate W(n+1) alerts on `finishedWorlds.World(n)`, which is the same staging ActiveOn needs.

## 2. What a Play Session consists of

These steps come from Toolbox's alert set **[TB:alertMeta.js]**, ordered by the wiki's [Daily Checklist](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Daily_Checklist) (2026-03-24, older than the latest patches). The order is a starting proposal for the Meta Path, not a sourced ranking.

1. **Claim.** Switching to each character claims what it gathered while AFK: items, coins, EXP and card drops. Shrines level up on claims, worship charge accrues, and AFK constellations need one unbroken AFK stretch [W:Tips_and_Tricks 2025-12-24; W:Construction 2026-10-07; W:Star_Signs 2026-10-05].
2. **Empty and deposit.** Send inventories to storage, collect anvil production on every character, and collect forge bars and refill the ore. Deposit statues and items the Slab or Gallery can use [W:Smithing 2026-10-05; W:Forging 2026-10-05; W:Statues 2026-10-14].
3. **Spend coins.** Stamps, Upgrade Vault, bribes, then Liquid Shop and P2W upgrades [W:Stamps 2026-10-05; W:Upgrade_Vault 2026-10-07; TB:alertMeta `account.World 1.stamps`, `account.World 2.alchemy.p2wUpgrades`].
4. **Spend resources and liquids.**
   - Alchemy: spend the cauldron bar on a new bubble or a boost, level bubbles and vials, and use vial attempts.
   - Post Office: fill the daily orders.
   - Construction: buildings, refinery ranks, salt balance, cogs and flags.
   - 3D printer: re-sample.
   - Library: book checkouts.
   - Atom collider: buy upgrades.

   [W:Alchemy 2026-10-05; W:Post_Office 2026-10-07; W:Construction 2026-10-07; W:3D_Printer 2026-10-14; W:Talent_Book_Library 2026-10-12]
5. **Per-character spends:**
   - unspent talent points
   - anvil points
   - post-office box points
   - empty obol, star sign and bubble slots
   - better tools
   - unequipped gear slots
   - carry-bag tiers

   [TB:alertMeta `characters.*`]
6. **Clickers and dailies:**
   - Owl and Poppy restarts.
   - Arcade balls, before the uncollected cap fills.
   - Picnic Stowaway daily quest.
   - Boss keys and colosseum tickets, then the boss fights.
   - Daily merit tasks.
   - W2 boss raid reset.
   - Dr Defecaus and Baba Yaga.
   - Random event.
   - Free pet.

   [TB:alertMeta `account.General.etc`, `account.World 1.owl`, `account.World 2.*`]
7. **Weeklies, when due:** Killroy run and skulls, Weekly Battles, Bonus Ballot vote, dungeon happy hour, Shimmer trial, and guild tasks [TB:alertMeta; W:Daily_Checklist].
8. **Active progression**, when there's time: push a new map, quest, or boss.
9. **AFK Setup.** For each character:
   - Choose the AFK target: a map (FIGHTING) or a skill node (MINING / CHOPPIN / FISHING / CATCHING), or a W3 station (cog board, cauldron).
   - Load the matching talent preset, card set, obols and food.
   - Turn on timed buffs, which "last the entire duration of your AFK".
   - Keep inventory and carry capacity free.
   - Check prayer curses.
   - Re-place traps on long timers.
   - Fully close the game, because trap timers stop on the character-select screen.

   [TB:parsers/character.ts `afkType`; W:Tips_and_Tricks 2025-12-24; W:Prayers 2026-10-03; W:Trapping 2026-10-07]

The AFK targets a character can have, taken from monster definitions in game data, are FIGHTING (312 maps/monsters), CHOPPIN, CATCHING, MINING, FISHING, SPELUNKING, COOKING, LABORATORY, DIVINITY and "Paying_Respect" [TB:data/website-data/monsters.json `AFKtype`]. AFK gain rate is base 2 plus additive sources:

- the W1 task bonus, the Beast Master family bonus and guild bonus 7
- cards and card set 5
- the Sleepin' On The Job talent and the Dream Catcher sigil
- lab chip 8, gear, obols and prayers
- arcade, Flurbo, divinity and pet (companion) bonuses

[TB:parsers/character.ts `getAfkGain`, lines ~3200–3240].

## 5. Toolbox already encodes the session checklist

`utility/dashboard/alertMeta.js` at [`f1e4240`](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/f1e4240a64b8e90ae21acb5dadc778cd66dc0f38/utility/dashboard/alertMeta.js) defines its alerts in three groups:

- **Account alerts**, sectioned General / World 1–7. The W1–3 entries are: tasks, guild, shops, misc dailies, stamps, owl, forge, alchemy, islands, postOffice, arcade, weeklyBosses, killRoy, kangaroo, printer, library, construction, hatRack, equinox, atomCollider and traps.
- **Per-character alerts:** cards, anvil, worship, traps, quests, alchemy, obols, postOffice, starSigns, tools, talents, equipment, bags and classSpecific.
- **Timers:** daily, weekly, the closest trap, flag, building and salt, the equinox bar, and others.

`account.js` returns early from the W(n+1) checks unless `account.finishedWorlds.World(n)` is set (for example, line ~440 checks World1 before running the W2 alerts). Each alert carries a user threshold (minutes before full, % full, N books, unspent points).

**Implication for ActiveOn:**

- Almost every "stockpile ready to spend" and "AFK Setup broken" condition for W1–3 already exists as tested logic over the parsed Account State, maintained within days of each patch (git history shows bursts after updates; see [ecosystem research](2026-10-16-ecosystem.md)).
- These conditions are a starting set of Next Action triggers. The Knowledge Base still has to supply the **priority and the "why"**, which Toolbox does not give.
- This is a lead for the architecture tickets, not a decision.
