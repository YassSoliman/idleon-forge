## Post Office

- **What it is:** six shipping companies post a daily order each. Filling one gives delivery boxes (points). Every character spends the account's whole box total on its own 24 upgradeable boxes, three bonuses each. [C1][C2][C3]
- **Unlocks:** Postboy Pablob's quest "Signed, Sealed and Lost in the Post" needs 5 Empty Boxes (item `Quest13`, craft requirement 15) from Anvil Tab 2; rewards 10,000 class EXP, 75 gems, 5 x 2 HR Time Candy [C21][C22]. He appears at class level 50 (decompiled game gate, via the earlier W1 dossier; not re-verified in this pass) [C24]. W2 Town per the wiki [C23].
- **Scope:** orders, streaks, shields, Silver Pens and box income are account-wide; box spending is per character, and each character can spend the full account total [C1][C2].
- **Inputs to outputs:** items in the storage chest (no carrying) fill orders [C25]; each order gives 1 box [C30], plus streak bonuses [C28], plus company rewards (stones, potions, keys, statues) [C31]. Extra box sources: Slug Slurp vial, option slots 131 and 347 [C1][C17][C36]. Box levels give permanent bonuses [C5][C6].
- **Goals and Milestones:**

| Milestone | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Bonus 2 starts paying | box level 26 (formula uses level minus 24.9, rounded) | [C5] |
| Bonus 3 starts paying | box level 101 | [C5] |
| Main cap | 400 on 20 boxes, 800 on 3 (Scurvy, Gosh, Gaming), 100,000 on Myriad | [C4] |
| Myriad bonus thresholds | bonus 2 at 101, bonus 3 at 301; 800-cap boxes at 51 and 201 | [C4][C5] |
| Streak bonuses | +1 / +3 / +5 / +9 at fixed streaks | [C28] |

| Silver Pens achievement "Ink Blot" | 101 pens used: 20% chance to keep a pen | [C19] |

- **Feeds / fed by:** fed by storage stockpiles (W1-W4 skilling resources, crafted gear, monster drops, rare 1-in-20K drops) [C30], the Slug Slurp vial (Alchemy) [C17], Silver Pens [C14]. Feeds damage, HP/MP, efficiency, AFK gain, Smithing/Alchemy/Trapping/Cooking/Lab/Construction/Worship values (see mechanics catalogue) and the Tome's delivery-box row [C20][C39].
- **Per-session actions:** (1) fill every company's order you can from storage; (2) spend boxes on every character; (3) Silver Pen an order you cannot fill when a long streak is at stake [C26]. Idle states: a company with no order completed today [C9][C11]; unspent boxes on any character [C1].
- **AFK Setup implications:** none direct; the system wants stockpiles, so skilling and fighting characters should keep producing what Simple Shippin, Dudes Next Door, Alpine Suppliers and Cosmic Carrier ask for. Quantities grow with the streak until streak 100 [C25].
- **Min-max by stage:** see the dedicated section below.
- **Common mistakes:** never unlocking it past level 50; boxes left unspent on alts; breaking a long streak instead of using a Silver Pen; spending a fighter's boxes on a skill box it never uses; expecting the 2nd/3rd bonus at level 25/100 exactly (they start one level later, C5); assuming every box bonus is wired into the game (Toolbox models some as unused, C39).
- **Save and Toolbox fields:** see the catalogue ("Fields" column) and the Fields section.
- **Sources:** see the claim ledger at the end.

## Fields summary (ActiveOn today vs gaps)

Gaps for a full system: box levels per character (`postOffice.boxes[].level`), per-company `streak` and `shield`, `currencies.SilverPens`, total orders, derived income (about 1.4 boxes/company/day with streak bonuses, derived from C28), and the computed bonus per box. ## Additional mechanics from adversarial verification (2026-10-19)

- [C46] (tier 1) Bonus 0 uses the raw level with no offset and no rounding; bonuses 1 and 2 use round(L - upgradeLevels[0]) and round(L - upgradeLevels[1]). The third thresholds (400.1 / 800.1 / 100000.1) are never read by getPostOfficeBonus. Source: parsers/world-3/postoffice.ts#L33-L41; shared-data.json postOffice[*].upgradeLevels. Confidence: H.
- [C47] (tier 1) Pen economy: the Silver Pen (currency CYSilverPens, hold-to-use item) is the only streak-protection consumable; the Ink Blot achievement (101 pens used, quantity 101 confirmed in data) gives a 20% chance to keep the pen, so effective cost drops after 101 uses. Source: items.json SilverPen; achievements.json Ink_Blot; misc.ts#L562. Confidence: H.
- [C48] (tier 1) Tome divergence: tome.ts uses the saved DeliveryBoxMisc, while postoffice.ts rebuilds misc as vial BoxPoints + OLA[131]; the Tome total can lag the real total until the Post Office is opened in game. The Slug Slurp vial therefore counts toward the Tome row only via the saved value. Source: parsers/world-4/tome.ts#L316-L318 vs parsers/world-3/postoffice.ts#L11-L13. Confidence: H.
- [C49] (tier 1) Box bonuses feed many parsers (anvil, cooking, lab, worship, talents, damage, efficiency, shrines, alchemy, character), so one box level change moves values in many systems; ActiveOn should model the box as a multi-system lever.
