# Star Signs and constellations: system dossier

Topic id: star-signs. Researched 2026-10-19 against Toolbox pin 6c37e3d (game 2.3.540). Claim tags [Cn] resolve to the claim list in section 14. Terms follow GLOSSARY.md. No account data from any real save is quoted; "the W2 test account" / "the W3 test account" are used where fixtures are referenced.

## Star Signs and constellations

- **What it is:** Star signs are small permanent bonuses each character wears (1 to 3 slots); constellations are map challenges that pay Star Chart Points (SCP), which unlock signs in three trees (Chronus, Hydron, Seraph). [C1] [C2]
- **Unlocks:** Reach the Telescope on Where the Branches End at the top of the W1 big tree (three kill-gated doors, per character), take and hand in "I'm Seeing Stars!" (needs constellation A-2). [C14] [C15] The hand-in is account-wide for existing characters. [C15]
- **Scope:** Account-wide: unlocked signs, SCP, constellation progress. Per-character: which signs are worn, who has done which constellation (letters), and the Telescope dialogue flag. [C6] [C7] [C19]
- **Inputs -> outputs:** Inputs: constellation completions (SCP, 229 total in game data) [C4]; AFK time/Time Candies, boss kills, levels, class lines, character count. Outputs: sign unlocks (cost from the tree), extra sign slots (Chronus Cosmos 2, Hydron Cosmos 3) [C13], per-character bonuses fed into damage, efficiency, carry cap, AFK gain, EXP and drop formulas [C44] [C43]. Late: Infinite Star Signs [C28], Silkrode Nanochip and Seraph Cosmos multipliers [C30] [C32].
- **Goals and Milestones:** see tables below. SCP cumulative by world [C52]; slot/chart milestones [C11] [C12] [C10].
- **Feeds / fed by:** Fed by: Time Candies (A-2 and AFK constellations) [C18]; boss keys/colosseum tickets (A-8, A-9, B-5, B-6) [C17]; Rift (Infinite Stars) [C28]; Breeding shiny pets (extra infinite slots) [C29]; Lab (Silkrode chip) [C30]; Summoning level, Meritocracy (Seraph) [C32]. Feeds: damage/HP/crit [C44], mining/chop/fish/catch/trap/worship efficiency and carry cap [C43], anvil and alchemy town speed [C45], cooking/breeding/construction/lab W4 signs [C46].
- **Per-session actions:** (1) If the quest is not handed in: walk one character to the Telescope. (2) Spend banked SCP in chart order. (3) Check the character count and cheap constellations (visit/claim/candy) that are claim-ready: an event or AFK constellation whose required letters are all recorded pays nothing until someone clicks the star [C16]. (4) At the Telescope, re-align each character for its activity. Idle/capped states: unspent SCP with an unlockable sign; claim-ready constellation; empty slot; a character wearing a starter after Chronus Cosmos exists.
- **AFK Setup implications:** Slot choice follows the AFK Target (fighting vs each skill), see Min-max. Constellation AFK-hours need one uninterrupted AFK claim on the named map (1h A-2, 2h A-7, 4h B-3, 8h B-7, 16h C-4, 10h C-6); candies of at least that length also count but never stack [C17] [C18]. Laboratory/Cooking targets cannot use candies [C18].
- **Min-max by stage:** section 12.
- **Common mistakes:** (a) Assuming Chronus Cosmos costs 3: the tree path is 19 [C11]. (b) Skipping the four 1-point stat signs: they are the cheapest route to open Hydron [C10] [C35]. (c) Not knowing the Telescope exists / not realising only one visit is needed for unlocking [C14]. (d) Doing a constellation on extra characters beyond the required letters (points pay once) [C16]. (e) Wearing The Forsaken/Mr No Sleep without understanding penalties [C38] [C39]. (f) Leaving a new character on a random starter instead of The Fuzzy Dice [C34]. (g) Forgetting Major signs need a class level [C27]. (h) Expecting equipped signs of other characters to apply to the played one [C33]. Detect from save: pointsUnspent > cheapest unlockable cost; claimed vs progress letters; character starSigns length < slots.
- **Save and Toolbox fields:** see Mechanics catalogue and section 13.
- **Sources:** inline per claim; claim list in section 14. Tier 1 = vendored Toolbox code/data (pin 6c37e3d, 2.3.540); tier 2 = idleon.wiki (fetched 2026-10-19, edit dates unknown unless stated); tier 3 = ActiveOn authored specs (derived from tier 1); tier 4 = community guides.

## Mechanics catalogue (Worlds 1-4, W5+ in one line each)

### M1. Star Chart Points (SCP)
- Rule: account-wide integer; +constellation.points on claim; -sign.cost on unlock. [C8]
- Cap: none in code; total obtainable 229 (W1-W4: 133). [C4] [C5]
- Field: raw OptionsListAccount[40] -> Account State `account.starSigns.pointsUnspent` (via `account.accountOptions[40]`). Not a Toolbox-computed field. [C8] [C50]

### M2. Constellations: claim rule
- Rule: visit the star after all required letters are recorded; pays once per constellation. Needs NPCdialogue.Telescope >= 1 on the visiting character. [C16]
- Field: `account.constellations[]` (parsed from SSprog): `rawIndex`, `mapIndex`, `completedChars` (letters), `done`, `points`, `requiredPlayers`, `requirement`, `location`. `calcTotalConstellations` counts done. [C6] [C19]
- Unlock: hand in the Telescope quest first (dialogue). [C15]

### M3. Constellation types
- type 0 reach (class ranges), type 1 level, type 3 AFK hours, type 4 events. [C17]
- W1 list [C20]; W2 list [C21]; W3 list [C22]; W4 list [C23]. Required players [C25], [C53]. Map worlds [C55].
- Challenge events (A-5 Dr. Defecaus, A-8/B-5 colosseums, A-9 Amarok <20 s, B-6 Efaunt as Beginner line, C-2 barehanded Sheepie, C-3 Trapping 15, C-5 Worship wave 11, C-8 Bloodbone; W4: D-2/D-5 kill all before respawn, D-3 Cooking 15, D-7 Clammy megacrit) are recorded by game events, not Toolbox-visible beyond the letters. [C17] [C23]
- W5+: E and F series, 16 more entries, 96 points, up to 10 characters [C24].

### M4. AFK-hours constellations and Time Candies
- Rule: one AFK claim of >= H hours on the map (or a candy >= H used there); no stacking. Candy ids Timecandy1/2/3 = 1/2/4h. Candy refused for Lab/Cooking/no target/moving. [C18] [C17]
- Related signs: Gum_Drop (+20% Time Candy chance on claim, +8h AFK gains) and Gum_Drop_Major (+40%, +40h); unmodelled in Toolbox parsers. [C48]
- Field: letters in `account.constellations[].completedChars`; candies are inventory/storage items (outside this system).

### M5. The Telescope and its quest
- Rule/route/rewards: [C14] [C15]. Field: `characters[].quests.Telescope` (Toolbox quests) and `characters[].telescopeRoute` (kills left per door, ActiveOn-derived). [C50]

### M6. Star Chart tree: unlocking signs
- Rule: parent unlocked + points >= cost. Starters cost 0. [C9]
- Costs: Chronus 71 / Hydron 96 / Seraph 59. [C2] Wiki Hydron 68 disagrees. [C3]
- Field: `account.starSigns[]` (catalog order, index = sign id): `starName`, `cost`, `bonuses[]`, `tree`, `unlocked`, `isInfiniteStar`, `indexedStarName`. [C1] Raw StarSg. [C6]

### M7. Chart gates
- Hydron >= 24 nodes, Seraph >= 58 nodes (counted among rows 0-63). [C10] Not computed by Toolbox; derived by ActiveOn from `account.starSigns[].unlocked`.

### M8. Slots and the Cosmos signs
- 1 / 2 (Chronus Cosmos) / 3 (Hydron Cosmos). [C13] Seraph Cosmos is not a slot but a multiplier (M12). [C32]
- Path costs [C11] [C12]. Field: `characters[].starSigns.length`, slot max derived from unlocked Cosmos signs.

### M9. Aligning signs
- Per character, only at the Telescope (map 31). [C14] Field: `characters[].starSigns[]` (objects from the catalog, empty slots dropped). [C7]

### M10. Bonus resolution (what each sign adds)
- Equipped (or Infinite) signs are summed per effect key, substring-matched; Lv pseudo-bonus gates Major signs by class level; shared AFK_Gain key. [C26] [C27]
- Used in: damage/HP/crit/defence/accuracy/multikill [C44]; efficiency and carry cap [C43]; town speed [C45].
- Caller: `getStarSignBonus(character, account, effect)` (Toolbox-computed; ActiveOn never recomputes, ADR 0005). Account-wide terms use the logged-in character. [C33]

### M11. Infinite Star Signs (W4 Rift; extension of W3/W4 progression)
- Rule: first N catalog signs (5 + shiny passive) that are unlocked always count and lose negatives; the Major level gate still applies. [C28] Initial five. [C29]
- Field: `account.starSigns[].isInfiniteStar`; N from `account.rift` and `account.breeding.pets` (Infinite_Star_Signs passive). Wiki contradiction on unlock condition label and Steam 2023 comment. [C28] [C42]

## 12. Min-max by stage

Source note: no tier-1/2 source ranks star signs by stage; priorities below combine game-data arithmetic (tier 1) with ActiveOn authored order [C36] and community guides (tier 4), each cited. Where they conflict both views are shown.

- **W1 early (before the Telescope):** pick The Fuzzy Dice at character creation [C34]; do not chase signs yet. Open the tree doors on one character (3,000 + 2,500 + 5,000 kills) [C14].
- **W1 mid:** Telescope quest via A-2 (1h AFK or 1h Time Candy) [C15] [C18]; then cheapest constellations: A-3, A-1 (Lv30 on 4), A-6/A-10 if class lines exist, A-7 (2h on 2). Target Chronus Cosmos (19 pts) [C11] [C36]. Community view: prioritise damage and gathering efficiency (The Buff Guy, Dwarfo Beardus, Hipster Logger, Pie Seas, Shoe Fly) and Pack Mule first [C40]; DigitalTQ agrees on skill signs plus Pirate Booty, Pack Mule [C39].
- **W1 late:** A-4, A-5, A-8, A-9 (needs Forest Villa Key) for W1's 37 points [C20]. Second slot: pair the activity sign with Silly Snoozer/Pirate Booty for fighting or The Big Comatose/The OG Skiller for skilling [C37].
- **W2:** B-series (32 pts): reach stars first, then B-3 (4h) and B-7 (8h) AFK with candies, B-6 Efaunt as Beginner line, B-8 Lv65 on 4 [C21]. Open Hydron: buy any cheapest 20 Chronus signs including the four 1-point stat signs [C10] [C36]. Counter-view: wiki and idleon.guide say skip stat-only signs [C35].
- **W3:** C-series (39 pts): C-1 Lv90 on 6, C-2/C-3/C-8 tasks, C-4 16h and C-6 10h on 9 players are long AFK jobs [C22]. Aim at Hydron Cosmos (14) for slot 3, total 51 [C12]. Slot fits: AFK fighter Silly Snoozer + Pirate Booty + Gum Drop; skiller its skill sign + Big Comatose + OG Skiller [C37] [C39]. Avoid The Forsaken (-80% HP) and Mr No Sleep (-6% AFK gain) [C38] [C36].
- **W4:** D-series (25 pts): D-1 needs 8 characters at Lv120; D-3 Cooking 15 on 4; D-2/D-5 kill-all-before-respawn; D-7 megacrit Clammy [C23]. With 133 total points through W4 [C5], finish Chronus (71) then Hydron signs by job: Sawsaw Salala (builder), Trapezoidburg (trapper), Preys Bea (worshipper), Gordonius Major (cooking), Breedabilli/Power Bowower (breeding), Scienscion (lab) [C46]; Major signs once class level allows (60/70/80/90/100) [C27]. Rift Infinite Stars gives 5 free always-on signs; level shiny pets for more [C28] [C29].
- **W5+ outline:** Seraph chart (58 nodes), Seraph Cosmos multiplier scaled by Summoning level, Silkrode Nanochip, E/F constellations [C47] [C32] [C30].
- **Per class:** Archer/Mage line characters are needed for A-6/A-10/B-2 [C20] [C21]; Beginner line for B-6; fighters pick Damage/Drop/AFK signs; skillers pick efficiency signs [C37] [C38].
- **Priority order summary (ActiveOn authored):** Chronus Cosmos -> 11 cheapest Hydron openers -> Hydron Cosmos -> fit signs. [C36]
