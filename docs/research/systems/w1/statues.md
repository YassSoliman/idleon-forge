# Statues

Dossier for IdleOn Forge / ActiveOn, M26. Verified 2026-10-19 against Toolbox @6c37e3d (game 2.3.540). Prior research: origin/research/w1-systems statues.md (2026-10-17), refreshed and extended here. Claims are numbered [C#]; the source of each is in the claims list at the end.

## Statues

- **What it is:** 32 statue types. Statue items drop from monsters (above all Crystal mobs), bosses and chests; deposited at the Statue Man (Town Marble) they add EXP, each level adds a fixed bonus, with no max level. Grades Gold, Onyx and Zenith multiply the bonus. [C1] [C4] [C7] [C8]
- **Unlocks:** Town Marble (class level 22, bought for 20 Silver Coins) and his quest with Sculpting Tools unlock depositing, per character. [C13] [C14] Gold needs Guilding Tools (W2). [C12] Onyx needs Monolith's tools (W4), after a 250-level/17-type check. [C15] [C16] Zenith needs Zenelith's tools (W7). [C18]
- **Scope:** per character until Gold, then account-wide; the account uses the highest level any character has. [C9] [C10] Class talents apply per character; Voodoo applies to all. [C28] [C27]
- **Inputs to outputs:** statues to EXP to levels (formula below); coins to Gold; a 20,000 or 1,000,000 stack to Onyx or Zenith. EXP to next level: [C5] [C6] Gold cost: [C11] Stacks are carryable up to 999,999,999. [C21]
- **Goals and Milestones:**

| Milestone | Why | Claims |
|---|---|---|
| Town Marble quest on the depositing character | Enables deposits | [C13] |
| Gold on a statue (cheapest first; first 5 cost 1.79M) | Account-wide; stops per-character loss | [C11] [C10] |
| Total statue LV 250 and 17 unique types in inventory | Monolith quest 1 gate | [C15] |
| 20,000 of one statue (Gold first) | Onyx: x2 (x2.3 with Lantern), +10% deposit EXP per type | [C19] [C22] [C35] |
| 100,000 statues looted from drops | Monolith 3: MONOLITHIALISM + 10k EhExPee + 10k Kachow | [C17] |
| 1,000,000 Power statues, then 1,000,000 of a statue | Zenith Tools, then Zenith grade (x1.5 on top of Onyx) | [C18] [C20] [C23] |

- **Feeds / fed by:**
  - Fed by drops [C40] [C41], quests [C42], the weekly boss shop [C43], Post Office (see post-office), double-drop sources [C38] [C39].
  - Feeds damage, HP, crit, accuracy, defence, food effect, mining/chopping/fishing/catching/trapping/worship power, class/skill/construction/alchemy/cooking/breeding/divinity/farming EXP, anvil speed, coins, sailing, sneaking, summoning, hole and coral systems. [C57] Also Tome points, Button tasks and Monolithialism multikill. [C51] [C52] [C50]
- **Per-session actions:** (1) deposit on the right character (main before Gold, any after); (2) buy the cheapest un-Golded statue when coins allow; (3) from W4 stop depositing stacks that are heading to 20K; (4) check the Skinny vial / Startue bubble / Flair before large deposits. [C33] [C34] [C36] Idle state: statues in storage while Gold is unbought or the quest is undone on the depositor.
- **AFK Setup implications:** Statue farming is fighting where Crystal mobs spawn and drop rate is high; an Active Play suggestion until Onyx makes it a Goal. [C44] [C48] [C47]
- **Min-max by stage:** see the dedicated section below.
- **Common mistakes:**
  - Depositing one type on several characters before Gold (everything but the highest is lost). Detect: grade 0 and level > 0 on two or more characters. [C10] [C9]
  - Not buying Gold when affordable. Detect grade 0 with coins > Gold cost. [C11]
  - Not doing Town Marble's quest on the depositor.
  - Depositing a stack that should reach Onyx/Zenith. [C46]
  - Depositing Monolith-3 Kachow/EhExPee before the EXP boosters are high. [C45]
  - Forgetting Gold before Onyx (the Onyx drop does nothing otherwise). [C19]
- **Save and Toolbox fields:** see the catalogue.
- **Sources:** per-claim, in the claims list.

## Min-max by stage

W1 early (class level under 22 to Town Marble):
- Do Town Marble's quest on the main character (the Power Statue reward is the first level). [C13]
- Deposit everything on one character until Gold is bought (avoid splitting). [C10] Farm Frogs/Beans (Crystal Carrot) for Power and Health (12% boss/chest Power drops). [C44] [C40] [C41] [C47]

W1 late / W2 (Guilding Tools):
- Buy Gold cheapest-first in index order (Power 98.8K, Speed 169K, Mining 284K, Feasty 470K, Health 772K, Kachow 1.26M, Lumberbob 2.04M); the first five cost 1.79M. [C11] [C12]
- Target total statue level 250 with 17 types in inventory for Monolith 1. [C15]
- Crystal Crabal supplies Thicc Skin, Oceanman, Ol Reliable, Exp, Anvil and Cauldron. [C44] [C40]

W3:
- Gold through Bullseye (88.6M) then EhExPee (357M) and Seesaw (568M); push the AutoReview tier 20 on the core statues and 40 on all. [C11] [C49]
- Farm Crystal Cattle for Speed, Mining, Feasty, Beholder, Bullseye and EhExPee. [C44] [C40]

W4:
- Complete Monolith quest 1 to 2: hand in 20,000 Power for Onyx Tools, then Onyx statues; hold Power for the 20K stack, deposit the rest. [C16] [C46]
- Onyx order (community AutoReview): core W1-W3 first, then Thicc Skin, EhExPee, W5-W6, then Kachow, Oceanman, Bullseye, Box, Twosoul, Seesaw. [C49]
- Each Onyx type adds +10% EXP to all later deposits, so Onyx early compounds. [C35]
- Crystal Custard gives Pecunia, Mutton, Egg. [C44]
- Monolith 3: save the 10,000 Kachow/EhExPee rewards until Startue EXP is high (level 540+) and Skinny is over 100%. [C17] [C45]

W5 onward (outline):
- Onyx Lantern raises Onyx to 2.3x; Voodoo Statufication for all statues; Statue Bonanza for Power/Speed/Mining/Lumberbob; Dragon Statue multiplies the rest. [C22] [C27] [C30] [C29]
- Zenith via 1M Power then 1M per statue; Zenith Market priority True Zen, Lamp Boost, Super Dupers. [C18] [C26]
- Royal Guardian Flair levels (7/15/25/33x EXP) for the long-grind statues. [C36]

Per class: Squire/Barbarian value Power/Mining, Hunter/Bowman Speed/Anvil, Wizard/Shaman Exp/Lumberbob, Maestro EhExPee/Kachow/Feasty. [C28] No community source gives per-class deposit-level priorities.
