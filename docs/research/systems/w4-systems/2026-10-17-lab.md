## Answer

- **The Lab opens on arrival in the W4 town (Outer World Town, map 150).** The Mainframe is the server rack beside the portal. Gobo's 5th quest asks you to upload players and light the first bonus. That needs **at least 2 uploaded players**, because the first node is 138 px from the prism. Nothing in code gates it by level (H).
- **A character counts as "in the lab" only while its AFK target is `Laboratory`**, meaning it idles in the lab tubes. From W5 on, the Arctis major divinity also counts. Leaving the lab drops the character out of the chain, and every bonus that depended on it switches off (H).
- **Connection rules:** the prism at (43, 229) connects to a player within that player's **Line Width**. Players then chain to each other. Each lit player lights every node or jewel within the **Connection Range**, which is 80 px base. Line Width is `50 + 2 × that character's Lab level`, plus meals, a card, a chip, a pet, a talent and gem tubes. Distance is an octagonal approximation, `0.96·max(dx,dy) + 0.40·min(dx,dy)` (H).
- **The 14 W4 Mainframe nodes are account-wide multipliers on W1–W3 systems.** Certified Stamp Book doubles non-MISC stamps. My 1st Chemistry Set doubles vials. Gilded Cyclical Tubing makes refinery cycles 3× faster. No Bubble Left Behind levels your lowest bubbles daily. Killer's Brightside counts each kill as 2 for portals and the Death Note. Shrine World Tour applies a town shrine to the whole world. Wired In doubles printer output. Sigils of Olden Alchemy unlocks Sigil leveling. Animal Farm, Banking Fury and Fungi Finger give damage and cash. Spelunker Obol makes jewels 1.5× stronger, and Viral Connection gives +50% range. Four more nodes arrive with W6 (H).
- **Chips** (22 of them) are per-character. Each character has **7 slots, unlocked at Lab Lv 5, 10, 15, 25, 35, 50 and 75**. Chips work whether or not the character is in the lab. 11 kinds stack. 11 kinds are capped at **1 per character** (Potato Chip, Conductive Software and Processor, Chocolatey, all Silkrode, Poker and both Omega chips) (H).
- **Chips are bought, never dropped.** The Chip Repository is unlocked by holding a stack of **3 Pile of Processor Chips** from the W4 town shop. It offers 2 chips a week: slot 1 always comes from the first 12 chips, slot 2 from all 22. Each costs a W1–W4 material plus 100 of a meal plus 100 of a pet spice. Each slot can be bought **once per week**. The rotation is server-global, `floor(GlobalTime / 604800)`, so it rolls over every Thursday 00:00 UTC. The gem shop "Console Chip" (385 gems, max 7 a week) gives a random chip (H).
- **Jewels** (24, account-wide, each owned once) are bought from the Jewel Spinner, one per week, from the same rotation. The spinner is unlocked by holding a stack of **7 Strung Jewels**, and the first jewel then costs 5 more. A jewel only works while a lit player is within range of it. Four "set" jewels double when their whole colour group is lit: purple 3, blue 4, orange 4, green 5. Spelunker Obol makes every jewel 1.5× stronger (H).
- **Lab inputs come from Breeding and Cooking.** Every chip and jewel costs pet **spices** (Breeding territories) and **meals** (Cooking). Lab Line Width in turn comes from meals (Pancakes, Wild Boar, Eel), shiny pets and the pet arena. ActiveOn should treat Lab, Cooking and Breeding as one loop (H).
- **Biggest casual mistakes:** never unlocking the Repository or Spinner (`accountOptions[91]` < 1 or < 2), empty unlocked chip slots while the repository holds chips, missing the weekly chip and jewel, and lab characters whose AFK target drifted away. All four can be read straight from the Account State, and Toolbox already raises the weekly rotation alert (H).
- **Gaps:** Reddit (Arctic Shift) was rate-limited the whole session, so the per-class chip priorities below are **O** (derived from the effects) and need a community check. Both fixture saves are pre-W4: Lab level 0, nothing unlocked.

## The Lab: Mainframe, chips and jewels

- **What it is:** the W4 town skill **Laboratory**. You "upload" characters into the Mainframe, and they act as light-beam relays from a prism to fixed bonus nodes and to jewels you buy. Each character also has 7 chip slots for personal passive bonuses. Lab EXP levels the Laboratory skill, which raises Line Width and opens chip slots. [Wiki](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Laboratory) (2026-10-07).

- **Scope:**
  - **Account-wide:** node bonuses, jewel ownership and effects, the chip repository, and the weekly rotation.
  - **Per-character:** Lab level, which sets Line Width and chip slots. Also tube position (x, y, with a different board position per character), the 7 chip slots, and whether the character is in the lab at all.
  - **Mixed:** a node is "on" for the whole account, but only while the characters forming its chain are AFK in the lab. Chip bonuses apply only to the character wearing the chip (`RecalcChipBonuses` reads the logged-in character's slots, N.js @7949485).
- **Inputs → outputs:**
  - **In:** AFK time in the lab, which gives Lab EXP. EXP scales with Lab efficiency against the Laboratory "monster" defence (N.js `SkillStats("LaboratoryEXPmulti")` @4244288; `TB:parsers/world-4/lab.ts#L413`). Chips and jewels cost **pet spices** (Breeding), **meals** (Cooking) and stacks of W1–W4 materials. Gems buy optional extras.
  - **Out:** 18 node bonuses, 24 jewel bonuses and 22 chip bonuses, all in the tables below. Lab level feeds Line Width and chip slots. Souped tubes give +30% Line Width, and `min(100, 4 × 30)` = **+100% Lab EXP** for that character (N.js @4244288; the gem text says "2x Lab EXP").
- **Connection mechanics** (H, N.js `_customBlock_Labb` @7971217–7975781, mirrored by `TB:parsers/world-4/lab.ts#L286-L376`):

  | Rule | Value |

  | Distance | `0.9604339 × max(|dx|,|dy|) + 0.397824735 × min(|dx|,|dy|)` |
  | Prism | Fixed at (43, 229). It connects the first in-lab player whose distance is less than that player's Line Width |
  | Player to player | A player is lit when it is within Line Width of an already lit player. Toolbox tests the candidate's own Line Width (M) |
  | Player to node or jewel | Within Connection Range of a lit player |
  | Connection Range | `floor(80 × (1 + (Pyrite Rhombol + Viral Connection)/100)) + W4 merit (1 px/level, 8 levels, 2 merits each) + Equinox "Laboratory Fuse" (1 px/level, max 10) + Summoning win bonus`. **Fixed exceptions:** Spelunker Obol, Viral Connection, Pyrite Rhombol and Pure Opal Navette are always 80 px; the three W7 jewels are always 100 px |

  | Gobo hint | "You'll need more than 1 player uploaded to reach the 1st bonus": Animal Farm is 138 px from the prism, more than 50 + 80 |

- **Per-session actions:**
  1. Read `account.lab.currentRotation` against this week's rotation. If a chip or jewel is unclaimed and affordable, buy it. That is Toolbox's `laboratory.chipsRotation` / `jewelsRotation` alert (`TB:utility/dashboard/account.js#L1073-L1101`).
  2. Equip new chips into any slot that is unlocked and empty: `playersChips[c][i] == -1` with Lab level ≥ the slot requirement while `chips[n].repoAmount > 0`.
  3. Check that every intended lab character still has AFK target `Laboratory`, and that it appears in `connectedPlayers`.
  4. After a Lab level-up, re-check positions: a wider Line Width may let the chain spread out to light a further node.
  5. **Idle states:**
    - A character in a tube but not connected (wasted).
    - Repository chips sitting unequipped while slots are free.
    - A jewel that is owned but not lit (wasted).
    - Grounded Processor equipped once the character's total drop rate is ≥ 5×, or Grounded Motherboard once move speed is ≥ 170% (both inert).
    - No Bubble Left Behind connected but the player did not log in that day (no trigger).
- **AFK Setup implications:**
  - The Lab wants a **dedicated set of characters AFK in the lab**, enough to reach Certified Stamp Book and later Chemistry Set. Those characters gain nothing else (no kills, no skilling), so pick the ones whose fighting or skilling matters least right now.
  - Chips, by contrast, help a character **wherever it is AFK**, so every character should fill its slots.
  - Souped tubes go to whoever entered the lab first. Enter with your lowest-Lab-level characters first, since they need the most help with Line Width and EXP (M).
  - From W5, Arctis major divinity lets a character count as in the lab while doing something else. The Idleon Incomplete Guide notes "you can still get the bonuses from lab without being AFK via Arctis god" (W4 tab, undated, post-W7).
- **Min-max by stage** (O unless cited):
  - **Arrival in W4:**
    1. Finish Gobo 1–5: Breeding, then Cooking, then Lab. The incomplete guide's W4 tab: "First thing … complete Gobo's questline".
    2. Buy 3 Processor Chips and 7 Strung Jewels as soon as coins allow.
    3. Upload 2–3 low-value characters to light Animal Farm and Wired In, and Gilded Tubing if the refinery is running.
  - **Early W4:**
    1. Push the chain to **No Bubble Left Behind, Sigils, Killer's Brightside, Shrine World Tour and Certified Stamp Book**. Stamp Book doubles every non-MISC stamp, and Sigils of Olden Alchemy is the only way to level Sigils.
    2. Chips: Galvanic Software (+10% dmg, stacks) and Conductive Software on fighters; Galvanic Motherboard and Processor and Conductive Processor on skillers; Conductive Nanochip and Motherboard on lab characters.
    3. Cook Pancakes for px Line Width.
  - **Mid W4:**
    1. Light Spelunker Obol early: it multiplies every jewel.
    2. Buy jewels in this order: Pyrite Rhombol (range), Sapphire Rhombol (Line Width aura, place lab characters near it), Pyrite Pyramite (damage), Amethyst Rhinestone (cooking), then complete colour sets.
    3. Reach My 1st Chemistry Set.
  - **Late W4:**
    1. Light Banking Fury and Viral Connection.
    2. Complete the orange and green sets.
    3. Put 1-per-character damage chips (Silkrode Nanochip, Poker Chip, Omega chips) on your main pusher.
    4. Use Grounded Processor only on characters below 5× drop rate.
  - **Per class:**
    - **Bubonic Conjuror** sits at the board's split point. Purple Tube gives players to its right more Line Width; Green Tube gives players to its left more Lab EXP. Upload Squared is for itself.
    - **Divine Knight / Elemental Sorcerer pushers** want damage chips.
    - **Wizard-line characters with Smart Efficiency** gain Lab efficiency.
  - **W5+ outline:** Arctis (lab without AFK), W6 Matrix nodes and Pure Opal jewels, and the W7 chip text swaps (Silkrode Nanochip and Motherboard, wiki changelog v1.00, 2025-11-06) plus Tesseract jewels.
- **Common mistakes** (detect from save):

  | Mistake | Detection |
  |---|---|
  | Never unlocked the Repository or Spinner | `account.accountOptions[91]` < 1 or < 2 while the account is in W4 |
  | Weekly chip or jewel skipped | `currentRotation[i] != this week's index` and the requirements are met (Toolbox alert) |
  | Empty unlocked chip slots | `playersChips[c][i] == -1`, `skillsInfo.laboratory.level ≥ [5,10,15,25,35,50,75][i]`, any `chips[].repoAmount > 0` |
  | Lab character drifted away | Intended lab character with `afkTarget != 'Laboratory'`; `connectedPlayers` shorter than the number in tubes |
  | Inert conditional chips | Grounded Processor with total drop rate ≥ 5×; Grounded Motherboard with move speed ≥ 170% |
  | Jewel owned but unlit | `jewels[n].acquired && !jewels[n].active` |
  | Two of a 1-per-player chip | Blocked by the game (`ChipDesc[n][9] == 1`), so it never happens |
  | Viaduct of the Gods lit early | −30% liquid regen. Only worth it once liquid caps bind (O) |

- **Save and Toolbox fields:**
  - **Raw `Lab`** (array of 17, parsed by `TB:parsers/world-4/lab.ts#L28-L48`):
    - `[0]` = x, y per character (24 values, 12 characters).
    - `[1..12]` = 7 chip slots per character (`-1` means empty).
    - `[13]` = this week's claimed `[chip1, chip2, jewel]`.
    - `[14]` = jewel acquired flags (30).
    - `[15]` = repository chip counts (25).
    - `[16]` = 4 values, not mapped (unknown).
  - **Parsed `account.lab`:**
    - `playersCords[]` `{x, y, playerId, lineWidth, soupedUp}`.
    - `playersChips[][]`.
    - `connectedPlayers[]`.
    - `jewels[]` `{acquired, active, bonus, multiplier, x, y}`.
    - `chips[]` `{repoAmount, amount, totalAmount}`.
    - `labBonuses[]` `{active, bonusOn, bonusOff, x, y}`.
    - `totalRawChips`, `currentRotation`.
  - **Per character:** `characters[].skillsInfo.laboratory.level` (Lv0[12]) and `characters[].afkTarget` (`'Laboratory'`).
  - **Account:** `account.accountOptions[91]` (unlock state), `account.gemShopPurchases[123]` (souped tube purchases).
  - **Toolbox-computed (ADR 0005):**
    - Line Width per player.
    - Active nodes and jewels, including the set doubling and Spelunker multiplier.
    - Lab efficiency breakdown (`getLabEfficiencyParts`, L413).
    - Future rotations (`getChipsAndJewels(account, weeks)`).
    - Chip bonus per character (`getPlayerLabChipBonus`).
  - **Fixtures:** both private fixtures (one W2 and one W3 account) parse to Lab level 0 for every character, `OLA[91] = 0`, all chip slots `-1`, and no connections. That is expected before W4, and it confirms the empty-state shape parses cleanly.
