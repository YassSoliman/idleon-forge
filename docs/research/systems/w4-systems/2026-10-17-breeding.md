## Answer

- **Unlock.** Breeding is a W4-town skill. Its **level and EXP are shared by all characters** (`"Breeding_Lv_and_EXP_are_shared between_all_characters"` @14020687). It is not a per-character AFK skill, so it never takes a character's AFK Setup slot. The first egg is rigged to give a Green Mushroom (`BREED_RIG` @7924600). The W4 row of Mobs opens only after you **defeat the Massive Troll**, the W4 boss (`Breeding[1][3]=1` on boss death, @4903054). (H)
- **The core loop is eggs → Mobs → Dead DNA → upgrades.** The nest makes 1 egg every **7,200 s (2 h)**, divided by egg-speed bonuses (`TotalTimeForEgg` @7930253). Capacity is **3 + Egg Capacity upgrade (max 5) + Royal Egg Cap gem + W4 Merit** (`TotalEggCapacity` @7921789). A full nest makes nothing more, so an idle-capped state is the nest being full. Trashing a Mob gives 3–5 Dead DNA (`GeneticsPerTrashPet` @7934435). Each upgrade costs **Dead DNA plus a Cooking meal**, which ties Breeding to Cooking. (H)
- **There are 12 real upgrades**, unlocked at Breeding Lv 1/5/10/15/20/25/30/40/50/60/70/80. The ones that unlock a multiplier must be bought at level 1 as soon as they open: **Breedability (Lv 10), Rarity of the Egg (Lv 20), Pastpresent Brood (Lv 30), Paint Bucket/Shiny (Lv 40), Overwhelmed Golden Egg (Lv 50).** Without these, the "New Mob Chance" multipliers stay at ×1. The game's own help text says the upgrades are "100% necessary". (H, code and help text @14707254)
- **68 Mobs** (W1 17, W2 17, W3 18, W4 16). Each has a **Gene** (combat, forager or special) and a **Shiny passive** that applies account-wide even outside the Fence Yard. Shiny levels come from keeping a shiny Mob in the Fence Yard. The base cap is **Lv 20**, plus 10 with the W7 **Menagerie unlock 11** (v1.23, `SpecialPassives` @7930582). (H)
- **Territories (26 live)** are won once in a Mob battle. After that they are worked by **forager-gene Mobs only**. Combat Mobs stopped counting for foraging in v2.41 (2025-07-06). Forage teams are 4 Mobs. Their multipliers depend on adjacent rows (Targeter, Badumdum, Borger) and on team makeup (Miasma ×4 if all genes differ, Flashy ×1.5 with no combat Mob). Each territory makes one **Spice**, which Cooking uses to discover meals and the Lab uses to buy chips. (H)
- **Daily cap on Spice claims:** 100 claims a day (`OLA[100]`, Toolbox `maxNumberOfSpiceClicks`). Each **Trasher** in the Fence Yard at reset gives +25 more. A good Play Session claims Spices before they pile up, and the toolbox alert fires when claims are unspent. (H for the reset code, M for the 100 cap, which comes from Toolbox)
- **The Mob Arena** (endless waves) pays **16 bonuses** at waves 2–200. Battle slots go 2 → 6 (waves 2/15/50/125). The others: +25%/+60% Library VIP, Post Office crates, **−50% kitchen upgrade cost (wave 35)**, **3rd large bubble (wave 100)**, +20% Lab line width (wave 150), Monster Cash ×1.5/×2, Total Damage ×1.2/×1.4. The W7 **Crucible** adds 16 more bonuses behind the Menagerie. (H, `RANDOlist[53]` @4461632)
- **Leverage for a W4 player like Joseph.** First get the Arena to waves 35 and 100 (cooking cost and an alchemy bubble slot) and unlock the early Territories for Spices (Cooking meals). Keep the nest emptied and trashed for Dead DNA. Shiny farming is a W5+ long game: Lv 20 needs about **2.69 M base days** (`floor((1+L^1.6)·1.7^L)`), so only speed multipliers make it reachable. (H for the numbers, M for the priority)
- **Save fields:** Toolbox gives everything under `account.breeding` (eggs, eggCapacity, petUpgrades, pets, fencePetsObject, territories, maxArenaLevel, timeToNextEgg) and `account.cooking.spices`. The skill level is `characters[i].skillsInfo.breeding` (shared). Both fixture saves (W2 and W3) are **untouched**: Breeding Lv 0, 3 default eggs, every upgrade at 0. (H)
- **Stale or contradicting sources:** the wiki still says shiny Mobs cap at Lv 20 and shows breedability hearts up to 10, but Toolbox caps hearts at 9. Every Territory "fight power to forage" rule in pre-July-2025 guides is obsolete. Guides from before Feb 2025 stop egg rarity at Void/Lustre, but it now goes to Godshard (11) since v2.29. (H)

## Breeding and Mobs

- **What it is:** an account-wide W4 skill. You hatch eggs into Mobs, keep or trash them, and use them in three places: Territory forage teams (Spices), the Mob Arena (account bonuses) and the Fence Yard (Breedability and Shiny progress). It also unlocks new Mob species, each with an account-wide Shiny passive.
- **Unlocks:**

| Step | Gate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Breeding station | W4 town (Hyperion Nebula). The skill shows Lv 0 until first use. In both fixture saves, `skillsInfo.breeding.level` is 0. | wiki Breeding infobox; fixtures |
| First Mob | The first breed is forced to Green Mushroom (`BREED_RIG`: `Exp0[11]==0 && Lv0[11]==1`) | N.js `_customBlock_Breeding("BREED_RIG")` @7924600 (H) |
| W2 / W3 Mob rows | Unlocked by breeding the W1 Red Mushroom (W2 row) and the W2 Pincermin (W3 row), per the wiki "Unlocked From" column | wiki Breeding, Unlockable Mobs (M) |
| W4 Mob row | **Defeat the Massive Troll** (W4 boss): `0==Breeding[1][3] && (Breeding[1][3]=1)` on boss death | N.js @4903054 (H) |
| Next species in a row | Random chance on each breed, `TotalBreedChance` (see Goals) | N.js @7928068 (H) |
| Upgrades | Breeding Lv 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80 (table below) | wiki Upgrade Tab (game-generated) (M). I found no level array in N.js; the UI derives it. |
| Shiny Mobs | Breeding Lv 40 + Paint Bucket Lv 1 (patch text "Unlock_Shiny_Mobs_at_Breeding_Lv_40") | N.js @14416459 (H) |
| Mob Arena | Breeding Lv 8 | wiki Breeding, Fighting (M) |
| Crucible (hard Arena) | W7 Menagerie unlock 8 | N.js `CalcTalentMAP.arenaCruc` @4461850 (H) |

- **Scope:**
  - **Account-wide:** Breeding Lv and EXP, eggs, Mobs, upgrades, Fence Yard, Territories, Spices, Arena.
  - **Per-character:** only who is logged in. `PetQTYonBreed` reads the logged-in character's `Lv0[11]`, which equals the shared level. Star-sign bonuses (Breedabilli `57a`/`57b`, Power Bowower) count only if the *active* character has them equipped (Toolbox `getBestActiveCharacter`). Beast Master talents **Curviture of the Paw** (×Mob power) and **Shining Beacon of Egg** (+Breeding EXP) apply "no matter which player you hatch eggs on" (Toolbox `talents.json` 372/373). The game takes the highest level across characters (`getbonus2(1,373,-1)`). (H)
- **Inputs → outputs:**

| Consumes | Produces |
|---|---|
| Time (eggs refill), nest space | Eggs (rarity 1–11) → Mobs with Power, Gene, possibly a Breedable (pink) or Shiny variant |
| Trashed Mobs | **Dead DNA** (`PetDeadCell`), 3–5 each (+Holes B_UPG 52, ×Crucible bonuses 0/6/11/13 = ×1.25 each) |
| DNA Splicer tool use (Gene Splicing) | Gene-boost Genes (`GeneticCost` @7929864) |
| Dead DNA + **Cooking meals** | Breeding upgrades |
| Mob teams in Territories | Spices (Cooking, Lab chips) |
| Mob teams in Arena | Arena bonuses (account-wide) |
| Fence Yard slots | Breedability levels, Shiny levels, Eggshell eggs, Trasher +25 spice claims |
| Breeding EXP | Breeding Lv → upgrade unlocks, higher Mob Power (`PetQTYonBreed`) |

EXP per breed is multiplied by `2 + mobsFound^1.5` (×5 that when the breed is a new Mob, ×25 when it is shiny, per the wiki). So **unlocking species compounds Breeding EXP**.

**Mob Power** (N.js `PetQTYonBreed` @7926061; Toolbox `getEggsPowerRange` [L485-508](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-4/breeding.ts#L485-L508)): `base = (4·Lv + (Lv/2)^3)^0.85`, × Curviture of the Paw (1–2.1), × egg scale `1 + 0.2·egg + 0.3·floor((egg+1)/4)`, × a random roll capped by `min(1.2 + Lv/12, 4)`. **Power is mostly Breeding Lv**, so old low-level Mobs should be trashed as level rises. (H)

**New Mob Chance** (N.js `TotalBreedChance` @7928068; Toolbox `getBreedingMulti` [L263-315](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-4/breeding.ts#L263-L315)). It is a product of:

| Vials + Petting the Rift bubble × Rift level | ×(1 + (NewPet vial + NewPetRift·Rift)/100) | W2 alchemy, W4 Rift |

**Breedability (pink Mobs, "hearts")**: the level is `min(9, floor((multi2 − 1)^0.8) + 1)`. ## Territories, Spices and forage teams

- **What it is:** 26 live Territories (rows 0–13 and 15–26; row 14 is the retired "Arena" row). You win each one once in a Mob battle (`battleName`). After that, a 4-Mob forage team fills a reward bar. Each fill gives that Territory's Spice into a claim pile.
- **Forage math** (N.js `TotalTrekkingHR` @7907546, `TotalTrekkingREQ` @7910846; Toolbox `getForageSpeed`/`getCombatMixGeneMulti` [L376-403](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-4/breeding.ts#L376-L403)). **Only forager-type genes add speed. Combat genes add 0.**

| Gene (forager) | Forage rule |
|---|---|
| any forager | contributes its Power |
| **Forager** | 2× its Power |
| **Targeter** | 5× if the Mob in the same slot of the Territory *above* is also a Targeter |
| **Opticular** | 3× if it has the highest Power on the team |
| **Borger** | 10× if the Territory above has any Forager |
| **Fleeter** | whole team ×1.3 each |
| **Badumdum** | Territories above *and below* ×1.2 each |
| **Flashy** | team ×1.5 each, only if the team has **no** combat Mob (empty slots count as none) |
| **Fastidious** | team ×1.5 each, only if the team has **at least one** combat Mob |

## Fence Yard

- **What it is:** a yard of **5 + Fence Extension (max 10) + 2 × Fenceyard Space gem (index 125)** slots (`FenceYardSlots` @7924186; Toolbox L72). Since v2.41 (2025-07-06) it accepts **only Breedable (pink, gene 4) and Shiny (gene 5)** Mobs, plus special genes that act there: Eggshell lays eggs, and Trasher gives +25 Spice claims if it is in the yard before the daily reset.
- **Stacking:** several copies of one species multiply that species' Breedability or Shiny progress rate (Toolbox `getTimeToLevel` divides by `copies`, [L324-334](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-4/breeding.ts#L324-L334)). The trade-off is concentrating slots on one key passive versus spreading them.

## Per-session actions

1. **Empty the nest.** If every unlocked slot holds an egg, egg production is idle (the Toolbox alert `breeding.eggs`). Breed the eggs, then **trash everything you won't use** for Dead DNA. Keep only top-Power forager genes for Territories, combat genes for the Arena, and pink or shiny copies for the yard.
2. **Spend Dead DNA and meals** on the next upgrade. The order is in Min-max below.
3. **Claim Spices.** Unspent daily claims are wasted. The `cooking.spices` alert fires while `100 − OLA[100] > 0`.
4. **Re-check forage teams after new Mobs.** Swap in higher-Power foragers. Never drop a Converter into a Territory mid-fill, because it resets progress.
5. **Push the Arena** whenever Mob damage has grown. Next Milestone: 35, then 100.
6. **Fence Yard.** Keep pinks and shinies in, and keep any Trasher in before the daily reset. Alerts fire when a shiny or breedability passes a set level, so you can swap to the next species.
- **Idle/capped states:** full nest; Spice claims unspent; a forage team with 0 speed (no forager genes); empty Fence Yard slots; affordable upgrades unbought.

## AFK Setup implications

- Breeding does **not** take a character's AFK slot. Eggs, foraging and Fence Yard progress run on real time for the account. So no AFK Setup "puts a character on Breeding".
- The **Expected Return** matters through nest capacity: `capacity × egg time` is how long until the nest caps (3 eggs × 2 h = 6 h at base). An AFK Stretch longer than that wastes eggs. That makes **Egg Capacity** the first quality-of-life buy.
- **Beast Master** (W4 elite, from Hunter) helps eggs through I Dream of Peace and Egg on AFK claims. The late-game "active BM/Wind Walker" trick, where active kills speed up the yard, Spices and eggs, needs a Voidwalker with Enhancement Eclipse 150+. That is W5+ (AutoReview `getActiveBMAdviceGroup`, M).
