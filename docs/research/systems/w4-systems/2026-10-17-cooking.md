## Answer

- **Unlock:** reach the W4 town (Hyperion Nebula). Kitchen 1 is already unlocked in every save; spices only arrive through **Gobo's W4 tutorial quest line** (breed a mushroom, win the Grasslands battle, forage, claim spice, then cook a new recipe), which also gives **19 Ladles** (8 + 5 + 6 across Gobo 3–5) and gates the portal out of W4 town. (H)
- **Meals are account-wide, permanent, always-on bonuses** (bonus = level × base per level × multipliers). The first level comes from **discovering** the recipe in a kitchen with spices; every further level is bought at the Dinner Menu with copies of that meal. Base max level is **30**. (H)
- **Speed is the whole game.** Meals per hour per kitchen = kitchen speed ÷ the meal's cook requirement (base speed 10/h). Cooking skill level does **not** change meal speed; it only drives Ladle income and Cooking EXP. The biggest W4-stage speed levers are: the kitchen's own **Speed upgrade (+10% per level, multiplicative)**, **more kitchens (10 total, bought with coins: 8.2M, 75M, 400M, 8B, 50B, 2.13T, 60T, 2Q, 1e17)**, the **Diamond Chef** bubble (multiplies speed by up to ~1.3x **per meal at Lv 11+**, compounding), and the speed meals Egg, Corndog, Soda and Cabbage. (H)
- **Each kitchen's three upgrades cost one specific spice each:** kitchen *k* uses spice 2k−2 (Speed), 2k−1 (Fire), 2k (Luck), counting spices from 0 (Grasslands). So kitchen 1 needs Grasslands/Jungle/Encroaching Forest, kitchen 4 needs Beach Docks/Coarse Mountains/Twilight Desert. A territory you have not won blocks that kitchen's upgrades. (H)
- **Recipe discovery:** pick 1–4 spices; the game lists the possible meals (each spice's own meal, plus meals near the **sum of the spice indices**). Fire must fill to a requirement set by the highest possible meal; each extra fire level costs 3x more and multiplies the new-recipe chance by 1.5x. Already-discovered meals still sit in the pool and dilute the odds, so **single-spice recipes are the fastest, surest discoveries**. Blood Berserker's **Taste Test** finishes all recipes instantly. (H)
- **Ladles** are cooking's Time Candy: one ladle pushes **all kitchens forward 1 hour** (whole stack used at once). They come from characters AFK in the W4 Kitchen area (15 × an efficiency step per day, × Cooking AFK rate), quests, and the Killroy skull shop. Spend them on meals close to a level-up or on long recipes; never hoard. (H)
- **Spices** come from Breeding territories (see the Breeding dossier). Claims are capped at **100 per day** (Toolbox constant; the Trasher pet gene adds +25 if it sits in the Fence Yard before reset). (H Toolbox / M game cap)
- **Min-max order on a fresh W4 account:** finish Gobo → discover every meal you can with single spices (cheap fire) → level speed meals (Egg, Corndog, Cabbage) and the kitchen Speed upgrades → buy kitchens 2–4 with coins as soon as affordable → get every meal to **Lv 11 (Diamond plate)** and level **Diamond Chef** → then damage (Turkey of Thank, Octoplop, Onion), Lab (Garlicles Bread, Pancakes, Sawdust) and Breeding (Frenk Fries, Pizza, Donut, Chocala Truffle) meals. (M; mechanics H, the exact order is ours)
- **Meal level cap rises late:** +10 per Jade Emporium bonus (×2, W6), +up to 60 from the Causticolumn artifact (W5 Sailing), +up to 20 from the Death Bringer Grimoire, +30 from the 6th W7 Lore Boss, and **+10 from the v1.23 Menagerie unlock "Boost the Max Cooking LV by +10"** (170 total). The wiki still says 160. (H)
- **Surprises / stale:** the wiki and the community sheet both list a 160 max meal level, which is one patch old (v1.23 adds +10). Toolbox's own "possible meals" helper (`getMealsFromSpiceValues`) uses a simpler rule than the game's `PossibleRecipes2` (no remap for spice sums ≥ 71), so for 3–4 high spices its list can differ from the in-game list; trust the game. (H)

Kitchens are upgraded with **spices** from Breeding territories; **ladles** fast-forward kitchens. ### Recipe discovery (new recipes)

- Put 1–4 spices in an idle kitchen. The game builds the list of possible meals (`PossibleRecipes2`, N.js @7881938): (H)
  1. each selected spice's own meal (`RANDOlist[49][spice]`, the right column of the table above);
  2. let S = the sum of the selected spice indices. Remap: if S < 71, S = min(55, S); if S < 87, S = min(65, S − 14); else S − 22;
  3. add up to min(spices, 3) meals centred on S (S−1, S, S+1 for 3+ spices), skipping any that are a "spice meal" from step 1's list.
- **Fire requirement** (`CookingFireREQ`, @7890692) = 2 × cook requirement of the **highest** possible meal (index capped at 66) × 25^max(0, index − 66). Each further **fire level** needs 3x the previous amount (`CookingFireREQlvMULTI` = 3) and multiplies the undiscovered-meal chance by 1.5x. (H)
- **Odds** (`CookingNewRecipeOdds`, @7893066): the possible meals are sorted low to high and get base weights 1, 0.20, 0.10, 0.05, 0.02, 0.01, 0.004, 0.001, 0.0005, 0.0003 (`RANDOlist[50]`); an **undiscovered** meal's weight is × Luck × 1.5^fire level, a discovered one keeps its base weight. A discovered meal in the pool therefore steals odds. (H code; M on how the weights are normalised, which the wiki's "1 in X" tables are consistent with)
- **Practical rule:** a one-spice recipe has at most two outcomes, so it is the fastest and surest way to discover most early meals. Multi-spice combos matter for the meals that no single spice gives (see the wiki's "Recipe for the Earliest Chance" column for each meal). (M, consistent with the wiki table)
- **Accelerators:** Fire upgrade, Pie and Canopy meals (`Rcook`), Spice Stamp (+2/lv New Recipe Speed), Spook Pint vial, Massive Troll card (fire part capped at +50%), Richelin (2x), Diamond Chef bubble and Fluoride atom (both apply to fire too), and Blood Berserker's **Taste Test** active: "Finishes all recipes, but the kitchens keep {% of the fire". Ladles also advance recipe fire ("all your kitchens ahead by 1 hour"). (H: N.js `"CookingFIRE"`; talents.json `TASTE_TEST`; M for ladles on recipes)

### Leveling meals (Dinner Menu)

### Spices and spice claiming

- Spices accumulate per Breeding **territory** while foraging Mobs sit there (`Territory[i]`: progress, …, spice name, amount). Claiming moves the amount into `Meals[3][spice]` (the spice bank). Foraging speed, Mob genes and territory battles are the Breeding dossier's job. (H: N.js claim handler @9794245)
- Each claim adds 1 to `OptionsListAccount[100]`, which resets to 0 at the daily reset (@15096626). Claims stop when the counter reaches the cap. Toolbox's cap is **`maxNumberOfSpiceClicks = 100`** (TB [cooking.ts L1167](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-4/cooking.ts#L1167)); the **Trasher** Mob gene adds +25 daily claims if it is in the Fence Yard before the day starts. (H Toolbox constant and gene text; M that the in-game cap is exactly 100, I could not resolve the minified `_GenINFO[115]`)
- **Spice Spillage** star talent: chance to claim all Mob spices automatically when you claim 1 h+ of AFK gains; a W4 merit raises its max level. Superbits "Spice Is Nice" (1.5x) and "Spice Is Nicer" (2x) multiply all claimed spice (W5 Gaming). (H: shared-data `merits[3][3]`, `superbitsUpgrades[14,17]`, star-talent text @13954983)
- Community: territories produce more slowly the longer they go unclaimed, so frequent claims (or Spice Spillage) produce more. (L: Incomplete Guide W4 tab)
- Achievement **Hunned Times a Day**: 10,000 individual spice claims, gives 8 Tab 2 talent points. (H: achievements.json 230)

- **Per-session actions:**
  1. **Level every meal whose stock covers its next level** (Toolbox's "Meals ready to level up" alert). Leveling is free value; stock has no other use except Lab and Mob-upgrade costs. (H: TB `utility/dashboard/account.js` L1000-L1017)
  2. **Claim spices** in Breeding (up to the daily cap) and spend them on kitchen upgrades, Speed first.
  3. **Check every kitchen is busy.** A kitchen idle at `status 1` is wasted. A kitchen stuck on a recipe at `status 3` adds no meal speed: if its fire bar is long, ladle it or switch it back to a meal. (H)
  4. **Re-point kitchens** when a meal reaches the max level (30 on W4): a kitchen cooking a capped meal only makes stock for chips, jewels and Mob upgrades.
  5. **Spend ladles** on a meal close to its next level or a near-done recipe; do not sit on stacks. Toolbox's `mealLadleCost` alert counts meals whose next level costs fewer ladles than a threshold. (H)
  6. **Idle/capped states:** every meal at max level with nothing left to discover; kitchen upgrades unaffordable because a spice's territory is not won; daily spice claims used up.
- **AFK Setup implications:**
  - One character should **AFK in the W4 Kitchen area** (its AFK target is the Cooking "monster") for Cooking EXP and ladles. Best fit: a **Blood Berserker** (Overflowing Ladle, Waiting To Cool), with Ladle Stamp, cooking gear and the Chefs Essentials PO box. (H mechanics; M choice, consistent with the gist's "Blood Berserker = boost the Kitchen")
  - That character is then **not** in the Lab or on a fighting map, so it competes with the Lab dossier's tube slots and with kill-count Goals. Pick the character whose fighting is least valuable. (M)
  - Kitchens run on their own: the AFK Setup only needs every kitchen on a useful meal before leaving. Over a long AFK Stretch, put the slowest meals (biggest cook req) on the most upgraded kitchens.
  - The logged-in character's star sign and Farming level change kitchen speed only while that character is loaded; this does not affect offline progress per se (M), so do not reshuffle star signs for it.
