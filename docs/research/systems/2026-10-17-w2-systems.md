## Answer

- **Alchemy is the W2 system with the most Leverage, and it is mostly account-wide.** Passive (small) bubbles, vials, liquids, Pay 2 Win and sigils are shared by the whole account. Only two parts are per character: which cauldron (or liquid, or sigil) a character is assigned to, and which **active (big) bubbles** it has equipped. A character gets 1 active slot, a 2nd from the Bribe "Changing the Code" (1.5M coins), and a 3rd from Breeding arena wave 100 (W4). (H)
- **Brew is not automatic: stored brew only becomes bubbles when the player spends it.** Each new bubble needs `MaxCauldronQTY(n)` brew per attempt (0.01, 0.15, 0.75, 5, 20, 88, 277, 677, 1.4k … for the 1st, 2nd … bubble) and succeeds with `139 × 0.73^(n+1) / max(1, 1 + 0.1n)` % (101% → 67% → 45% → 30% → 21% → 14% → 9.6% …) times luck multipliers. Toolbox already computes `cauldrons.*.newBubble.expectedBubbles`. **Both fixture accounts had brew banked for many attempts and, on the W2 account, no active bubble equipped on any character.** That is the single most common W2 mistake we can detect. (H; the fixture finding is an aggregate observation)
- **Vials unlock by dropping the item on the W2 town cauldron and rolling a d100**: success if the roll ≥ the vial's "score" (Copper 1+, Oak 10+ … Tail Time 91+, W3+ items mostly 99+). Each drop uses one daily attempt (3 + the Pay 2 Win "Attempts" level, refilled at the daily reset). Upgrading is deterministic: level n → n+1 costs the vial's material (100, 1k, 2.5k, 10k, 50k, 100k, 500k, 1M, 5M, 25M, 100M, 1B) plus 3n of its liquid, up to **Lv 13**. (H)
- **Liquids cap and stop.** Water Droplets from Alchemy Lv 1, Liquid N2 at 20, Trench H2O at 35, Toxic Hg at 80 (highest Alchemy level of any character). A full liquid stops regenerating, so every Play Session should spend liquid (bubbles, vials, Liquid Shop dailies such as the Bargain Tag) before leaving. Toolbox has `liquidCauldrons[].maxLiquid` and an alert. (H for code, M for the level gates, which are the wiki's and AutoReview's)
- **Sigils are W4-gated:** nothing charges until the W4 Lab bonus "Sigils of Olden Alchemy" is connected. Then characters assigned to a sigil in Alchemy add Sigil EXP per hour. For W1–W3 the only action is to know it exists. (H)
- **Arcade (W2 town clown, class Lv 8):** balls accrue at 1 per `4000 / (1 + bonus%)` s (never faster than 1 per 1800 s), banked for at most **48 h + up to 10 h from stamps**. Past that cap, balls are lost, so a Play Session that is 2 days apart must open the Arcade. Gold balls buy permanent levels (Lv 100 cap) in a rotating shop. In the current rotation the W2-relevant buys are Class XP multi, Cap for all Liquids and Gold Ball Gain. (H)
- **Poppy's Fish Pond (the Kangaroo minigame):** reached through the Secret Map bought from the W2 town shop. Fish bank in the background but **only convert while the Poppy menu is open**, so opening it every Play Session is the whole loop. The real-game payoff is "Bonuses from Poppy" (Fishing Eff, Defence, Fishing XP, Accuracy, Total DMG, AFK Gains, Cash, one step every 7 levels), multiplied by Megafish. (H)
- **Killroy and the Bonus Ballot are weekly.** Killroy: 2 rooms a week (3rd from a 1% Skull Shop roll), each locked to one class family. Skulls buy candy, pearls, 10 arcade balls (50 a week), library checkouts and more. AutoReview's rule is Timer:Skulls at 3:2, Skulls capped at 900 levels, and only 1 level in Talent Points, Dungeon Credits and Pearls. The Ballot is a **server-wide** vote. If the winning bonus belongs to a world you have not reached, you get 1.25× damage instead (this week's winner is a W3 Equinox bonus, so W2 accounts get the damage). (H, except the AutoReview ratios, M)
- **W2 boss, keys and gates:** Efaunt (Normal 1 key, Chaotic 5, Gilded 40) drops the **Glacier Quartz** that the Constructor Crow needs to open the W3 portal. Keys come from Djonnut's 3 quests (5 each), then daily: **one key per character who finished Djonnut's 2nd quest, banked up to 3 days**. King Doot also drops a key 80% of the time. **Subclass = Speccius** (100 Kebab Sticks + 50 Crabby Cakey, no level gate). Journeyman → **Maestro is a different quest chain (Cactolyte, Djonnuttown)**. The Post Office opens through Postboy Pablob (5 Empty Boxes). (H)
- **For ActiveOn:** almost every Next Action here maps to a Toolbox field or an existing Toolbox alert: liquids full, vial attempts unused, vials upgradeable, Bargain Tag/gem dailies, affordable Pay 2 Win, empty bubble slots, characters with no alchemy activity, arcade balls near cap, Killroy not done, keys overdue (≥ 3 days). ADR 0005 holds: no new formulas are needed for W2. (H)

## Alchemy Bubbles (brewing cauldrons and bubbles)

- **What it is:** four brewing cauldrons (Power/orange, Quicc/green, High-IQ/purple, Kazam/yellow). Characters assigned to a cauldron fill its brew bar. Brew is spent on attempts to discover the cauldron's next bubble, or on upgrading the cauldron itself ("Boost"). Bubbles are levelled with materials plus liquid and give stat bonuses. Small bubbles are passive and account-wide. Big bubbles are "active" and only count when equipped.
- **Unlocks:** World 2 town (YumYum Grotto, map 50). "Each character must go to World 2 town and click on the cauldron! They cannot gain experience nor be assigned to cauldrons until this is done!" ([wiki Alchemy](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Alchemy)). The game's codex text matches (N.js `Alchemy_1` codex entry @14698230 region). No level gate is stated by the wiki or the game's codex text, and AutoReview treats Alchemy as open from Alchemy Lv 1. Cowbo Jones's 5th and 6th quests ("Alchemial Apprentice": unlock 1 bubble and buy 1 Distilled Water; then unlock 2 vials) are the in-game tutorial. TB [`data/website-data/quests.json`](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/data/website-data/quests.json) `Cowbo_Jones`. (H)
- **Scope:**
  - Account-wide: bubble levels, cauldron brew progress, cauldron Boost levels, which bubbles are unlocked.
  - Per character: the cauldron it is assigned to (`CauldronJobs1[charIndex]`), its Alchemy skill level (which sets how much brew it adds), and its equipped active bubbles (`CauldronBubbles[charIndex]`, 1–3 slots). (H)
  - Class-dependent: the class-multiplier bubbles. **Warriors Rule** (orange #1), **Archer or Bust** (green #1) and **Mage is Best** (purple #1) multiply the *small* bubbles of their own cauldron, for characters of that class family only. They exclude index 0, the cauldron multi bubble (#16), bubbles at index ≥ 30, Carpenter, and any stat tagged `passz`, `ACTIVE` or `AllCharz`. TB [`alchemy.ts` L393-413 `isClassMultiplied`](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-2/alchemy.ts#L393-L413), [L418-479 `getBubbleBonus`](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-2/alchemy.ts#L418-L479). Kazam (yellow) has no class bubble. (H)
- **Inputs → outputs:**
  - **Brew rate** per character ≈ `round(AlchLv^0.8) × (1 + stamp + guild + Brewstachio + Potion Package)% × (1 + P2W speed × AlchLv × WIS curve × (Busy Brewin' + …))% × Mafioso`. The cauldron multiplier is (vial brew speed) × (P2W cauldron speed) × Ivory cauldron 1.5× × (1 + 5% × Boost "speed" level). TB [`getCharacterBrewSpeed` L706-761](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-2/alchemy.ts#L706-L761), [`getCauldronBrewMulti` L763-781](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-2/alchemy.ts#L763-L781). Characters assigned to brew also gain Alchemy EXP. (H)
  - **New bubble:** brew per attempt `MaxCauldronQTY(n)` = 0.01, 0.15, 0.75, 5, 20 for n = 0–4, then `1 + (3(n−3))^2.2 × 1.3^(n−3)`. Chance `PctChanceNewBubble(n)` = `139 × 0.73^(n+1) / max(1, 0.1n + 1)` %, times luck (`round(10 × (1 + 0.05 × LuckLv))/10`), the P2W New Bubble multiplier, the Bubble Breakthrough talent and Ivory 1.5×. The game only counts the unbroken run of unlocked bubbles from the first. TB [L593-614](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-2/alchemy.ts#L593-L614), [L653-704](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-2/alchemy.ts#L653-L704). The wiki's charge table agrees ([Alchemy](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Alchemy)). (H)
  - **Boost** (spend a full bar on the cauldron): four stats per cauldron. Speed is +5% brew per level, Luck raises new-bubble odds (+10% every 2 levels), Cost lowers bubble costs, and Extra (2x) gives a chance to boost twice. TB caps brewing Boosts at 170 ([`CAULDRONS_MAX_LEVELS` L72-81](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-2/alchemy.ts#L72-L81)), and the wiki agrees. (H)
  - **Levelling a bubble** costs its 1–4 listed materials plus liquid. Material cost = `base × (1.35 − 0.3L/(50+L))^L` (index ≤ 14) or `(1.37 − 0.28L/(60+L))^L` (index > 14), times the discounts (per-colour Bargain bubble, the cauldron's Cost boost, Undeveloped Costs + Barley Brew vial, Bargain Tag, S-M-R-T), clamped to [1, 1e9]. Liquid cost = `base + floor(L/20)`. TB [`getBubbleUpgradeCost` L129-143](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-2/alchemy.ts#L129-L143), constants in `shared-data.json` `gameTables.bubbleCost`. (H)
  - **Value curves** (TB [`utility/wiki/growth.mjs`](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/utility/wiki/growth.mjs)): `decay` = x1·L/(L+x2), which reaches **half its max at L = x2**, 75% at 3·x2 and 90% at 9·x2. `decayMulti` = 1 + x1·L/(L+x2). `addDECAY` = x1·L (linear to Lv 50,000). `bigBase` = x1 + x2·L (linear; most active bubbles). (H)

- **Feeds / fed by:**
  - Feeds: total STR/AGI/WIS/LUK (Roid Ragin, Swift Steppin, Stable Jenius, Lotto Skills) and so damage. Skill efficiency (Hearty Diggy, Hocus Choppus, the three Tools bubbles) and multi-resource (Wyoming Blood, Sploosh Sploosh, Bug², Molto Loggo) feed Mining, Fishing, Choppin and Catching.   - Fed by: Alchemy skill levels (characters), Alchemy Vials (brew speed vials 0–3, Barley Brew cost), Liquids (every bubble costs liquid), Pay 2 Win (speed, new bubble odds), W1 materials and monster drops (most bubble costs are W1–W3 items, so W1 farming characters feed this), Stamps (brew speed, bubble cost), Gem Shop (Ivory Bubble Cauldrons 1.5×), Bribe "Changing the Code" (2nd slot), Breeding arena wave 100 (3rd slot), W4 Lab "No Bubble Left Behind" (auto-levels the lowest bubbles), W5 Divinity Kattlekruk (daily bubble levels), W3 Atom Collider Boron (pay particles instead of the 1st material once it costs ≥ 1e8). (H)
- **Per-session actions:**
  1. Spend banked brew in each cauldron: roll for new bubbles while the attempt is cheap relative to the bar; once new-bubble odds fall low (≈ 5%, around the 9th bubble of a cauldron), alternate with Boost (Speed first). Toolbox: `cauldrons.<c>.progress`, `.req`, `.attempts`, `.newBubble.chance`, `.newBubble.expectedBubbles`. (H for the fields; the "≈ 5%" switch point is L)
  2. Level bubbles with the materials and liquid on hand, in the priority order below, and use the day's Bargain Tag first (see Liquids).
  3. Check every character has its active slots filled with the right big bubble for its AFK activity (see AFK Setup implications). Toolbox raises `missingBubbles` per character ([`utility/dashboard/characters.js` L110-127](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/utility/dashboard/characters.js#L110-L127)). (H)
  4. Check no character has `activity === -1` (assigned nowhere) unless intended. Toolbox alert `noActivity`, same lines. (H)
  - Idle/capped state: brew keeps accumulating with no cap found in code, so there is no waste in leaving it. The waste is the unrealised bubbles and Boosts, and characters with no assignment. (M, no clamp found in the parser or code paths read)
- **AFK Setup implications:**
  - Every character that is AFK-ing anything should also sit in a cauldron or liquid. Alchemy assignment is a town job that runs alongside the character's field or skill AFK; it does not replace it. (M: wiki and codex describe assignment, not exclusivity; both fixtures had all active characters assigned)
  - Put characters in the cauldron whose next bubble or Boost matters most this week. High-WIS characters (mages) brew faster, because each brewer's own WIS curve multiplies its speed. Brewstachio's class multi follows the *logged-in* character, so it is not a reason to pick a brewer. (H for the formula; the choice is L)

- **Min-max by stage** (passive bubbles; AutoReview tiers above are the cited Milestones, the ordering is ours, L):
  - **Early W2 (first week in W2):** unlock bubbles in all four cauldrons, since the first 4–5 are near-free (0.01–20 brew, ≥ 20% odds). Then level the class stat bubble (#0) and the class multi (#1) of every class you play, Hearty Diggy / Hocus Choppus (skill eff), the three Tools bubbles, FMJ (defence), Shaquracy (accuracy), Droppin Loads (drop rate) and Prowesessary. Put early materials into **Undeveloped Costs** (Kazam #6) and the colour Bargain bubbles (#14) once they appear, because they cut every later cost.
  - **Mid W2 → start of W3:** reach AutoReview tier 2 (20 bubbles; stat bubbles 25, class multis 13). Level Hammer Hammer for anvil characters. Warriors Rule, Archer or Bust and Mage is Best scale every small bubble of their colour on that class, so on a 1-of-each-class account they are worth more than any single small bubble.
  - **W3:** Call Me Bob, Call Me Ash and Call Me Pope for the new skills; Name I Guess (base damage from MP) for mages; All For Kill (attack talents count more offline). Aim for tier 3 (40 bubbles) before W4.
  - **W4+ outline:** No Bubble Left Behind (Lab) auto-levels the cheapest bubbles; the Multorange/Premigreen/Severapurple multipliers (#16) and Laaarrrryyyy (Kazam #9, chance for double upgrades) become the main targets; the 3rd active slot comes from Breeding.
  - **Per class:** a Warrior gets Warriors Rule on orange smalls, an Archer gets Archer or Bust on green, a Mage gets Mage is Best on purple. Buff Boi, Fast Boi and Smart Boi Talent (#13) give talent points to their class only (Fast Boi also to Journeyman). Power Trione, Tritwo and Trithree (#24) are class-locked damage. (H from bubble text)
- **Common mistakes:**
  - **Brew left unspent.** Detect: `alchemy.cauldrons.<c>.attempts > 0`, or `newBubble.expectedBubbles ≥ 1`. On 2026-10-17 both fixture accounts had banked attempts in every cauldron (one cauldron on the W2 account had brew for ~800 attempts at its current requirement). (H)
  - **No active bubbles equipped.** Detect: `characters[].equippedBubbles` with null entries while the account owns the 2nd-slot bribe (`account.bribes[15].done`). The W2 fixture had 0 equipped on all 5 characters despite owning the bribe. (H)
  - Levelling linear stat bubbles forever while a `decay` bubble sits below its x2 "half" point. Detect from levels vs x2 in the data. (L)
  - Never touching Boost, so new-bubble odds collapse with Luck at 0. Detect: `boosts.luck.level === 0` with ≥ 6 bubbles unlocked. (L)

## Alchemy Vials

- **What it is:** a collection of 87 vials, each tied to one item. Dropping that item on the W2 town cauldron rolls to discover the vial. Each vial is then levelled 1–13 with the same item plus liquid, for a permanent, account-wide bonus.
- **Unlocks:** available with Alchemy (W2 town). **Discovery roll (code):** the item must be dropped on the cauldron area in map 50, while `CauldronP2W[5][0]` (daily attempts) > 0. One attempt is consumed (15% chance to keep it, once the P2W RNG bonus is ≥ 100%). `_VialRNG = randomInt(1,100)`, and the vial unlocks if `_VialRNG ≥ round(100 − AlchemyVialItemsPCT[i])`. N.js item-drop handler @8656112–8658500, table `AlchemyVialItemsPCT` @14092212. Daily attempts refill to `VialAttempts` (3 + P2W Attempts level) in the daily routine (@15121958). Toolbox's `discoveryScore` is that roll threshold. (H; the 15% keep rule is M, a minified condition)
- **Scope:** fully account-wide. Exception: the Shaman talent "Virile Vials" counts vials at Lv 4+ (AutoReview `alchemy_vials.py` L30-31, [link](https://github.com/TwoSpookyBoos/IdleOnAutoReviewBot/blob/05ac4496c335644dedd68e0be65ac893971efc73/mysite/w2/alchemy_vials.py#L30-L31)). (M)
- **Inputs → outputs:** item drops + daily attempts → discovery. Upgrade Lv n → n+1 costs the vial's item and `3n` of its liquid. Lv 13 max. ## Alchemy Pay 2 Win (cauldron upgrades)

- **What it is:** the coin sink inside Alchemy (the tab is named "Pay 2 Win", but it costs in-game coins, not gems). Coins buy permanent levels for each brewing cauldron (Speed, New Bubble chance, Boost Requirement), each liquid (Regen, Capacity), vials (daily Attempts, RNG) and the player (Alchemy Speed and Extra EXP per Alchemy level).
- **Unlocks:** with Alchemy. (H)
- **Scope:** account-wide. The Player upgrades scale with the alchemy level of whoever is brewing. Their max level equals the highest Alchemy level on the account ([wiki](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Alchemy); AutoReview `alchemy_p2w.py` L35). (H/M)
- **Inputs → outputs:** coins → levels. Costs (TB [`getP2wCauldronCost` L312-326](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-2/alchemy.ts#L312-L326)) (H):

  | New Bubble Chance (×4) | 125 | 3200 × (1.18 − 0.145L/(100+L))^L | 2.39× odds |
  | Boost Requirement (×4) | 100 | 3750 × (1.2 − 0.14L/(100+L))^L | −35% boost reqs |
  | Liquid Regen (×4) | 100 | 2500 × (1.19 − 0.135L/(100+L))^L | — |
  | Liquid Capacity (×4) | 80 | 3500 × (1.2 − 0.13L/(100+L))^L | — |
  | Vial Attempts | 15 | (in game) | 18 attempts a day |
  | Vial RNG | 45 | (in game) | 420% "epic gamer moment" chance |
  | Player Alchemy Speed / Extra EXP | highest Alchemy Lv | (in game) | per-level % |

## Arcade

- **What it is:** a pachinko minigame run by the juggling Clown in W2 town. Balls accrue over time. Each launched ball lands in a bucket (coins, gems, obol fragments, time candy, more balls, golden balls, portals to better boards). **Gold balls** buy permanent levels in the Arcade Shop, which shows a rotating set of bonuses.
- **Unlocks:** talk to the Clown in YumYum Grotto at **class Lv 8**. N.js `addDialogueFor("Clown")` @15433376: `addLine_LevelReq … LevelReq:8` ("Noobs cant play! Return at Lv. 8"). The wiki says "accessed via the Juggling clown … as well as Quick Ref" ([Arcade](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Arcade)). (H)
- **Scope:** account-wide (balls, gold balls and shop levels are in `OptionsListAccount` and `ArcadeUpg`). (H)
- **Inputs → outputs:**
  - Ball accrual: `secPerBall = 4000 / (1 + (achievements + Ball Pickle Jar vial + 5 × task + min(50, stamps) + Weighted Marbles bribe) / 100)`, never below 1800 s. Claimable time is at most `3600 × (48 + min(10, stamp))` s. ## Poppy's Fish Pond (the Kangaroo minigame)

- **What it is:** an incremental "clicker" run by Poppy the Kangaroo Mouse in W2 (added v2.11 "The Roo", 2024-06-20). Fish buy upgrades that speed up fishing, and the "Bonuses from Poppy" upgrade gives seven permanent real-game stats. Resets (Fisheroo Reset, then Greatest Catch for a **Megafish**) give multipliers; Tartar fish fund a second upgrade tree.
- **Unlocks:** buy the **Secret Map** from the W2 town (YumYum Grotto) vendor; use it to find **The Oasis**, after which the map teleports there ([wiki Poppy](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Poppy_the_Kangaroo_Mouse%27s_Fish_Pond); [Changelog 2024 v2.11](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Changelog_2024): "Find her using the new Map bought from the World 2 Town shop (costs like 15 gold coins)"). ## Bonus Ballot (and the Multi Meritocracy outline)

- **What it is:** the Voter Slime in W2 town. Each week the whole server votes on 3 candidate bonuses, and the winner applies to everyone the following week. Multi Meritocracy is a second, W6-unlocked ballot in the same place.
- **Unlocks:** reach W2 (AutoReview `bonus_ballot.py` L91-100). Meritocracy needs Spirit Village (W6 town) reached, and its base is 25% until the Demonflesh is handed in. TB [`voteBallot.ts` L25-33](https://github.com/Morta1/IdleonToolbox/blob/6827d3f2afcaa63336446f2a08c92e0e060cbcd1/parsers/world-2/voteBallot.ts#L25-L33). (H)
- **Scope:** server-wide outcome; the bonus multiplier (`voteMulti`) is per account. (H)

## W2 bosses and keys

- **What it is:** Efaunt (the W2 world boss in Efaunt's Tomb, entered with Efaunt's Tomb Keys), plus two secret desert minibosses (Biggie Hours and King Doot) and the Desert dungeon boss reached through Bandit Bob (dungeons are another dossier). Weekly Battles, the weekly boss in W2 town, belongs to the cross-cutting dossier.
- **Unlocks:**
  - Efaunt: reach the last W2 map (Djonnuttown area) and hold keys. Djonnut's quests give the first keys. (H)
  - Biggie Hours: craft **Googley Eyes** (recipe from Crabcakes) and drop them on the broken hourglass next to the Colosseum in The Mimic Hole (map 52). No cooldown. N.js drop handler: item `Quest35` dropped on map 52 spawns `babaHour` (@8658700 region; reading `Quest35` as the Googley Eyes is M). [Wiki Biggie Hours](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Biggie_Hours). (H)

The first summon needs one player of each base class present on the server; each kill drops a Dootophone for solo summons. [Wiki King Doot](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/King_Doot). (M: wiki prose)

## Code vs other sources, and things to flag

- **Liquid Shop "A Pair Of Gems" price step:** game data ×10.5 per buy; the wiki page shows ×8.5. Prefer game data. (H)
- **Wiki "Score" on Vials is the d100 roll needed, not a chance.** The chance is (101 − score)%. (H)
- **Efaunt HP:** game data `MonsterHPTotal` = 1M; wiki infobox 4M. Unresolved; prefer game data. (M)
- **Djonnut daily key count:** code counts characters with Djonnut dialogue > 5; Toolbox counts > 6. Possible one-off undercount in Toolbox. Worth an upstream check before ActiveOn relies on `amountPerDay`. (M)
- **Active bubble slots:** Toolbox's `missingBubbles` alert assumes 2 slots (3 with arena wave 100). It does not check the "Changing the Code" bribe, so on an account without that bribe it would over-report by one. (H)
- **Killroy 2nd room:** the wiki says it unlocks at W4; Toolbox always shows 2 rooms. Unresolved in code. (M)
- **AutoReview tiers** are dated 2026-09-23 (before v1.22's follow-ups and v1.23). W2 bubble, vial and sigil data have not changed in the v1.2x changelogs read, so the tiers stand, but entries citing them are one patch old. Mark them **Stale-risk: low**. (M)
- **Possibly outdated:** the Killroy NPC wiki page (2022). The Arcade wiki page was edited after v1.23 (2026-10-17 10:54Z).
