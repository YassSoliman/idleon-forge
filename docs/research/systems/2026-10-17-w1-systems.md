## Answer

- **Stamps are the biggest W1 lever, and both MVP accounts under-use them.** There are 128 stamps in the data (121 obtainable). Coins buy levels up to a stamp's cap; materials raise the cap by a fixed step. Carry capacity is the real wall, since the material has to fit in one character's inventory. The W2 fixture had 8 stamps and the W3 fixture 17, out of 121. Collecting more stamps is a top-ranked Goal for both. ([Stamps](w1-systems/stamps.md))
- **Raise stamp caps in batches.** The W3 Hydrogen atom cuts cap-raise material cost by (its level)% per day logged in, up to 90%. It resets to 0% whenever *any* stamp's cap goes up. Gilded Stamps (from Rift 26, W4) cut one cap raise by 95% and stack with the atom. The Envelope Pile sigil and two Alchemy vials cut it further. Skill stamps lose part of their bonus once their level runs past the character's level in that skill. (H, game code)
- **Bribes are cheap, permanent and account-wide; buy each set as soon as it opens.** Set 1 costs ~36K coins in total and set 2 ~0.7M. Set 3 includes "Changing the Code" (2nd bubble slot for every character, 1.5M). Insider Trading's text says −5% stamp coin cost, but the game applies **8%**. ([Bribes](w1-systems/bribes.md))
- **Anvil production is per character and runs during AFK, so every character should always have every hammer producing.** It is the main source of Smithing EXP. Points: +1 per Smithing level, up to 600 bought with coins, and up to 700 bought with monster materials. Spend them on speed. Capacity comes from the Material bag and is capped at 2B. ([Anvil and Smithing](w1-systems/anvil-smithing.md))
- **The Forge is account-wide.** It unlocks when any character reaches class Lv 18. Never leave a slot empty, and buy slots and speed first. Smelt times were cut by 80% in v2.29 (Feb 2025), so old guides overstate them. ([Forge](w1-systems/forge.md))
- **Statues are per character until each one is upgraded to Gold.** Gold makes a statue account-wide but keeps only the *highest* character's level. Until then, deposit each statue type on one character only, or the rest are lost. Gold costs `floor((1e5 + 25e3·n)^(0.95+0.03·n))` coins for statue n (99K for Power, up to a 2.1B cap). Level n needs `round(n^1.17 · 1.35^(n/10) + 1)` statues. Both fixtures have only W1–W2 statues at Gold and levels under 10. ([Statues](w1-systems/statues.md))
- **The Post Office is really W2: Postboy Pablob appears once any character reaches Lv 50,** and his quest needs 5 Empty Boxes from Anvil Tab 2. Orders are account-wide. **Every character gets the whole account's box total to spend on its own boxes.** Neither fixture has unlocked it, which makes it a ready-made Goal. ([Post Office](w1-systems/post-office.md))
- **Orion the Owl (W1 map 42, Grand Owl Perch) is a free, account-wide idle clicker that keeps generating while you are away.** Bonuses of Orion never resets and cycles +Class XP, +Base DMG, +Total DMG, +Skill XP, +Drop Rate and +All Stats. Megafeathers multiply those bonuses. Each Play Session, restart and mega-reset whenever you can afford it, and always buy Bonuses of Orion. Both fixtures already hold 12 Megafeathers. ([Owl](w1-systems/owl.md))
- **Amarok, the W1 boss, drops the Desert Topaz that Builder Bird needs to open the W2 portal.** Entry costs 1, 5 or 30 Forest Villa Keys (Normal, Chaotic, Radiant). Dog Bone gives 1 key per day for **each character that has finished his quests**, banked up to 3 days. Running his quests on every character multiplies the key income. Amarok gear (Slabs) lasts into W5–W6. ([Amarok and W1 quests](w1-systems/amarok-and-quests.md))
- **W1 town NPCs unlock when the account's highest class level reaches a threshold:** Storage Chest at 7, Mr Pigibank (Stamps and Bribes) at 14, Forge at 18, Town Marble (Statues) at 22, Grasslands Gary (Tasks) at 28 and Obol Altar at 30. Postboy Pablob (W2) appears at 50. The wiki says stamps open at "class level 12", but the code checks 14. **The Arcade is W2** (Yum-Yum Desert), so it is in the W2 dossier ([#21](https://github.com/YassSoliman/idleon-forge/issues/21)), not here.

```
   │        ▲ cap raise ◄── materials (mining/choppin/fishing/catching, monster drops, anvil products, bars)
   │        ▲ cheaper  ◄── Bribe "Insider Trading", Hydrogen atom (W3), Envelope Pile sigil (W2/W4 Lab), Blue Flav and Venison Malt vials (Alchemy), Gilded Stamps (Rift 26, W4)

   ├──► Forge upgrades ──► bars ──► gear, stamps, Refinery and vials (W2/W3)
   ├──► Anvil points ──► anvil products (Thread, Nails ...) ──► crafting, stamps, Post Office orders, Smithing EXP
   └──► Gold statues ──► account-wide statue bonuses
Monster kills / crystal mobs ──► statues (deposit) ──► per-character statue bonuses
Orion (passive feathers) ──► Bonuses of Orion: Class XP, Skill XP, Drop Rate, damage, All Stats (account-wide)
Post Office orders (skilling resources, gear, drops) ──► boxes ──► per-character bonuses (incl. Anvil speed, skill efficiency, Drop Rate)
Dog Bone quests (per character) ──► Forest Villa Keys ──► Amarok ──► Desert Topaz (W2 portal), Amarok Slabs (gear), card
```
