## Class: Warrior -> Squire -> Divine Knight

- **What it is:** The Warrior line is the strength class line. Warrior (base, Lv 10) is a mining specialist, Squire (subclass) a Construction/Refinery specialist, Divine Knight (elite) a Gaming specialist [C1][C42][C40][C41]. Barbarian is the line's other subclass and is covered by its own dossier.
- **Unlocks:** Warrior: Promotheus, needs Lv 10 and a class pick [C2]. Squire: Speccius (W2), 100 Kebab Sticks and 50 Crabby Cakey, then pick the subclass [C3]. No level gate is in the quest data (open question Q1). Divine Knight: Eliteus on W4 map 154, 10 Greenie Gang kills, then pick [C4]. Royal Guardian follows in W5 [C39].
- **Scope:** Talents, points, presets and class EXP are per character [C7]. Account-wide: the Squire family bonus (best Squire-line character), Warrior STR family bonus, DK refinery-speed family bonus [C29][C30][C31]; Refinery Throttle cycles summed over every Squire-line character [C25]; King of the Remembered, Bitty Litty, 1000 Hours Played and The Family Guy read the account's best holder [C34][C33][C35].
- **Inputs -> outputs:** Input: talent points (per tab formula [C10]), plus books to raise max level [C9], plus added levels [C8]. Output: damage, HP, mining efficiency, construction speed/EXP, refinery cycles and rank-scaled damage, printer sampling, Gaming EXP/bits, and family bonuses.
- **Goals and Milestones:**

- **Feeds / fed by:** Fed by Refinery rank (Precision Power), Redox Salt stock (Redox Rates), Copper Ore stock (Copper Collector), Gaming level (Gamer Strength), orb kills (King of the Remembered), Warriors Rule bubble (Blocky Bottles) [C22][C26][C18][C33][C34][C12]. Feeds Refinery (cycles, DK speed), Construction (build rate, EXP), 3D Printer (Super Samples, King), Mining, statues (Shieldiest), Gaming [C25][C31][C26][C27][C28][C23][C33].
- **Per-session actions:** cast Refinery Throttle on each Squire-line character every 20 h (cooldown 72,000 s); check accuracy against the AFK target's Defence for Precision Power; spend unspent points on the active preset; for DKs keep Orb of Remembrance cast during active farming. Idle state: Throttle off cooldown, unspent points, Precision Power below its accuracy gate [C25][C22][C11].
- **AFK Setup implications:** Warrior and Squire skilling preset sits on Mining (Big Pick on the bar), Construction and Refinery; fighting preset uses Idle Brawling. DK sits on Gaming. The AFK target must keep 100% hit chance and, for Squire, 2.25x Defence [C18][C22][C17][C44].
- **Min-max by stage:** see the dedicated section below.
- **Common mistakes:** (1) Precision Power points while accuracy is below 2.25x Defence: they add nothing [C22]. (2) Thinking WIS is useless: it is the Warrior line's accuracy stat [C5][C47]. (3) Reading Refinery Throttle at current level when only max level counts in Toolbox [C25]. (4) Putting points into Blocky Bottles without a Warriors Rule bubble level [C24]. (5) Resetting only one preset: potions work on the active preset only [C37]. (6) Two Squires without a plan; one token swaps a Squire to Barbarian [C37][C48].
- **Save and Toolbox fields:** `characters[].talents` (indexed list per tab, `talents[2]` = Squire tab) and `flatTalents` (level incl. added levels, `baseLevel`, `maxLevel`), `characters[].addedLevels`, `characters[].familyBonuses[className]`, `characters[].cooldowns[130]`, `account.refinery.totalLevels`, `account.accountOptions[138]` (orb kills), `characters[].skillsInfo.gaming.level`, `account.talentPoints` (CYTalentPoints) and ADR 0010's `characters[].talents.active.talentPoints`. Raw keys: `SL_` SkillLevels, `SLpre_` presets, `SM_` SkillLevelsMAX, `AtkCD_` cooldowns [C9][C10][C25][C34].
- **Sources:** see the register (tiers 1 and 3). ActiveOn state today [C51].

## Min-max by stage

Evidence is tier 3 (community builds via earlier research, dated) on top of tier 1 data. Confidence L-M; no new community pass was possible this session.

- **W1 early (Warrior, Lv 10-30):** one preset, fighting. Sharpened Axe and Gilded Sword first, then Meat Shank 20, Power Strike 20, Whirl 20, Double Strike 10, Firmly Grasp It 1, Carry A Big Stick 50 for AFK [C46]. Stat points go to Fist of Rage; Book of the Wise only until 100% hit chance [C47][C17]. Confidence M (builds are v1.23, Stale, but match the 2026 core).
- **W1 mid/late:** second preset for mining: Brute Efficiency 80, Idle Skilling 50, Big Pick 50 on the attack bar, Copper Collector 15, Motherlode Miner 15, Firmly Grasp It 1 [C46][C18].
- **W2 early (Squire at Speccius):** take Squire if the Meta Path wants Refinery/Construction; a second Squire is allowed and stacks Throttle [C48]. Fighting preset: tab 1 Gilded Sword -> Sharpened Axe -> Idle Brawling 50 -> Fist of Rage -> Lucky Clover; tab 2 Power Strike = Whirl 54, Double Strike 45, Firmly 1, Big Stick, Strength In Numbers, Health Overdrive, Garb = Absolute Unit; tab 3 Precision Power -> STR Summore -> Back To Basics, 1 point each in Shockwave Slash, Daggerang, Bricky Skin, Blocky Bottles [C43].
- **W2 late:** skilling preset: tab 1 Brute Efficiency -> Idle Skilling -> Fist of Rage -> Health Booster -> Lucky Clover; tab 2 Big Pick -> Copper Collector -> Tool Proficiency -> Motherlode Miner -> Health Overdrive -> Absolute Unit -> Haungry For Gold [C44].
- **W3 early-mid:** tab 3 Refinery Throttle -> Redox Rates -> Sharper Saws -> Shieldiest Statues -> STR Summore [C44]. Raise Throttle in multiples of 8 (3+floor(L/8)); Post Office Mining or Construction box after the first 200 Civil War box [C25][C49]. Confidence M.
- **W3 late:** Precision Power only when accuracy >= 2.25x Defence of the AFK target, then it scales with total Refinery rank; Super Samples as a printer lever (Toolbox does not count it, Q3) [C22][C28][C50]. Late AFK combat build: Health Booster/Sharpened Axe/Gilded Sword 160, Fist of Rage 260, Meat Shank 260, Idle Brawling 160, Power Strike = Whirl 55, Double Strike 41, Precision Power 160, Refinery Throttle 160 [C45]. Confidence L-M.
- **W4 (Divine Knight):** promotion via Eliteus; spend tab 4 on Gaming talents (Gamer Strength, 1000 Hours Played, Bitty Litty, Undying Passion), King of the Remembered, The Family Guy (buffs DK refinery family) and Symbols of Beyond; DK sits on Gaming. Orb of Remembrance for active farming [C32][C33][C34][C31][C35]. No dated community DK build was found (tier 3 earlier research lists only a Stale sheet for statue and rare-bag farming). Confidence L.
- **Per class:** Squire vs Barbarian: Squire = Refinery/Construction/Mining, Barbarian = fishing/ZOW; wrong double-up fixed by a Sub Class Swap Token [C37].
