## Mage line (Mage, Shaman, Bubonic Conjuror; Wizard and Arcane Cultist noted for contrast)

- **What it is:** the WIS caster line. Mage is the first class, Shaman the alchemy subclass, Bubonic Conjuror the laboratory elite class [C1][C2]. Wizard (worship/divinity branch) is the sibling subclass; Arcane Cultist is the master class on top of Bubonic Conjuror; Elemental Sorcerer in turn promotes on to Spiritual Monk (classPromotions.json `promotesTo`), which the original line omitted [C1, H].
- **Unlocks:** Mage: Promotheus (Bean map, after Frogs; location wiki-only, M), quest 1 needs class Lv 10, quest 2 selects the class [C3]. Shaman: Speccius (Yum-Yum Desert), bring 100 Kebab Sticks and 50 Crabby Cakey, then select the subclass (the 10,000 coins is the quest reward, not a cost) [C4, H]; NPC location is wiki-only (M). Bubonic Conjuror: Eliteus (outskirts of Fallstar Isle, W4), defeat the 10 Greenie Genies, then select the elite class (Eliteus quests reward 750,000,000 and 150,964,823 coins, not costs; no level gate; NPC location wiki-only, M) [C5]. Class level gates for the tabs are in the Mechanics catalogue (tab 2 from Lv 7, tab 3 from Lv 30, tab 4 from Lv 90) [C6].
- **Scope:** per-character (talents, tabs, presets, vials-driven damage), with account-wide spillover: the family bonuses (Mage +WIS, Shaman golden food, Bubonic all stats), Green/Purple Tube applying to other characters in the Lab, Bubble Breakthrough's best-on-account lookup, and class bubbles (Smart Boi Talent, Mage is Best) [C20][C33][C34][C36][C31][C9][C52].
- **Inputs -> outputs:** in: class levels, skill levels, talent-point sources (stamps, vials, bubble, achievements), vials upgraded to green, Lab position, WIS. Out: damage (Overclocked Energy, Knowledge is Power, Virile Vials, Paperwork-style passives), AFK fighting rate (Idle Casting), choppin/alchemy efficiency and speed, Lab EXP and line width, multikill [C12][C13][C16][C26][C29][C30][C31][C36][C38].
- **Goals and Milestones:**

- **Feeds / fed by:** fed by Alchemy bubbles and vials (Virile Vials, Fantasia Flasks, Smart Boi Talent, Mage is Best), Stamps (Talent I-IV), the Talent Book Library, Obols (Occult Obols), Statues (Stupendous Statues). Feeds Alchemy (Cranium Cooking, Busy Brewin', Bubble Breakthrough, Sharing Some Smarts), Choppin (Log On Logs, Leaf Thief, Deforesting All Doubt), Lab (Upload Squared, tubes), Death Note and portals (Memorial Skulls), golden food (family), Worship (Wizard side) [C18][C29][C30][C31][C37][C38][C33].
- **Per-session actions:** spend new tab points per preset; put the right attacks on the bar; park a Bubonic Conjuror at the Lab's right end; check green vial count and Mage bubble level; use Cranium Cooking only when fighting somewhere that matters to alchemy (see mistakes).
- **AFK Setup implications:** fighting preset: Idle Casting, Overclocked Energy, the attacks on the bar. Alchemy skilling preset: Shaman tab 3 Bubble Breakthrough, Busy Brewin', Sharing Some Smarts and Smart Efficiency / Active AFK'er [C48]. Cranium Cooking belongs on the fighting bar of an AFK-fighting Shaman, not the skilling bar [C29][C48].
- **Min-max by stage:** see the dedicated section below.
- **Common mistakes:** (1) Fantasia/Fuscia Flasks points before the Mage bubble passes 100; (2) the Idle Casting talent at 0 on a fighting preset; (3) attack talents levelled but off the bar; (4) a Bubonic Conjuror placed left of the characters it should buff in the Lab (Green Tube only reaches characters to its left) [C36]; (5) spending Cranium Cooking's one-shot (about 22 h cooldown) in a map with nothing to kill [C29]; (6) the wiki says tab 4 counts "class levels above 50", the code says above 89 [C6].
- **Save and Toolbox fields:** see the catalogue; talents live in `characters[].talents` / `flatTalents` (`talentId`, `baseLevel`, `level`, `maxLevel`), `characters[].talentPreset`, `characters[].flatStarTalents`, `account.talentPoints`, `account.alchemy.vials`, `account.lab.playersCords`, `characters[].stats.wisdom`.

## Mechanics catalogue

Format: **name** - rule/formula - cap - unlock/cost - field. Talent growth: `decay` = x1*L/(L+x2); `add` with x2=0 = x1*L, but with x2!=0 it is non-linear: (((x1+x2)/x2+0.5*(L-1))/(x1/x2))*L*x1 (Smart Efficiency, Bubble Breakthrough y, Mana Overdrive use this; other variants addLower, decayLower, reduce also exist) [C11, H]; `bigBase` = x1+x2*L; `intervalAdd` = x1+floor(L/x2); `decayMulti` = 1+x1*L/(L+x2) [C11]. Default max talent level 100 is **unverified in the cited sources** (it comes from the save's `maxLevel`; confidence L) (raised by books, bubbles, Wis Wumbo, which is confirmed) [C10]. ### Class structure and points
1. **Class tree.** Beginner(1) -> Mage(31) -> Wizard(32) / Shaman(33) -> Elemental Sorcerer(34) / Bubonic Conjuror(36) -> Arcane Cultist(40). Tabs: Savvy_Basics, Mage, Shaman, Bubonic_Conjuror, Arcane_Cultist [C1]. Field: `characters[].class` (Toolbox string, e.g. `Bubonic_Conjuror`; note the game's spelling is Conjuror and ActiveOn's sprite key is `class-bubonic-conjurer`). TBD/classPromotions.json.
2. **Main stat WIS, accuracy AGI.** `mainStatMap` maps Mage, Shaman, Bubonic_Conjuror, Arcane_Cultist, Wizard, Elemental_Sorcerer to wisdom [C2]. Field: `characters[].stats.wisdom`.
3. **Tab points.** Tab 1 3(L-1) + skill terms; tab 2 3(L-6) + half skills; tab 3 3(L-29); tab 4 3*min(L-89,100) + max(0,2(L-189)); tab 5 3(L-249); star L-1 [C6]. Skill terms: tab 1 = sum of skills 1-9 minus 3; tabs 2-3 = sum of floor(skill/2); tab 4/5 floor(skill/5) [C7]. ActiveOn port: AS/talent-points-formula.ts.
4. **Extra tab points for the Mage line.** Earlier Education (talent 494, Shaman tab 3): +1 tab-2 point per level, so a maxed one is +100 [C8]. Smart Boi Talent (bubble, high-iq cauldron b13): "Talent Points for each tab" (bigBase 5,1) for mage-class ids (>=31) [C9] (formula shape from TBD/cauldrons.json, M). Vials Sippy Soul (tab 2), Bunny Brew (tab 3), Ash Agua (tab 4) and stamps Talent I-IV add to the matching tab [C9]. Field: `account.talentPoints` (CYTalentPoints, 6 entries), `flatTalents[494]`.
5. **Max level.** Default 100. Book of the Wise is raised by Wis Wumbo (488, Wizard and Shaman tab 3, +1 max level per level) and Utmost Intellect (533, Bubonic tab 4: +1 max level per level) [C10][C40]. Overclocked Energy (446) and Farsight (447) are raised by the Mage cauldron bubble through Fuscia Flasks (474, Wizard) / Fantasia Flasks (489, Shaman): "each level of Mage is Best raises max Lv, up to +L" (data text only, no Toolbox consumer; Fuscia Flasks side from prior research, confidence M/L) [C28]. 446, 447 and Book of the Wise (12) are excluded from Talent Book Library raises [C55]. Field: `flatTalents[].maxLevel` (`SkillLevelsMAX`).
6. **Added levels.** Every talent above Lv 1 gets +levels from Symbols of Beyond ~P (Bubonic, +1+floor(L/20)), family (Elemental Sorcerer), god Arctis, achievement, companion, Equinox, Grimoire, Kattlekruk set, Tesseract (capped 5), ninja mastery, superbit [C43][C54]. Field: `flatTalents[].level` vs `baseLevel`.
7. **Attack cooldown divisor.** Cooldown = ceil(cd / (1 + (WOAH,_THAT_WAS_FAST! + Magician Starterpack box)/100)) [C56]. Field: `flatTalents[].cooldown`.
8. **Attack bar.** Attacks add to AFK fighting only on the bar; each multiplies by D*(1+L/(L+100)), kill-rate factor clamped [1, 2.2] (the per-talent factor is confirmed at damage.ts L1295; the clamp is at about L1275 on K, confidence M) [C57]. Field: `characters[].talentsLoadout`.

9. **Health Booster (0)** add 1,0.15; **Mana Booster (1)** add 1,0.1: max HP/MP. **Star Player (8)**: add 1,0, +1 Star Talent Point per level (consumed by calcTotalStarTalent, talents.ts L567; star talents such as Stonks! unlock later) [H]. **Bucklered Up (9)** decay 40,60: total defence %. [talents.json]
10. **Stat talents:** Fist of Rage (10) STR, Quickness Boots (11) AGI, Book of the Wise (12) WIS, Lucky Clover (13) LUK: +x1*L base stat (add 1,0). Put points in Quickness Boots only until hit chance is 100% for a Mage line; Book of the Wise adds to the main stat and Choppin/alchemy efficiency [C2].
11. **Sharpened Axe (5)** 0.4/lv base weapon power; **Gilded Sword (6)** 1.5%/lv damage, both uncapped (L50 = 75%) [C12].
12. **Smart Efficiency (445)** add 1,0.02: +% efficiency of all specialized skills (L50 75.5%, L100 201%). **Active AFK'er (449)** decay 20,40: AFK specialized-skill gain (L50 11.1%, L100 14.3%). **Idle Casting (448)** decay 20,50: AFK fighting gain (L50 10%, L100 13.3%, L150 15%) [C12].
13. **Overclocked Energy (446)** decay 150,80: % damage per power of 10 Max MP (L50 57.7%, L100 83.3%); read in `getDamageFromHpMp` (damage.ts:827) [C13]. **Farsight (447)** data: x curve decay 17,45 and y curve decay 33,45 (L100 11.7% / 22.8%). **Disputed:** data text implies chance uses x and damage uses y, but both Toolbox call sites (damage.ts getCritDamage L1178 and getCritChance L1215) pass yBonus=true, so Toolbox uses only the 33,45 curve for both. Code vs data text disagree; confidence M, code wins per ADR 0004 [C61].

### Shaman (tab 3)
18. **Crazy Concoctions (480)** potency 17+3L %, mp 15, cast 0.95 s, cd 25 s. Data text: "throws 1 of 4 potions"; wiki: "throws 3 potions", four colours (red AoE damage, green heal, purple mark, yellow debuff for EXP) plus a black invincibility potion. Sources disagree, confidence L on count [C21].
19. **Auspicious Aura (481)** dmg 10+0.6L %, aura count 1+floor(L/35), mp 40, cd 40 s [C22]. **Sizzling Skull (482)** dmg 125+L %, bounces to 3+floor(L/20) monsters, mp 20, cd 18 s [C23].
20. **Tenteyecle (483)** decay 100,100: chance on kill to cut attack cooldowns by 2+floor(L/200) s (L100 50%) [C24]. **Instant Invincibility (484)** decay 5,100: extra invincibility seconds after being hit (L100 2.5 s) [C25].
21. **Virile Vials (485)** decay 12,100: % damage per alchemy vial at level > 3 (green or better): L50 4%, L100 6%, L150 7.2% each; damage.ts:713-716 counts `vials` with `level > 3` [C26].
22. **Occult Obols (486)** decay 135,80: obols give +% more WIS (L100 75%); **Stupendous Statues (487)** decay 100,50: Exp, Lumberbob and Cauldron statues give +% more (L100 66.7%). Occult Obols and Wis Wumbo share ids with the Wizard tab (Wizard's own third talent is Staring Statues) [C27].
23. **Wis Wumbo (488)** +1 Book of the Wise max level per level; **Fantasia Flasks (489)** raises Farsight max by the Mage is Best bubble level; **Earlier Education (494)** +1 tab-2 point per level [C10][C28][C8].
24. **Cranium Cooking (490)** mp 100, cast 0.95 s, cooldown 80,000 s (about 22 h 13 min), lasts 10+floor(L/2) s, each kill in that window gives 40L/(L+100) s of instant alchemy progress, credited when the window ends; resets if you change map (wiki only, unverified in code, L). L100: 20 s per kill for 60 s. Toolbox lists id 490 as a "relevant talent" (talents.ts:546) but computes no value [C29].
25. **Busy Brewin' (491)** add 1,0: +1% brew speed per level for this character; Toolbox does consume it: world-2/alchemy.ts L722-728 characterSpeed = p2wSpeed*alchemyLevel*wisdomMulti*(1+(busyBrewin+brokenTime+starSign)/100) [C30, H]; **Bubble Breakthrough (492)** +L % Alchemy EXP (character.ts:822) and a second effect (y: non-linear add 1,0.02, L100 about 201%) giving odds multiplier 1 + y/100 (about 3.01x at L100) on new-bubble odds, using the best Bubble Breakthrough on the account (world-2/alchemy.ts getBestBubbleBreakthrough helper and the odds application near L672; the EXP part is the brewing character's own talent) [H]; **Sharing Some Smarts (493)** +L % more Alch EXP per the talent description; Toolbox applies it only to the owner's own Alchemy EXP (character.ts:823) and models no same-cauldron sharing, so the sharing effect is unverified (L) [C30][C31][C32].
26. **Family bonus (Shaman):** golden foods give 1 + 0.4*n/(100+n) times the bonus, n = class level - 29 (decayMulti 0.4,100 from Lv 29); used in `getGoldenFoodMulti` (misc.ts:925) [C33].
