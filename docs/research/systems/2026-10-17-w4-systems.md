## Answer

- **Lab, Cooking and Breeding are one loop.** Breeding Territories make Spices. Spices discover meals and pay for kitchen upgrades. Chips and jewels cost meals plus Spices. Meals, shiny Mobs and the Mob Arena raise Lab Line Width. ActiveOn should rank them together, not as three separate Systems.
- **Lab:** a character is "in the lab" only while its AFK target is Laboratory. The 14 W4 Mainframe nodes multiply W1–W3 systems (stamps ×2, vials ×2, refinery 3× faster, kills ×2 for portals and the Death Note). Chips are per-character, with 7 slots at Lab Lv 5/10/15/25/35/50/75. Jewels are account-wide. Both are sold on a weekly rotation that is server-global and therefore predictable.
- **Cooking:** meals are permanent account-wide bonuses, and speed is everything. Cooking skill level does not change meal speed. The levers are kitchen Speed upgrades, more kitchens (coins, from 8.2M up), and Lv 11 on every meal plus the Diamond Chef bubble. The base meal cap is 30 in W4. The current max is 170 (v1.23 added +10), while the wiki still says 160.
- **Breeding:** its level is account-wide and it never uses an AFK slot. The W4 Mob row opens after the Massive Troll kill. Buy the five New Mob Chance upgrades at level 1 as they unlock. Push the Mob Arena to wave 35 (−50% kitchen upgrade cost) and wave 100 (3rd large bubble). Spice claims are capped at 100 a day.
- **Since v1.05 (2026-02-20) breeding creatures are called "Mobs"** and Companions are "Pets". Toolbox and older guides still say "pets", so the Knowledge Base needs that alias. Combat Mobs stopped foraging in v2.41 (2025-07-06), so older Territory advice is wrong.
- **The W4 boss is the Massive Troll, not Chizoar.** Chizoar is the W3 boss, and his drop opens W4. The Troll has no daily limit, costs 1/5/60 keys for Normal/Chaotic/Blitzkrieg, and drops the item that opens W5. The W4 miniboss is the Mutated Mush.
- **The elite class is not a choice.** Each subclass has exactly one elite. There is no level gate: the requirements are being a subclass, reaching map 154 (Outskirts of Fallstar Isle) and killing 10 Greenie Gang genies. Any subclass in W4 without its elite is a Verdict-worthy gap.
- **The Rift has 65 levels, a bonus every 5 levels, and class gates.** Several levels need a Divine Knight, an Elemental Sorcerer or a Siege Breaker. Level 60 needs 11 characters. Skill Mastery (Rift 15) is the biggest early prize and rewards skill levels spread across many characters.
- **Sigils charge only while the Lab "Sigils of Olden Alchemy" node is lit.** They charge at 1 EXP/h per assigned character, with only the first 10 characters counting. A W4 account reaches only the Unlock and Boost tiers. Level-ups seem to apply only when the Sigils page is open, so a session should open it (unverified).
- **The Tome pays from account level 350, before W4.** A W3 account at level 379 already gets about +16% damage. Blue pages (W4 shop) and Red pages (W5 shop) add Skill Efficiency and Drop Rate.

## Gaps for later decision tickets

- Reddit (Arctic Shift) was rate-limited for all four researchers. The per-class chip priorities and the sigil priority order are therefore derived from the effects (marked O/M) and still need a dated community check.
- Both fixture saves are pre-W4: Lab, Cooking and Breeding are all at level 0. The first post-W4 save should check: the daily spice-claim cap of 100 (Toolbox constant, not read in game code), sigil level-up on page open, Tome points on a pre-W4 save, and whether the cap is 9 or 10 breedability hearts (Toolbox says 9, the wiki shows 10).
