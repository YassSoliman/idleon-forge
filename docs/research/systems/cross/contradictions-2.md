## Mechanics catalogue

- **Unending Energy (prayer index 2):** Soul1 (Forest) prayer, unlock Goblin Gorefest wave 51, costMulti 600, max level 50; bonus +25% Class and Skill EXP (x1 = 25), curse text "Max AFK time is now 10 hours". Bonus `x1 + x1*(L-1)/10`. EXP read at `P/character.ts#L1668` (skill) and `#L2162` (class); no cap read anywhere in `calculateAfkTime` (`P/misc.ts#L681-L683`, time away = PlayerAwayTime x 1000 ms). Fields: `account.prayers[2].level`, `characters[].activePrayers[]`. [C1][C2][C3]
- **Toolbox AFK alert:** `worshipAlerts` flags Unending Energy equipped and AFK > 10 h (`utility/dashboard/characters.js#L68-L75`); it is an advisory built on the curse text, not proof of enforcement. **Disputed [C3]:** the earlier claim that the wiki v1.50 note states the 10 h behaviour was refuted by the source lens (the note covers the prayer level cap and cost adjustments; the 10 h wording appears only as the curse text). Data lens could not verify either way. Confidence L for the wiki attribution.
- **Insider Trading (bribes[0]):** price 750 coins, value 8, StampCostPct; stamp coin cost = `baseCoin * (1 - 8/100)` when `bribes[0].done`; field `account.bribes[0].done`; code `P/world-1/stamps.ts#L237-L243`. Description text says 5%. [C4][C5]
- **Pet Arena (W4 Breeding):** thresholds wave 20 = "1.5X MONSTER CASH" (+0.5) and wave 175 = "2X MONSTER CASH" (+1) in coin factor 18 `1 + (0.5*arena20 + friend5 + 1*arena175 + statue19/100)`; best wave in `account.accountOptions[89]`. Pet Arena Crucible bonus 10 multiplies Bubba meat and bonus 15 feeds class EXP (see the Crucible entry below for the data-confirmed list). [C6][C7][C15]
- **Speedna (Bowman talent 290):** `SPEEDNA * floor(min(moveSpeed/100 - 1, 10) / 0.15)` added to the per-X damage group (`P/damage.ts#L729-L734`); cap at speed bonus 10 (+1000%). Step table (computed with JS floats): 100-115% = 0, 116-129% = 1, 130-144% = 2, 145-159% = 3, 160% = 4, 205% = 6. Input `playerInfo.movementSpeed`. [C8][C9][C10]

- **Movement-speed sources feeding Speedna:** `getPlayerSpeedBonus` (`P/character.ts#L3192-L3222`) sums food boosts, talent FEATHERWEIGHT, the Movement_Speed stamp, active STRAFE buff, statue, star sign, gear, card, FEATHER_FLIGHT, then adds an agility term (`(agi+1)^0.4 - 1)/40` below 1000 agility, else `(agi-1000)/(agi+2500)*0.5 + 0.371`) divided by 2.2. Above 200% it is floored to 0.01; between 175% and 200% it adds TIPTOE_QUICKNESS and caps at 200%; at or below 175% it adds salt lick 7, grounded motherboard chip, tiptoe and the TUFT_OF_HAIR sigil with a 175% cap. So in the sub-175% branch the result is a two-decimal floor, which matters for exact step boundaries. Confidence H (code), M for in-game equivalence. [C17]
