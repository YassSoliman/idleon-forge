## Answer

- **Construction:** building upgrades cost salts only (materials were removed in v1.05, 2026-02-20, so the wiki's material fields are stale). Construction XP and build rate come only from characters slotted on the Cog Board or Cog Shelf, whatever they do while AFK. Two Squire or Divine Knight builders is the community meta (opinion), so Joseph's two Squires fit it.
- **Refinery:** Auto Refine never ranks a salt up. Only a manual Refine on a full bar does. Salt costs grow faster than output (rank^1.5 against rank^1.3), so over-ranking a salt starves the one below it. Toolbox's `getSaltsBalance` gives a max safe rank per salt.
- **3D Printer and Salt Lick:** the Printer level is the number of characters that can print, up to 11 (the wiki's 10 is stale), and samples cap at 90%. The Salt Lick has 11 upgrades, all paid in salt since v1.17. Cheap first maxes are Printer Sample Size, obol slots, talent-book level and multikill.
- **Atom Collider:** built in W3 (it needs Nullo, the 6th salt) but pays off mostly from W4. On a W3 account only Hydrogen (cheaper stamp max levels) matters.
- **Worship:** each character banks its own charge. The skull sets max charge (50 with no skull, up to 6,000). The Next Action is to spend charge before it caps, on the totem with the best EXP per charge. Push waves only up to the Prayer-unlock waves.
- **Trapping:** traps run in real time and never use up the character's AFK activity. The AFK Setup rule is to fill every slot with a trap that ends at or after the Expected Return. Collection is stuck at its floors without a Hunter's quest progress.
- **Death Note:** multikill is off until the Death Note building reaches level 1. Points per mob step up at 25k, 100k, 250k, 500k, 1M, 5M, 100M and 1B kills. 1M kills on all 40 W1–W3 mobs is the big pre-W4 Goal, since it gates the Voidwalker elite class.
- **Shrines and Prayers:** shrines level only from AFK time claimed on their own map, and moving one resets its progress. Prayer slots depend only on the best Wizard-line class level (1 slot at Lv 1, up to 8 at Lv 250), not Shaman. Curses scale with blessings. Unending Energy caps AFK at 10 h.
- **Equinox, boss and quests:** Equinox needs the Equinox Mirror (Bloodbone drop), and a full bar wastes fill. The W3 boss is Chizoar (Efaunt is W2). Each Chizoar kill drops a Galaxial Amethyst, needed for the W4 portal. Must-do quests are Hoggindaz 1–5, Bellows 1–3 on every character (+1 Chizoar key a day each), Snouts and Crystalswine, and Carpenter Cardinal.
- **For later tickets:** Toolbox's trap `exp` column is mislabelled for 3 trap sets, `equinox.unlocked` is true even on a W2 save, and Toolbox computes neither shrine time-to-next-level nor the prayer slot count. Joseph's W3 save shows a lot of unspent or idle value: buildings built but unpaid, an Explosive Salt switched off, no Worship skulls, 0 Death Note building, an unspent full Equinox bar, no prayers equipped and shrines in W1 town. Priority orders that use Reddit opinion need a human pass (Arctic Shift was rate-limited).
