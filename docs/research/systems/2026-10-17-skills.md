# Skills System Dossiers: index and answer (2026-10-17)

Ticket: [#25](https://github.com/YassSoliman/idleon-forge/issues/25), part of map [#1](https://github.com/YassSoliman/idleon-forge/issues/1). It covers every skill reachable in W1–W4, plus a Divinity outline: what to AFK at each level band, what each AFK target produces and which Systems consume it, tools per band, and which classes suit which skill. The format is [SYSTEM-DOSSIER.md](../SYSTEM-DOSSIER.md), and the terms are from [GLOSSARY.md](../../../GLOSSARY.md). Verified on game v1.23 "Lumi Menagerie" (2026-10-16/17) against Toolbox @ 6827d3f2 (gameVer 2.3.540) and the game client `N.js` (Last-Modified 2026-10-17).

## Answer

- **One AFK target, plus several parallel slots.** A character has exactly one AFK target. Only 9 AFK types exist (Fighting, Mining, Choppin, Fishing, Catching, Cooking, Laboratory, Divinity, Spelunking), per Toolbox `monsters.json` `AFKtype`. Trapping (placed traps), Construction (cog-board seat), Worship (charge refill) and Alchemy (cauldron assignment) run **in parallel** with it, and Breeding is session-only (hatching). A good AFK Setup fills every parallel slot for every character, not just the AFK target.
- **Classes follow game data.** Mining: Warrior line. Smithing: Archer line. Choppin: Mage. Fishing: Barbarian. Catching: **Bowman, not Hunter**. Trapping: Hunter. Construction: Squire. Worship: Wizard. Alchemy: Shaman. Cooking: Blood Berserker. Breeding: Beast Master. Lab: Bubonic Conjuror. Divinity: Elemental Sorcerer. Each matches the skill talents in `talents.json`. Main stats: STR for Mining, Fishing and Cooking; AGI for Catching and Trapping; WIS for Choppin, Worship, Alchemy and Lab.
- **AFK targets by band:**
  - **Mining:** doors are gated by Mining Lv 10/25/40/60. For EXP, the order is Copper → Iron → Gold → Plat → Void (from ~100K efficiency), skipping Dementia, then Starfire (~3M) → Dreadlo (~30M).
  - **Choppin:** follow efficiency order, not world order. Potty and Veiny (W1 maps) need more efficiency than W2 Tropilogs.
  - **Fishing:** efficiency picks the depth (small fish 50/200/400, medium 10K/50K/200K).
  - **Every gathering skill:** the next tool costs the output of the tier the current tool reaches, so the tool chain is the plan.
- **Outputs feed many Systems.** Ores, logs, fish and bugs feed stamps, Anvil and Forge (bars), bubbles and vials, refinery salts, smithing recipes, quests, Post Office orders and, in W4, Lab chips (10K–10M of one resource each). Critters feed vials (e.g. Goosey Glug: +base critters), trap and pouch recipes, and salts (Scorpie → Dioxide, Mousey → Purple). Souls feed prayers, skulls, vials (Sippy Soul: tab-2 talent points) and salts. Construction never takes raw ore, logs or fish; it reaches them only through salts.
- **Smithing EXP comes mostly from Anvil production, and Thread gives the most EXP per hour at every level.** Craft material pouches instead of buying capacity.
- **W3 skills are free value most casual accounts leave idle.** A W3 test account had **0 traps** placed, **2 of 6** characters on the cog board, **4 of 6** at full worship charge with no skull, and every character at Trapping Lv 1. Toolbox already ships the alerts: `missingTraps`, `trapsOverdue`, `chargeOverdue` (≥ 95%), `alchemy.noActivity`, and `hasAvailableToolUpgrade`, which covers every tool slot.
- **Trap duration should match the Expected Return.** The per-hour rate falls as traps get longer: Royal 20 min gives 6× quantity per hour, Royal 40 h gives 1.75×. Natural 8 h is the best EXP trap. Trap timers stop on the character-select screen. Worship souls scale about as (highest wave)^2.6 and are 0 below a totem's minimum efficiency.
- **W4:**
  - **Lab:** the default AFK target for every non-pushing character. Its Leverage is account-wide nodes (2× stamps, 2× vials, 3× refinery, 2× kills), and pulling one character out can break the chain for everyone behind it.
  - **Cooking:** one character is enough (Blood Berserker). Kitchen speed is account-wide, and the kitchen AFK character only gains Cooking EXP and ladles.
  - **Breeding:** hatch eggs each session. Equip DNA Splicers on fighters. Arena wave 100 gives the 3rd bubble slot.
- **Divinity (W5) outline:** link everyone to Snehebatu (+30% AFK gains). Arctis keeps a character "in the lab" while it does other things, so Lab levels and chain layout carry over into W5.
- **Patch and Stale status:**
  - v1.23 changes none of these skills for W1–W4. It adds a W7-only auto-ladle unlock, caps No Meal Left Behind at Lv 150, and adds the Crucible arena.
  - Every wiki page used predates v1.23. Community sheets are pre-Royal Guardian, so they are Stale for roster advice.
  - Reddit was rate-limited and is not used.
