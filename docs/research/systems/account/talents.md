## Talents (including star talents, presets and resets)

- **What it is:** each character's skill tree: one tab per class in its line (up to five), plus a separate Star tab, two swappable Presets each with its own allocation and Attack bar, and a set of reset items. [C1 · D/classPromotions.json (talentTabs, promotesTo, released); P/classDefinitions.ts#L22-L33] Points are the main lever on damage, AFK gains and skill efficiency.

### M1. Class tabs and talent pages

- **Rule.** Talent tabs per class line are fixed by the game tree: Beginner = [Beginner]; Journeyman = + Journeyman; Maestro = + Maestro; Voidwalker = + Voidwalker (4 tabs); Warrior line = Rage_Basics, Warrior, then Barbarian or Squire, then Blood_Berserker or Divine_Knight, then Death_Bringer or Royal_Guardian; Archer line = Calm_Basics, Archer, then Bowman or Hunter, then Siege_Breaker (from Bowman) or Beast_Master (from Hunter), then Wind_Walker; Mage line = Savvy_Basics, Mage, then Wizard or Shaman, then Elemental_Sorcerer or Bubonic_Conjuror, then Arcane_Cultist. ### M5. Growth curves (value of a level)

- **Rule.** Talent values come from growth(funcX, level, x1, x2): add (x2=0: x1*L; x2!=0: ((x1+x2)/x2 + 0.5(L-1))/(x1/x2)*L*x1), decay x1*L/(L+x2), decayMulti 1+x1*L/(L+x2), bigBase x1+x2*L, intervalAdd x1+floor(L/x2), reduce x1-x2*L, special1 100-L*x1/(L+x2), plus the addLower/decayLower/addDECAY variants; an unknown function returns 0. [C12] Zero-level behaviour: getTalentBonus answers 0 for a talent at level <= 0 (the game answers 0 for an unlearned talent), not growth() at level 0, which would be x1 for bigBase, intervalAdd, reduce and decayMulti. [C49 · P/talents.ts#L33-L38]
- **Breakpoint talents** pay only at multiples. Breakpoint (intervalAdd) talents: Apocalypse ZOW (id 110, x1 2, step 33), Refinery Throttle (130, 3, step 8), Acme Anvil (281, 1, step 15), Bear Trap (301, 2, step 30), Log On Logs (460, 2, step 10); they pay only at multiples of the step. [C50 · D/talents.json (110, 130, 281, 301, 460); U/wiki/growth.mjs#L58-L60]
- **Linear talents never saturate** (Gilded Sword, Sharpened Axe, Brute/Elusive/Smart Efficiency, the stat talents); `decay` talents give half their cap at `L = x2`. Reference values: Gilded Sword (add 1.5) +1.5% damage per level (150% at 100); Sharpened Axe (add 0.4) +0.4 base weapon power per level (40 at 100); Idle fighting talents (decay 20/50) 10% at Lv 50, 13.3% at 100, 15.2% at 160, cap 20%; Idle Skilling (decay 20/40) 11.1% at 50, 14.3% at 100; Sleepin' On The Job (decay 21/50) 10.5% at 50, 14% at 100; Brute/Elusive/Smart Efficiency (add 1/0.02) 201% at 100. [C13]
- **Fields.** TB `getTalentBonus(flatTalents, 'NAME', yBonus)`; every talent exposes `funcX/x1/x2` and `funcY/y1/y2`.

### M6. Max levels

## Common mistakes (and detection)

- **Second preset never set up, or both presets the same role.** Detect `characters[].talents.other` all zero or same-role classification. [C61]
- **AFK on the wrong preset.** Detect `selectedTalentPreset` role vs `afkType`. [C23]
- **Idle fighting talent at 0 on a fighting preset.** Detect `flatTalents[88|268|448].baseLevel == 0` with `afkType == FIGHTING`; the talent adds up to +10% at 50 points. [C13] [C21]
- **Attacks levelled but off the bar, or too few attacks on the bar.** Detect `talentsLoadout`. [C26]
- **Points left unspent on one preset after a reset or new levels.** Detect `talentPoints[tab].unspent > 0` per preset. [C32] [C22]
- **Points in bottle/vessel/flask talents while the class bubble is at or under 100, or in bubble-capped talents above the cap.** [C14]
- **Accuracy-stat talent levelled past 100% hit chance.** [C67] [C104]
- **Books checked out for talents not yet at their current max.** [C54]
- **Reset potion used on the wrong preset, or gem potions bought while free ones sit unused.** [C75] [C76]
- **Subclass token used without a plan for both presets (it wipes both presets and both bars).** [C78]
- **Star points spent on one preset only.** [C9]
- **Trapping or skill actions done on the damage preset (trap stats lock at placement).** [C28]
- **Treating buffed levels as bought levels** (added levels inflate `level` but not points). [C56]
