## Monster maps, AFK setups and portals

- **What it is:** Every character is parked on an AFK Target (a monster on a map, or a skill node). While off-screen it earns kills, drops, coins and EXP at an AFK gain rate times a kills-per-hour model, and every kill counts toward that map's portal doors, the Death Note, and card/statue/crystal drops. The system is the engine under most of the Now screen's AFK Setup. [C1][C19][C20][C37]
- **Unlocks:** Always on. Maps open by killing their quota on the previous map (doors, see M2); W2, W3, W4 open by boss (Amarok, Efaunt, Chizoar), each needing a boss key at the arena. Death Note (multikill) opens through Construction in W3; Sleepin' On The Job/Idle talents come with the class tabs. [C2][C9][C18]
- **Scope:** Per-character: AFK Target, kills per map (`KLA_<slot>`), survivability, food, talents, equipped cards, obols, star signs, prayers. Account-wide: Death Note rank (kills summed over characters), merits, arcade, vault, guild, Post Office is per character, shrines are per map, drop-rate multipliers like stamps/cards-collected are account. [C3][C17][C18][C33]
- **Inputs → outputs:** In: time away, the map's monster stats, character damage/accuracy/HP/DEF, food, and AFK-rate bonuses. Out: kills (x kill-per-kill) → portal progress and Death Note ranks; drops (coins, materials, cards, talent books, crystals, statues); class EXP; guild/shrine EXP. [C37][C39][C45][C47][C49]
- **Goals and Milestones:** the kill ladder of each world (table below), Death Note ranks per monster (25k / 100k / 250k / 500k / 1M / 5M / 100M kills), and the Barbarian Zow Chow thresholds. [C2][C10][C17][C59]

- **Feeds / fed by:** Fed by: AFK gain sources (talents, bribes, cards, guild, Post Office, star signs, prayers, shrines), respawn sources (merits W1-W4, shrines, star signs, chips), kill-per-kill sources (skulls talents, Fibers prayer, Bubba, vault), drop-rate sources (stamps, cards, bubbles, arcade). Feeds: portals (map progression), Death Note, cards, statues (crystal mobs), vials/materials (monster drops), class EXP, boss keys (via maps). [C20][C43][C14][C45][C33]
- **Per-session actions:** (1) Claim on login; (2) for each character: AFK Target set, survivability 100%, food enough for the Expected Return, right talent preset and cards, shrine on the right map, prayer curses checked; (3) push the frontier: put the Portal pusher on the frontier map; (4) swap to a farm map once the doors are open and a card or drop is the goal. Idle/capped state: no AFK Target; survivability < 100; food ends before return; Unending Energy curse text says AFK is capped at 10 h (**Disputed**, M, text only; C35). [C19][C39][C34][C35]
- **AFK Setup implications:** The core of an AFK Setup: pusher on the frontier, the rest on the highest-EXP map they survive at 100%, a specialist on crystal/card farming, fighters at Death Note-relevant maps. Carry cap and food are the two stockpile limits.
- **Min-max by stage:** see the dedicated section below.
- **Common mistakes:** AFK where survivability < 100%; stale food; leaving a Zerg Rushogen or Ruck Sack prayer with the wrong curse; ignoring the Unending Energy curse text (10 h cap, **Disputed**, confidence M; C35); shrines on a map nobody AFKs on; staying on a cleared map after its card is done; pushing a map whose damage cannot overkill. [C34][C35][C33]

- **Sources:** per claim.

## Mechanics catalogue

Fields: `char.*` = the Toolbox character object (`characters[]` in Account State), `OLA[n]` = `accountOptions[n]`. Parser files are in `parsers/`.

### M1. Maps, monsters and AFK types
- **Rule:** 327 map slots, each with one AFK monster whose `AFKtype` decides what the character does there (FIGHTING, MINING, FISHING, CATCHING, ...). W1 maps 1-40, W2 51-72, W3 101-120, W4 151-166 (W5 from 201). [C1]
- **Caps / unlock:** none; unlock by doors (M2). **Costs:** none.
- **Field:** `d/shared-data.json` `mapNames`, `mapEnemiesArray`, `mapEnemies`, `mapMonsterCounts`, `mapDetails`; `d/monsters.json`; adapter: `characters[].afkType/afkTarget/monsterFace/targetMonster` [C19].

### M2. Portal doors and kill counters
- **Rule:** each map has 1+ doors; door i opens at `mapPortals[map][i]` kills on that map by this character; save stores kills LEFT (`KLA_<slot>`), Toolbox keeps kills done for door 0 only (`char.kills[map]`). A map is cleared when kills >= door 0. Cleared is per character, not per account. [C2][C3][C4][C5]
- **W1-W4 values:** see the map table; spikes: W1 Poopy Sewers 20,000 and Rats Nest 35,000, Base of the Bark second door 50,000, W3 Stache Split second door 100,000 and Crystal Basecamp 120,000, W3 Wam Wonderland 250,000, W4 up to 350,000. [C2][C10]
- **Quirks:** hidden/second doors may be missing from MapDetails (map 203, W5); boss arenas are not kill doors; the wiki labels a few rows by a different map than the data. [C6][C7][C9][C11]
- **Field:** `getMapDoorRequirements`, `hasClearedMap`, `getPortalKillsLeftDisplay`, `getFilteredPortals` in `portals.ts`; Account State `characters[].nextPortal`, `account.frontier` (raw `KLA_*`), `characters[].portalKillsLeft`. [C3][C4][C5][C6][C7][C54]

### M3. Kill-per-kill (how many kills one death is worth)
- **Rule:** see claim; applies to portals AND Death Note. Fibers of Absence, Monster Decimator and the three Skulls talents are the explicitly portal-named sources. W-band gear applies from W2 (map index 50+). [C12][C13][C14][C15]
- **Caps:** none in code; diminishing returns only from map index 300 (applied to the base and per tier). Confidence H. **Unlock:** talents by class tier; Fibers via worship waves.
- **Field:** `getKillPerKill` in `damage.ts#L1503`; `char.fighting.killPerKill`/`finalKillsPerHour` in the adapter. [C12]

### M4. Multikill, overkill and the Death Note
- **Rule:** overkill tiers = 1 + count of i with maxDamage >= 2 x HP x 2^(i+1) (exponent 5 instead of 2 on maps >= 300) [C16]; each tier adds the world's Death Note rank plus other per-tier sources; the term joins kill-per-kill only while overkilling and with `towersTwo` > 0.5 and accuracy > 1.5 x DEF. Death Note ranks per monster as in the table. [C16][C17][C18]
- **Field:** `getMultiKillBase`, `getMultiKillPerTier`, `getMultiKillTiers` (`damage.ts`), `account.deathNote[world].rank/mobs`, `account.deathNote.miniBosses`. The mini-boss (sneaking) Death Note page rank is added to MultiKill perTier on every map regardless of world [C68]. [C16][C17]

### M5. AFK target
- **Rule:** the monster rawName in `AFKtarget_<slot>`; `Nothing` = no gains; `Paying_Respect` = no rate. A character earns nothing with no target. [C19][C20]
- **Field:** `char.afkTarget`, `char.afkType`, `char.targetMonster`, `char.mapIndex`; `CurrentMap_<slot>`. [C19][C54]

### M6. Fighting AFK gain rate
- **Rule:** `rate = (0.40 + S/100) x afkMulti`, min 1%. S and the shared pool are in the claims. Talents (max asymptotes): Sleepin' On The Job 21, Idle Brawling/Shooting/Casting 20, Tick Tock 8, each `x1 x L/(L+x2)` with x2 = 50. Cheap flat sources: bribe +5 (6,000 coins), Silly Snoozer +2, Forsaken +6, Post Office Civil War Memory Box 13 x L/(L+200) (max 400 levels), guild REM Fighting 10 x L/(L+50), Chaotic Amarok card +2.5 x star tier, the W2 merit +2% per level for the first N family characters. [C20][C21][C24][C25][C26][C29][C31][C30][C27][C63][C32]
- **Caps:** no global cap in code; floor 1%. The old wiki "85% max" is invalid. Prayer: Zerg Rushogen (max level 20). [C20][C23][C34]
- **Unlock/costs:** see claims; bribe at the W1 pig bank; guild and arcade per their systems.
- **Field:** `getAfkGain(character, characters, account)` in `character.ts#L3232`; result `{afkGains, breakdown}` -> `char.afkGains`. [C20]

### M7. Skilling AFK gain rate (cross-reference)
- **Rule:** base 50% for specialised skills; see claim; owned by the skills dossiers. Divinity is a flat 100%; Spelunking has its own composition. [C22]

### M8. AFK time: claim, max time, candy, double claim
- **Rule:** time away = `PlayerAwayTime x 1000` ms, no cap in Toolbox; the only stated max is the Unending Energy curse text (10 h, **Disputed**, confidence M: text only, no code path reads it as a cap [C35]). Time candies convert to AFK time on one character; Biggie Hours card and the Double Exp Scheme bribe give a chance of a double claim/EXP; the Double AFK Gain Ticket is an account currency. [C35][C36][C27][C26]
- **Unknown:** the default maximum AFK time. See Open questions. [C35]
- **Field:** `char.afkTime` (ms); `account.timeAway.GlobalTime`; `calculateAfkTime` (`misc.ts#L681`; cap text lives in the Unending_Energy prayer, read at `character.ts#L1668`/`#L2162`).

### M9. Kills per hour
- **Rule:** see claim (spawn-limited ceiling vs kill-speed-limited), then x AFK rate x survivability x kill-per-kill. The data-derived Froggy Fields ceiling (computed 9,112 kills/h at the W1 merit maximum) disagrees with the v1.14 wiki figure (3,709). [C37][C38][C41][C23]
- **Field:** `getMaxDamage(character, ...)` -> `killsPerHour`, `finalKillsPerHour`, `survivability`, `respawnRate`. Zero hit chance zeroes kills/h outright [C69]; AFK kill talents D/K and attack-speed sources feed the model [C66][C67]; monster max-HP curses feed `getMonsterHpTotal` [C64]. ActiveOn `characters[].fighting.*`, `afk.fighting.finalKillsPerHour`. [C37][C54]

### M10. Survivability, food, deaths
- **Rule:** see claim: if net damage per hour (map factor x per-hit damage - food heal per hour) is <= 0, survivability is 100%; otherwise `t/(t + respawn/3600)`. Per-hit damage uses DEF powers; Bricky Skin, Mana Is Life, Goblet of Hemoglobin, Bored To Death and Locally Sourced Organs modify it. [C39][C40][C41][C44][C58]
- **Field:** `getSurvivability`/`getMonsterDamage` (`damage.ts`), `getHealthFoodBonus` (`misc.ts#L1371`); ActiveOn `AfkFighting` fields. [C54]

### M11. Accuracy and hit chance
- **Rule:** see claim; monsters need accuracy >= 0.5 x DEF for any hit, 1.5 x DEF for 100%. Hit chance feeds both kills/h and overkill eligibility. [C42][C16]
- **Field:** `calcHitChance` -> `playerInfo.hitChance`; `utility/hit-chance.mjs`.

### M12. Respawn rate
- **Rule:** see claim. Merits: +2% per level, 10 levels each in W1, W2, W3, W4 (account tasks `Tasks[2][world][1]`). Other sources are shrine, schematic, chip, gear, star sign, achievement. [C43][C62]
- **Field:** `getRespawnRate` (`character.ts#L1789`).

### M13. Drop rate
- **Rule:** see claim (additive base x long multiplier chain); one merged stat since 2026-03-06. W1-W4 relevant sources: luck, stamps, cards, Clover Shrine, Gold Charm guild, arcade, achievements 377/381, Midas Minded prayer, Droppin' Loads bubble, owl, vault, Trove sigil. [C45][C46][C62]
- **Field:** `getDropRate` (`character.ts#L2378`) -> `char.dropRate`.

### M14. Card drops
- **Rule:** per-kill base chances in the map table; drop rate multiplies; a card you do not own has a much higher chance (wiki). Cards are covered by the cards dossier. [C49][C50][C46]
- **Field:** `d/monsterDrops.json` (`Cards*` rawNames); `account.cards`.

### M15. Crystal mobs (and statues)
- **Rule:** see claim; base 0.05% per mob, capped at 10%, extra chance becomes EXP; crystal HP = 15x. Statues drop from crystals. [C47][C48]
- **Field:** `getPlayerCrystalChance` (`character.ts#L3082`), `OLA[101]` guaranteed crystal kills used, merit W4 row 0.

### M16. Map-bound modifiers: shrines and prayers
- **Rule:** shrines apply to everyone standing on the map where built and level up from AFK claims there; Primordial (AFK +), Undead (respawn +), Clover (drop +), Crescent (crystal/giant +), Summereading (EXP +). Prayers equip per character and always carry a curse. [C33][C34][C35]
- **Field:** `account.shrines`, `getShrineBonus(shrines, i, mapIndex, ...)`; `char.activePrayers`.

### M17. Boss arenas and keys
- **Rule:** arena maps are listed but opened by keys, not kills. Already in the KB (`map.fact.boss-gates`). [C9][C2]

### M18. Multi-character door lock (W6+)
- **Rule:** door 0 of ten W6+ maps needs N characters to have cleared it. No W1-W4 map has it. [C8]
- **Field:** `getMultiCharDoorLock` (`portals.ts#L90`).

### M19. W5+ extensions (one line each)
- W5: maps 201-213 need 15,000 to 3,000,000 kills; map 203 has a hidden second door; Smolderin' Plateau card set +fighting AFK; Zerg-type prayers continue. [C6][C53][C27]
- W6: multi-character door lock; Death Note world-6 page; kill-per-kill band index 90 (250-299). [C8][C13]
- W7: respawn scaling 0.65, diminishing multikill from map 300, Tesseract map bonuses. [C53][C52]

### M20. Monster stat curve (EXP / HP / DEF)

Computed at GFmulti = 1: qty 10,000 gives 0.574 x Amount, qty 1,000,000 gives 1.141 x Amount (a Golden Dumpling, Amount 4, is then +2.30% and +4.56% AllAFK). ### M33. Stat point allocation and the main stat (AFK damage lever)
- **Rule:** the main stat per class (`mainStatMap`) is Strength (Warrior line, Squire line), Agility (Archer line, Hunter line), Wisdom (Mage line, Shaman, Wizard) or Luck (Beginner, Journeyman, Maestro, Voidwalker). `damageFromStat = mainStat * (1 + (STRENGTH_IN_NUMBERS + KNOWLEDGE_IS_POWER + LUCKY_HIT + main-stat bubble [FARQUAD_FORCE / QUICKDRAW_QUIVER / SMARTER_SPELLS]) / 100)`. It enters damage three ways: (a) the HP/MP term `1 + (damageFromStat^0.7 + ...)/100`; (b) base damage adds `damageFromStat` plus `min(150, 2 * WeaponPower + damageFromStat)` inside the weapon term; (c) bubbles scaling per 250 points of STR/AGI/WIS (Trione, Tritwo, Trithree; STR also uses max(STR, LUK) for Luck classes). Damage feeds kills/h through `first` (average hit incl. crit, hit chance, D effect) and the overkill tier. [C85][C86]
- **Rule (the other stats):** accuracy is built from the stat that is NOT the main one: STR classes use Wisdom, AGI classes use Strength, WIS classes use Agility, Luck classes use Luck; `accuracy = (acc^... )`, specifically `(stat * bubbles * buffs) -> (x/4)^1.4 + x + baseAccuracy`, times `(1 + (x + 2 * CardSet4)/200)`. Zero hit chance (accuracy < 0.5 x monster DEF) sets AFK kills/h to 0, so under-investing in the accuracy stat can zero an AFK map. Agility also drives movement speed (`(agi+1)^0.4 - 1)/40` below 1,000; `(agi-1000)/(agi+2500)*0.5 + 0.371` above), which enters kills/h as `mapDist / (130 * speed/100)`, and crit chance (`((agi-1000)/(agi+2500))*0.5 + 0.255`, /2.3). Strength drives crit damage (same shape, 0.255) and max HP (`strength*(1+STRENGTH_IN_NUMBERS/100)^1.05`); Wisdom adds flat Max MP. Max HP feeds survivability (`maxHp / monsterDamage...`), the 100% gate of AFK. [C87][C88][C89]
- **Rule (allocation):** the save keeps the four stats in `PersonalValuesMap.StatList` (index 0 STR, 1 AGI, 2 WIS, 3 LUK, 4 level); Toolbox exposes them as `char.stats.{strength,agility,wisdom,luck,level}` but does NOT read unspent stat points or points-per-level, so "how many points to put where" cannot be computed from the pin. The max-of-damage-first rule below is derived from the formulas above, not a tier-3/4 source. [C90]
- **Field:** `getMaxDamage` invariants (`damage.ts#L96-L120`), `getBaseDamage` (`damage.ts#L890-L980`), `getDamageFromHpMp` (`damage.ts#L823`), `getAccuracy` (`damage.ts#L984-L1047`), `getPlayerSpeedBonus` (`character.ts#L3192`), `getCritChance`/`getCritDamage`, `getMaxHp` (`damage.ts#L1071`), `mainStatMap` (`talents.ts#L87`), `character.ts#L310-L317` (StatList parse).

## Common mistakes (detect from the save)

- No AFK Target (`AFKtarget_<i>` = Nothing): zero gains. [C19]
- Survivability below 100%: `getMaxDamage().survivability < 100`; food missing. [C39][C58]
- Overkill prerequisites not met: max damage < 2x HP or accuracy < 1.5x DEF means the multikill term is ignored in kill-per-kill. [C16]
- Standing on a map whose door is already open and whose card is done while the frontier waits. [C2][C4]
- Unending Energy active (curse text: 10 h cap, **Disputed**, M, C35) or Ruck Sack curse active (-AFK). [C34][C35]
- Shrine on a map nobody uses (shrines only level from AFK claims on their own map). [C33]
- Stacked monster-HP curses (Midas Minded + Big Brain Time + Jawbreaker = +700% HP) silently cutting kills/h and overkill. [C64]
- Zero hit chance (accuracy < 0.5 x DEF): kills/h is 0. [C69]
