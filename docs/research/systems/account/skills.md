## Skills (system)

- **What it is:** the 18 non-combat skills (+ class level). Four gathering skills (Mining, Choppin, Fishing, Catching) are AFK targets ruled by one shared efficiency-vs-toughness engine; Smithing is an anvil-driven town skill; Trapping and Worship are W3 skills with their own dossiers. Skill index map: 1 mining, 2 smithing, 3 chopping, 4 fishing, 5 alchemy, 6 catching, 7 trapping, 8 construction, 9 worship, 10 cooking, 11 breeding, 12 laboratory, 13 sailing, 14 divinity, 15 gaming, 16 farming, 17 sneaking, 18 summoning, 19 spelunking, 20 research (0 = character/class level) [C1 · T1 · packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/parsers/parseMaps.ts#L89-L110] (H)
- **Unlocks:** Mining/Choppin/Smithing from the start of W1; Fishing and Catching from W2; Trapping/Worship W3. Nodes have no skill-level gate except mine doors and two fishing portals; tools carry the level requirement. Mine doors by Mining level (mapPortals): Tunnels Entrance (map 6) door needs 10 -> Freefall Caverns (7); map 7 doors need 25 (-> Ol' Straightaway) and 81; map 10 needs 40 (-> Echoing Egress); map 11 needs 50 and 60 (the 60 door -> Slip Slidy Ledges); map 12 needs 60 (-> Motherlode Pit); portal requirement for a mining/fishing map is the skill level, not kills [C23 · T1 · packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/data/website-data/shared-data.json mapPortals; packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/parsers/portals.ts#L42-L50] (H) Fishing portals: Faraway Piers (map 54) needs Fishing 15, Deepwater Docks (map 55) needs Fishing 30; Large fish need Seasalt Island via Island Expeditions [C40 · T1 · packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/data/website-data/shared-data.json mapPortals 54/55; https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Fishing (rev 2026-10-03)] (H)
- **Scope:** per-character: skill level/EXP, tool, efficiency, AFK target, talents, Post Office box, star sign, obols, equipped cards. Account-wide: Skill Mastery totals, stamps, bubbles, vials, statues, storage counts (Copper Ore, Oak Logs, Grass Leaves), Smithy sets, minigame highscores. Each character's skillsInfo is built from raw Lv0 (level), Exp0 (exp) and ExpReq0 (exp needed) arrays for indexes 0-20; a level of -1 is read as 0 [C2 · T1 · packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/parsers/character.ts#L391-L405] (H)
- **Inputs -> outputs:** time (AFK or active) + a tool + efficiency -> items (ore/logs/fish/bugs), skill EXP, card drops; EXP feeds levels, which feed Mastery, tool equip levels, doors and recipes.
- **Feeds / fed by:** tools come from the Anvil (Smithing); logs, fish and bugs gate the next tool tier of the other skills (hatchet chain needs trees reachable with the previous hatchet; Nets 1-3 need Choppin logs; Catch Net stamp eats Goldfish, Fishing Rod stamp eats Flies, Bugsack eats Hermit Can). Ore feeds Forge, Stamps, Alchemy, Refinery salts (Redox, Nullo), Lab chips.

### M-A. Shared framework
1. **Skill level EXP curve.** Skill level EXP requirement: index 2 (smithing) (15+t^2+13t)*(1.225-min(0.114,0.135t/(t+50)))^t-26; index 9 (worship) (15+t^1.3+6t)*(1.17-min(0.07,0.135t/(t+50)))^t-26; index 8 (construction) a separate curve with a break at t=71; index 0 (class) its own curve; every other skill incl. mining, chopping, fishing, catching, trapping uses (15+t^2+15t)*(1.225-min(0.18,0.135t/(t+50)))^t-30 [C3 · T1 · packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/parsers/misc.ts#L882-L899] (H) Field: `characters[].skillsInfo.<skill>.{level,exp,expReq}` from Lv0/Exp0/ExpReq0. 2. **Skill Mastery.** Skill Mastery sums a skill's level over all characters (calculateTotalSkillsLevel) and ranks it 0-7 at total levels 150 / 200 / 300 / 400 / 500 / 750 / 1000 [C4 · T1 · packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/parsers/misc.ts#L804-L840] (H) Every Skill Mastery bonus is zero while rift.currentRift < 15 (code gate); the prior research and the wiki say the Rift is cleared at 'Rift 16' (likely 1-indexed vs the save's 0-indexed counter) [C5 · T1 · packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/parsers/misc.ts#L842-L844] (H) Mastery rewards for a gathering skill: 150 +25% that skill's EXP; 200 +10% that skill's efficiency; 300 every card of that skill becomes passive (read from all owned cards, not just equipped); 400 +10% (applied as per-skill sum) skill EXP; 500 +5% all-skill efficiency; 750 +1% 3D Printer output; 1000 +25% all-skill EXP [C6 · T2 · https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Mining (rev 2026-10-06) + packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/parsers/misc.ts#L848-L880, efficiency.ts#L340, character.ts#L606-L612] (H) Field: `account.totalSkillsLevels.<skill>.{level,rank}`, `account.rift.currentRift`.
3. **Efficiency shape.** Every gathering skill's power = tool Weapon_Power x tool multiplier + flat base + tool again + statue + slab bubble x floor(looted items/100) + endgame bubble x max(1, floor((class level-500)/10)); efficiency then uses power^1.3 in the base and (1+power/100) as a multiplier, so tool power counts three times (the 'three times' wording is interpretation); flat base is 4 for Mining, Choppin, Trapping and Worship and 3 for Fishing and Catching [C12 · T1 · packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/parsers/efficiency.ts#L234-L247] (M) The wiki states each skill level gives +0.5% of that skill's efficiency, matching the (1 + level/200) multiplier in Toolbox for mining, chopping, fishing, catching and worship (trapping uses level/100) [C11 · T2 · https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Skills + packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/parsers/efficiency.ts#L295, L392, L466, L522, L580] (H) AllBaseSkillEff is a plain sum of Shiny, Stamp (All_Skill_Efficiency), Nobisect blessing, Post Office Myriad Crate, Lab chip 11, Supersource star talent and a Lab jewel; ## Per-session actions

- Every skiller: confirm a pickaxe/hatchet/rod/net is equipped (slot 0-3), AFK target harvest chance >= 100% (efficiency >= 10x Defence) or the best EXP/material pick; craft the next tool when Lv and materials allow.
- Fishers: set bait and line to the wanted depth; fishing 30+ should leave Salty Shores; play the 5 daily minigames (Mining, Choppin, Fishing, Catching) which feed Bobbin' Bobbers.
- Keep Copper Ore, Oak Logs, Grass Leaves in storage (talents scale with log of stored counts).
- Idle/capped: harvest chance 0; carry cap full; no tool; anvil slots empty/full.
