## Alchemy cauldrons (brew, new bubbles, Boosts)

- **What it is:** four coloured brewing cauldrons (Power, Quicc, High-IQ, Kazam) that characters stand in to fill a brew bar. A full bar is spent either on a roll for the cauldron's next bubble, or on a Boost (Speed, Luck, Cost, 2x Extra). Bubbles are then levelled with materials plus liquid. [C1][C3][C11]
- **Unlocks:** W2 town; each character must click the W2 cauldron once before it can gain Alchemy EXP or be assigned (wiki, confirmed 2026-10-19) [C51]. Cowbo Jones quest tutorial is unverified prior research [C63]. Liquid cauldrons at Alchemy 1/20/35/80 (wiki, confirmed) [C34]; the level gate is not in the parsers. Sigils need the W4 Lab node [C44].
- **Scope:** mostly account-wide (bubble levels, Boosts, P2W, vials). Per character: the activity slot (cauldron, liquid or sigil), Alchemy level, WIS, talents, and the 1-3 equipped big (ACTIVE) bubbles. [C2][C17][C31][C33]
- **Inputs -> outputs:** time x brewers' speed -> brew; brew -> new bubble roll or Boost level; materials + liquid -> bubble levels; coins -> Pay 2 Win. [C11][C17][C21][C24]
- **Goals and Milestones:**

| Milestone | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Boost level cap per stat | 170 | [C4] |
| P2W caps | cauldron Speed 150, New Bubble 125, Boost Req 100, liquid Regen 100 / Cap 80 | [C22] |
| Brew per roll steps | 0.01, 0.15, 0.75, 5, 20, then 88 (6th), 277, 677 ... | [C11][C16] |
| Odds of the n-th roll (1x) | 101% -> 67% -> 45% -> 30% -> 21% -> 14% -> 9.6% -> 6.6% -> 4.6% -> 3.1% | [C12][C16] |
| Seats | 1 base slot for big bubbles, 2nd via bribe, 3rd via Breeding R100 | [C31][C32] |
| Ivory cauldron | up to 4 purchases, 1.5x brew and 1.5x new-bubble odds each | [C37][C38] |
| NBLB daily bubbles | min 3, max 10 | [C47] |

- **Feeds / fed by:** fed by Alchemy level, WIS, stamps, guild, Post Office, talents, vials, gem shop, W1-W3 materials, liquid, coins. Feeds stats (TotalSTR/AGI/WIS/LUK), skill efficiency, anvil, construction, cooking, lab, etc. via bubble stats [C1][C18][C27].
- **Per-session actions:** seat every character; spend banked brew (roll or Boost); spend liquid and materials on bubble levels; equip big bubbles for the AFK Target; check Ivory seats. Idle states: character activity -1, brew bar full and unspent (`attempts >= 1`), empty big-bubble slot. [C14][C31][C33]
- **AFK Setup implications:** the alchemy seat is a Parallel Slot beside the AFK Target. Brew per character scales with Alchemy level and WIS, so the best brewers go in cauldrons, low-value characters in liquids (the earlier "2x Alchemy EXP in liquids" claim was unsupported by the wiki and is dropped). [C17][C33]
- **Min-max by stage:** see section below.
- **Common mistakes:** brew left banked; rolling at 1-5% odds instead of Boosting; Luck/Extra at 0 while rolling; Cost Boost before Water-limited accounts need it; big bubbles not matched to the AFK Target; Ivory never bought; no Bargain-bubble use. [C10][C16][C61]
- **Save and Toolbox fields:** see Mechanics catalogue.
- **Sources:** inline per claim.

## Mechanics catalogue

M1. **Cauldrons and indices.** `CAULDRON_INFO` order power, quicc, high-iq, kazam; bubble raw name `aUpgrades` + letter O/G/P/Y + index; CauldronBubbles prefix `_`, `a`, `b`, `c`. Field: `account.alchemy.bubbles.<cauldron>[i].{level,index,rawName,func,x1,x2,stat,itemReq}` from raw `CauldronInfo[0..3][i]`. [C1][C2]

M2. **Bubble catalogue.** 35 slots per cauldron (0-34); 0-19 are W1-W4 material bubbles, 20-24 use Bits (W5 Gaming), Sailing or Lava items, 25-34 are W5-W7 or `BUBBLE` placeholders. Passive = small, `ACTIVE` stat tag = big. Full list in the table below. [C66][C31]

M3. **Bubble value curves.** `growth(func,level,x1,x2)` unrounded: `addDECAY` = x1*L (soft cap past 50k), `decay` = x1*L/(L+x2), `decayMulti` = 1+x1*L/(L+x2), `bigBase` = x1+x2*L. Maximum of `decay` is x1; of `decayMulti` is x1+1. `getBubbleBonus(account, NAME, shouldRound, shouldMultiply, cauldron)`. [C27]

M4. **Class bubble (index 1).** Warriors Rule / Archer or Bust / Mage Is Best (`decayMulti` 2, 50) multiply every small bubble of the same cauldron with index > 1, != 16, < 30, not Carpenter, not tagged passz/ACTIVE/AllCharz, for characters of the matching main stat only. Kazam has none. [C28]

M5. **Cauldron multi bubble (index 16).** Multorange / Premigreen / Severapurple (`decayMulti` 1.4, 30) multiply only: quicc {0,6,9,12,14}, power {0,2,4,7,14}, high-iq {0,2,6,12,14}. [C29]

M6. **Brew requirement per roll** `MaxCauldronQTY(n)`, n = unbroken run of unlocked bubbles: n<5 -> 0.01/0.15/0.75/5/20; else `1 + (3(n-3))^2.2 * 1.3^(n-3)`. `cauldrons.<c>.req`, `.progress`, `.attempts = floor(progress/req)`, `.unlockedBubbles`. [C11][C14]

M7. **New-bubble odds** base `139*0.73^(n+1)/max(0.1n+1,1)` %; multiplier = Luck Boost x P2W New Bubble x (1+Bubble Breakthrough/100) x Ivory. `cauldrons.<c>.newBubble.{chance,multi,expectedBubbles,breakdown}`. [C12][C13][C15][C64]

M8. **Expected bubbles** from banked brew: loop of rolls with rising cost and falling odds, chance capped at 100%, up to 1000. [C15]

M9. **Character brew speed** (per hour): `round(AlchLv^0.8) * (1+(stamp AlchSpd + guild[5] + Brewstachio + Potion Package)/100) * (1 + P2Wplayer * AlchLv * (1+statCurve(WIS)/0.6) * (1+(Busy Brewin' + Broken Time + Speed_in_Town star sign)/100)/100) * (1+Mafioso/100)`. Only the logged-in character uses live WIS, added talent levels, star sign and town speed. `cauldrons.<c>.brewSpeed.{value,multi}`. [C17][C18][C19]

M10. **Cauldron brew multiplier:** `(1+vialBrew/100)(1+P2Wspeed/100) * Ivory(1.5) * (1+round(5*SpeedBoost)/100)`; cauldron brew per hour = sum of brewers' speeds x this. [C7][C20]

M11. **Speed Boost:** +5% per level to the multiplier above, max 170 (= +850%). `cauldrons.<c>.boosts.speed.level`. [C4][C7]

M12. **Luck Boost:** new-bubble odds x `round(10*(1+0.05L))/10` (1.0 at 0, 1.1 at L1-2, 2.0 at 20, 3.0 at 40, 9.5 at 170). Wiki words it "+10% on odd levels, even do nothing"; the rounding gives 1.1 at L1-2, 1.2 at L3-4, so both views agree (step of +0.1 per two levels). `boosts.luck.level`. [C8][C9]

M13. **Cost Boost:** reduces bubble material cost (wiki, no percentage given). Toolbox stores the level but its cost function takes the discount as a caller-supplied `cauldronLevel` factor and nothing in the vendored parsers computes it. Formula UNKNOWN here. Liquid takes no discounts. [C9][C10][C25]

M14. **2x Extra Boost:** +2% per level chance a Boost lands twice (more than twice above 100%), applies to Boosting all four stats (wiki). Not modelled by Toolbox. [C9][C10]

M15. **Boost requirement (brew per level).** Each stat has its own `[progress, level]`; `req(L) = floor(1 + 2*(1.5(L+1))^1.6 * 1.073^(L+1) * m)`, `m = max((100 - 70P/(P+100))/100, 0.05)`, P = P2W Boost Req level. At P=0: L0 5, L10 386, L25 4,389, L50 75,072, L100 7.59M, L150 489M, L169 2.26B (P=100 multiplies by ~0.65). `boosts.<stat>.{progress,level,req}`. [C3][C5][C6]

M16. **Bubble level cost** per material `base*(rate - decay*L/(offset+L))^L` x discounts, clamped [1,1e9] and rounded; low bubbles (index <= 14) rate 1.35 / decay 0.3 / offset 50, high (> 14) 1.37 / 0.28 / 60; Liquid `base + floor(L/20)`; Bits `base*2^L` with only Bargain Tag and S-M-R-T. [C24][C25]

M17. **Cost reducers:** per-colour Bargain bubble (index 14, `decay` 40, 12), Cost Boost, Undeveloped Costs (Kazam 6) + Barley Brew vial (+1%/lvl), Liquid Shop Bargain Tag (x1=1, x2=1.19 per buy, resets daily), S-M-R-T. [C25][C26][C40]

M18. **Big bubbles / seats.** Equipped list `CauldronBubbles[charIdx]` strings (prefix+index); `account.alchemy` active bonus via `getActiveBubbleBonus` (equipped only; companion index 4 treats all as equipped). Slots: 1, +Changing the Code bribe (1.5M coins), +Breeding R100. [C31][C32]

M19. **Activity slot.** `CauldronJobs1[charIdx]`: 0-3 cauldron, 4-7 liquid (Water to Toxic), >=100 sigil (activity-100), -1 none; counted only for existing characters. `alchemy.activities[]`, `cauldrons.<c>.players[]`. [C33]

M20. **Pay 2 Win (coins), cauldron side.** Per cauldron Speed (cap 150, `3.2L+0.1L(L-1)` % brew), New Bubble (cap 125, x`1+2.5L/(L+100)`, 2.39x at 125), Boost Req (cap 100, -`70L/(L+100)`% = -35% at 100). Costs: Speed `2500*(1.15-0.117L/(100+L))^L`, New Bubble `3200*(1.18-0.145L/(100+L))^L`, Boost Req `3750*(1.2-0.14L/(100+L))^L`. Raw `CauldronP2W` holds levels (not coins; coin costs are computed): 12 cauldron levels (speed, newBubble, boostReq per cauldron), 8 liquid levels, vial [attempts, rng], player [speedLv, extraExpLv], sigil pairs, remaining vial attempts. [C2] `alchemy.p2w.cauldrons[i].{speed,newBubble,boostReq}.{level,cost,costToMax}`. [C20][C21][C22]

M21. **Pay 2 Win, Player.** `CauldronP2W[3]`: speedLv (`35L/(L+100)`, multiplies Alchemy level in character speed) and extraExpLv (`add` 1, 0.1). `p2w.player`. [C19][C23]

M22. **Liquid cauldrons (inputs).** Cap `ceil((1+Bleach/Dragonic+(meal+5*mastery)/100) * Viaduct * (10 + decantCap + vial + P2W cap + Da Daily Drip*(AlchLv/25)^0.3 + stamp + arcade))`; Decant Cap/Rate: next-level requirement is `floor(1.6+(1.25(L+1))^1.8)` brew, and each cap level adds +1 capacity (Water..Toxic; cauldron-side cap uses `round(10*decay(90,100,L))/10`). Bleach/Dragonic factor and regeneration: see M31-M32. [C35][C36][C67]

M23. **Gem shop:** Ivory Bubble Cauldrons (index 105, 300 gems, max 4), Bleach (106, 250, max 4), Dragonic (idx 93, 969 gems, max 1; **stale/possibly time-limited listing**, lastSeen 2.3.42 against game 2.3.540; the parser reads Dragonic from `accountOptions[123]`) [C68], Sigil Supercharge (110, 140, max 10, +20% Sigil EXP each). Ivory flags per cauldron in `accountOptions[32]` digit. [C37][C38]

M24. **Liquid Shop** (daily reset; price `round(x1*x2^purchasesToday)` with a per-item x2, not a flat doubling): Mediocre Obols, Weak Upg Stone, Distilled Water, One Measly Gem (5, x7.5), Star Book, EXP Balloon, Small Donation, Decent Obols, Talent Point (3, x1.3), Grand Obols, Bargain Tag (1, x1.19), Dense Water, A Pair of Gems (5, x10.5), Luminous Shell, second Talent Point row. ## Min-max by stage

Derived framework (tier 1 formulas, confidence L-M, to be validated per account): spending brew has three sinks and one currency. The cost of the next roll per bubble is `req(n)/odds(n)`: n=3 17, n=4 97, n=5 628, n=6 2.9k, n=7 10.3k, n=8 31.6k, n=9 88.7k, n=10 233k, n=12 1.41M brew (1x odds) [C16]. A Speed level at level L pays back its brew cost after `req(L)*(1+0.05L)/0.05` brew: L0 100, L10 11.6k, L25 197k, L50 5.25M [C61]. Luck and P2W New Bubble divide the roll cost (Luck 40 = 3x; P2W 125 = 2.39x). So: roll while expected cost per bubble is below the next Speed/Extra payback; Boost when it is above; re-evaluate when brew per hour changes. [C61]

- **W1 early / mid / late:** Alchemy is not open. Prepare: farm the W1-W2 bubble materials listed in the catalogue (Copper, Oak, Spore Cap, Jungle Logs, Fly, Iron, Gold; bubble costs are mostly W1-W3 drops) [C24][C66]; community: "focus on anything related to gaining materials first" [C53]; stamp AlchSpd and a Potion Package Post Office box feed brew later [C18]. Confidence M.
- **W2 early (first bubbles):** click the cauldron on every character [C51]; seat all characters, high Alchemy level and WIS brewing [C17]; roll the first 4-5 bubbles of every cauldron, they cost <= 20 brew at >= 21% odds [C16]; unlock bubbles then level to 5-10 so they are "primed" [C55]; use a Shaman (Bubble Breakthrough) to force early bubbles [C55][C64]; buy Ivory cauldrons first among gem items (wiki: worth it early) [C52]; P2W Player speed after each Alchemy level (prior order, L) [C59]; liquid rate before cap [C53]. Level order of bubbles: stat bubbles first (Roid Ragin, Swift Steppin, Stable Jenius), class bubble, skill efficiency (Hearty Diggy, Hocus Choppus), tools, big bubbles for the AFK Target; community tier SSS: Warriors Rule, Wyoming Blood, Sploosh Sploosh, Swift Steppin, Shaquracy, Bug Squared, Mage Is Best, Stable Jenius [C57]. Existing ActiveOn Bubble Plan has the same order [C60].
- **W2 mid:** once the 6th-7th roll costs ~3-10k brew (odds ~7-10%), switch bars to Boosts: Speed and 2x Extra first, Luck/Cost later [C53][C59]; Luck is worth its level mostly on odd steps [C9]; keep levelling to 50% of bubble maximum, then 75%, then 90% [C54]; next 5 bubbles use W2 materials, then W3 [C58]; Kazam: Prowesessary, Undeveloped Costs (cost reducer) [C57][C60]; spend Water daily (bargain tags from Alchemy 20/35 unlocks) [C34][C26]; vials: upgrade when cheap [C53].
- **W2 late:** Laaarrrryyyy (SS tier), Cookin Roadkill (SS, Alchemy EXP), Brewstachio (A, brew speed), Droppin Loads, Grind Time (S) [C57]; Bargain bubbles once index 14 is reached [C25]; P2W cauldron Speed then New Bubble then Boost Req last; "buy P2W when cheap, where stuck" [C53][C59].
- **W3 early / mid:** bubble costs shift to W3 materials, Trench H2O at Alchemy 35 funds them [C34]; roll only as long as expected cost < next Speed payback, otherwise Boost; Atom Collider Boron pays 1e8+ first ingredients in particles [C48]; keep levels to the 75-90% band [C54]; Droppin' Loads, Grind Time, Call Me Bob (late) [C57].
- **W3 late:** Boost levels dominate brew spend; expect Speed ~25-50 where a level costs 4k-75k brew (derived) [C6]; P2W Boost Req (-35% at 100) becomes worth it as `req(L)` reaches millions [C6][C22]; Bleach/Dragonic liquid cauldrons for liquid caps [C37].
- **W4:** the Lab's No Bubble Left Behind gives free levels to the lowest bubbles (3-10 a day) so keep low bubbles for it [C47]; Sigils open with the Lab node; community: only after maxing coloured cauldrons and investing in liquid cauldrons; first sigils Big Muscle, Pumped Kicks, Odd Literature, Good Fortune are cheap (2/3/5/8 points) [C44][C46][C53]; Peculiar Vial (liquid regen) and Pea Pod (sigil speed) are the late picks [C46]; Sigil Supercharge gem item (+20% EXP, 10x) [C37].
- **Per class:** Warriors/Archers/Mages benefit from their class bubble only on that class's characters, so the account should keep a matching-class brewer or accept the multiplier applies to those characters' bubble bonuses [C28]; Shamans carry Bubble Breakthrough [C64].
- **Disagreement:** gameslikefinder (2026-09-17) says Speed + 2x Extra first; the 2025 Steam thread (pre-patch) says unlock bubbles first and keep Boosts modest (5-10) before heavy investment; ActiveOn's prior dossier says roll until ~5% odds then Speed. They agree on the order roll-cheap-then-Speed; they disagree on the switch point (4-5 bubbles vs ~9th). Confidence L-M. [C53][C55][C59]
