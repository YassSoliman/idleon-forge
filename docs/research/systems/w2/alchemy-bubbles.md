## Alchemy Bubbles

- **What it is:** four brewing cauldrons (Power/orange, Quicc/green, High-IQ/purple, Kazam/yellow). Characters assigned to a cauldron fill its brew bar. A full bar is spent either on a roll for the cauldron's next bubble or on a cauldron Boost. Bubbles are levelled with materials plus liquid and give stat bonuses. Small (passive) bubbles count account-wide with no setup; big (active) bubbles count only when equipped on a character. [C1] [C2] [C3]
- **Unlocks:** W2 town (YumYum Grotto). Each character must click the cauldron once before it can brew or gain Alchemy EXP; no level gate is stated. Cowbo Jones' quests teach it. The first 5 bubbles of a cauldron are nearly free. [C4] [C5]
- **Scope:** bubble levels, brew bars, Boost levels, Pay 2 Win levels, which bubbles are unlocked: account-wide. Per character: the cauldron it brews in (`CauldronJobs1[i]`), its Alchemy level (brew speed), and its equipped big bubbles (`CauldronBubbles[i]`). Class matters for the class-multi bubbles. [C6] [C7] [C8]
- **Inputs → outputs:** in: brew (character time x speed), bubble materials (W1-W3 items, Bits), liquid (Water Drops, N2, Trench H2O, Toxic Hg). Out: stat bonuses (STR/AGI/WIS/LUK, damage, efficiency, drop rate, skill multi-chance, class EXP), cost discounts, and more bubble levels. [C9] [C10]
- **Goals and Milestones:** see "Milestones" under the Mechanics catalogue (new-bubble table, half-value levels, AutoReview account tiers). [C11] [C12] [C13]
- **Feeds / fed by:**
  - Feeds: total stats, damage, mining/choppin efficiency (Hearty Diggy, Hocus Choppus), tools, multi-ore/fish/log/bug (big bubbles), Anvil (Hammer Hammer, Anvilnomics), Construction (Call Me Bob, Carpenter), Trapping (Call Me Ash), Worship (Call Me Pope, Gospel Leader), Statues (Startue EXP), Cooking/Lab/Breeding/Sailing/Gaming/Divinity (W4-W5 bubbles), Upgrade Vault (sum of bubble levels capped at 100 each), Atom Collider (Atom Split), Class EXP (Grind Time). [C14] [C15] [C16]
  - Fed by: Alchemy levels, Vials (brew speed vials, Barley Brew cost), Liquids, Pay 2 Win, Stamps (brew speed, bubble cost), Gem Shop (Ivory Bubble Cauldrons 1.5x), Post Office (Potion Package), Guild, Bribe "Changing the Code" (2nd slot), Breeding arena wave 100 (3rd slot), Companion Sheepie, W4 Lab No Bubble Left Behind, W3 Atom Collider Boron, W5 Divinity Kattlekruk. [C17] [C18] [C19] [C20]
- **Per-session actions:** (1) spend banked brew (new bubble or Boost); (2) level bubbles with materials and liquid on hand, Bargain Tag first on days it is bought; (3) check every character has its big-bubble slots filled with the bubble its AFK Target uses; (4) check no character is unassigned (`activity === -1`). [C21] [C22]
  Idle/capped states: banked brew (`attempts > 0`) is not capped in code, but is unrealised value; liquid at cap stops regenerating (Liquids dossier). [C23]
- **AFK Setup implications:** the cauldron assignment is a parallel slot (alongside the AFK Target). Big bubble per AFK target: Mining Wyoming Blood, Fishing Sploosh Sploosh, Choppin Molto Loggo, Catching Bug², anvil Hammer Hammer, Construction Call Me Bob, Trapping Call Me Ash, Worship Call Me Pope, fighting Grind Time / Kill Per Kill / Level Up Gift / Big Game Hunter / Mr Massacre, food burners Bite But Not Chew. A bubble may be equipped on any number of characters at once. [C24] [C25] [C26]
- **Min-max by stage:** see the dedicated section below.
- **Common mistakes:** brew left unspent; no/wrong big bubbles equipped (Toolbox raises `missingBubbles` when equipped < slots); levelling a low-value `decay` bubble past its half-value point while a cheaper strong bubble sits at level 1; never touching Boost Luck; leaving one cauldron without a brewer. [C27] [C28]
- **Save and Toolbox fields:** see the Mechanics catalogue (exact paths per mechanic) and the Fields table at the end.
- **Sources:** inline per claim; wiki and community pages verified 2026-10-19.
