# Dossier: Worship & Tower Defense (M26)

Verified 2026-10-19. Ground truth: IdleOn Toolbox @6c37e3d (game 2.3.540) vendored parsers and data; game-client facts come from the v1.23 client `N.js` fetched by this workflow (offsets shift per patch, names stay stable). ## Worship

- **What it is:** a W3 skill (skill index 9) where each character banks **charge** over time and spends it at a **totem**, either on a one-click WORSHIP claim (EXP plus that totem's Soul) or on a SUMMON that starts a **Tower Defense (TD)** run that pushes the totem's best wave. [C23] [C24]
- **Unlocks:** the first totem (Goblin Gorefest, W1 Forest Outskirts) needs Worship Lv 1; the others unlock by Worship level (10, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80, 100). The Wax Skull is sold in the Frostbite Towndra (W3 town). The quest Hoggindaz4 teaches TD (start a round on the Goblin map with a Wax Skull equipped). A tower is usable once its Construction building is Lv 1+. [C4] [C11] [C36]
- **Scope:** per character: charge, max charge, rate, skull, Worship level/efficiency, equipped prayers. Account-wide: best wave per totem, saved resume wave/points, Souls in Storage, tower levels, prayers unlocked. [C52]
- **Inputs to outputs:** In: hours (charge accrues AFK and active), charge cost per totem, Soul stock for skills/prayers, TD kill points. Out: Worship EXP, Souls, Soul cards (5% per claim), Prayer unlocks at wave milestones, Totalizer/Menagerie inputs. [C1] [C3] [C17] [C41] [C54]
- **Goals and Milestones:**

| Goal | Milestones | Reward | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Totem unlock | Worship Lv 1/10/30/40/50/60/80/100 | next Soul type, better EXP per charge | [C4] |
| Prayer unlock | best wave: Gorefest 10/25/51/81/121; Wakawaka 11/31/71/101; Acorn 21/41/71/111; Frosty 21/51/121; Clash of Cans 21/51/131 | 19 prayers | [C37] |
| Skull tiers | Worship Lv 1/10/25/35/40/50/60/70/90/120/150 | max charge 100 to 6000, speed 4 to 10 | [C9] |
| Total waves | each 10 waves (sum of best waves) | Totalizer bonuses (needs superbit), Tome | [C41] |
| Equinox | wave 50 on first 6 summons; wave 121 on first 6 summons | next upgrade; +200 starting TD pts | [C44] |
| Soul-stack achievements | exactly 1,000 Forest / 10,000 Dune / 250,000 Rooted | arcade balls, +15 TD pts, RNG item | [C46] |

- **Feeds / fed by:** Feeds: Prayers, Alchemy vials and stamps (Souls), Death Note via Fibers of Absence, Construction (Souls), Smithing (skull recipes), Gaming MSA Totalizer, Menagerie. Fed by: Construction towers and Salt Lick, Refinery salts, bubbles, stamps, cards, Post Office, vials, Arcade, Upgrade Vault, Equinox, Dementia set, Species Epoch, Wizard talents. [C22] [C19] [C43] [C42]
- **Per-session actions:** (1) any character with charge >= claim cost: claim at the best EXP-per-charge totem you have played; (2) before leaving, spend charge so it will not cap during the Expected Return (hours to full = (max-current)/rate); (3) Wizard: Syphon everyone's charge into the Wizard first (3600 s cooldown, C62); (4) if a Prayer-unlock wave is within reach, spend one summon; (5) restock Souls for the next skull/prayer. Capped = current >= max (Toolbox alerts at 95%). [C7] [C6] [C13] [C8]
- **AFK Setup implications:** charge accrues whatever the character does, so Worship constrains only the Expected Return (do not exceed hours-to-full) and the Unending Energy prayer (10 h AFK cap curse). The cap is Disputed, confidence M: view A is the curse text (prayers.md C13), view B is that no Toolbox code path enforces it (monster-afk.md C35); keep it as a Check, not a rule, until an in-game test. [C8] [C38] [C40]
- **Min-max by stage:** see the dedicated section below.
- **Common mistakes:** no skull on a character (max charge 50, full in ~38 h); letting charge sit at cap; only the Wizard ever claims although every character has its own charge; Unending Energy equipped on long AFKs (cap Disputed, M); claiming at a low-EXP totem when a better one is unlocked; summoning without a resume save (progress saved only on waves multiple of 10). Earlier research on the W3 test account saw 6/6 characters without a skull and 4/6 at cap. [C6] [C27] [C26] [C51]
- **Save and Toolbox fields:** see Mechanics catalogue field column. `account.totems[]`, `account.towers`, `account.prayers`, `characters[].worship`, `characters[].tools[5]`, `characters[].skillsInfo.worship`. [C53]

## Missed mechanics added after verification

Each item is also in the claim register (C56-C69). Items marked L rest on N.js reports that the data lens could not check (game bundle not vendored).

- **TD kill-point multipliers (C56, L):** Summoning Meritoc bonus 16, Cannoli (1+TDpts/100) and a size multiplier max(.34, 2*SizeMulti-min(SizeMulti+1,4)) multiply kill points on top of C29. Mob HP tiers (SkullTypeHP 4/20/100/500) per C5.
- **Slush Skull low-speed charge branch (C57, H):** Speed<3 uses a slower curve (about 0.60/h at L50). An empty slot reads Blank (Speed 3, lvReqToCraft 0), so an empty slot out-charges Slush; Slush matters for its +250 base damage and as a Dreadnaught recipe ingredient.
- **Skull unique bonuses (C58, H):** Slush +250 base damage, +2% All Stats; Dreadnaught +3% Total Damage, +3% All Stats; Cultist +15% Money; Crystal +4% All AFK Gain, +10% Drop Rate; Prehistoric +6% All AFK Gain, +5% Damage Bonus.
- **Worship efficiency inputs (C59, H):** Le Brain Tools bubble multiplies the skull's contribution to power; Smart Efficiency counts only with the Worship skill-mastery rift bonus; Skill Wiz scales Wisdom; Sooouls talent scales with Forest Soul stock and the Helium atom; statue 16; Right Hand of Action; star signs; Post Office 18a. Skill mastery also adds to Worship EXP (C15).
- **Dementia set (C60, H):** needs 2 tools (the Prickle Worship Skull is one of its tool slots) and 1 weapon; +50% Critters and Souls. Bubba soul gain and Ring of Greed feed the food-group term in C3 (reported by the data lens; not independently traced).
- **Early next-wave call bonus (C61, L):** calling the next wave early awards floor(timer/4)+wave+floor((wave+1)/10) points, capped at 999 (N.js only).
- **Charge Syphon cooldown (C62, H):** 3600 s, cast time 0.95, mana 10.
- **Prayer slot count (C63, L):** depends on best Wizard level (wiki).
- **Giant monsters via Tachion (C64, M):** Tachion_of_the_Titans (max level 2) lets Giant Monsters spawn on kill; spawn rate is N.js-only.
- **Construction build cost (C65, H):** tower cost = buildMultiplier[index] * bonusInc^level; bonusInc per tower: Pulse 1.365, Fireball 1.33, Boulder 1.276, Frozone 1.246, Stormcaller 1.23, Party Starter 1.222, Kraken 1.22, Poisonic 1.21, Voidinator 1.4. In-progress build slots are TowerInfo[54..61] (reported, L).
- **Construction Mastery (C66, H):** rift 40 bonuses by total building levels: 500 Trapper Drone Lv +35, 750 damage 2*floor((total-750)/10), 1000 Talent Library Lv +100, 1250 build speed 5*floor((total-1250)/10), 1500 Shrine Lv +100, 2500 Wizard tower Lv +30 (the 250 threshold in the constants array is unused by the bonus code); refinery speed floor(total/10). Gambit 9 adds to tower max level (base 50). Over-levels (>50) feed wizard damage.
- **Refinery salt per tower (C67, H):** Pulse 2 Redox, Fireball 4 Redox, Boulder 4 Explosive, Frozone 7 Explosive, Stormcaller 5 Spontaneity, Party Starter 9 Spontaneity, Kraken 7 Dioxide Synthesis, Poisonic 10 Purple, Voidinator 20 Nullo.
- **Starting TD points sources (C68, M):** Crab Juice and Calcium Carbonate vials, Dune/Rooted/Deepsea cards, arcade 15, Banked Pts stamp, Post Office 18c, Equinox +200, achievement 'Start with 15 more points'.
- **Unverifiable (C69, L):** bulk worship claim multipliers and W7 research bonuses exist only in N.js.
