## Bubba

All facts below are numbered [Cn]; the Claims register at the end gives each one's Source, tier (1 game data / Toolbox code, 2 Toolbox logic or wiki, 3 wiki, 4 community) and verification date (2026-10-19, game 2.3.540, Toolbox @6c37e3d). Tier numbers in the register follow the harness precedence list given for M26 (1 data/Toolbox code, 2 Toolbox logic/dashboard, 3 wiki, 4 community).

- **What it is:** an account-wide clicker in the Codex (alongside Orion and Poppy). It makes Meat Slices per second, even AFK; you spend them on 28 upgrades, then buy Megaflesh to reset almost everything for a permanent multiplier. Bubba Boon never resets and grants seven permanent account bonuses [C1][C14][C15][C16][C17].
- **Unlocks:** per character, once that character has opened YumYum Grotto's door (map 50, door 0) to World 3; shipped v1.02 2025-11-23, v1.03 (2025-11-29) cut some cost scaling [C25]. Production needs 1st Slice above level 0, and logging in on a character without the door forfeits away time [C26].
- **Scope:** account-wide (one save record `Bubba`). Charisma levels, XP, gifts, dice, smoked meats are per-run and reset at Megaflesh [C15].
- **Inputs to outputs:** meat slices -> upgrades; Megaflesh -> multiplier on Boon bonuses (1.2x/1.4x/1.6x/1.8x then Chromatic) and meat; Boon levels -> Build Rate, Critter Gain, Soul Gain, Total Damage, All Kills, Class EXP, Atom Cost [C17][C18].
- **Feeds / fed by:** feeds Construction (Build Rate), Trapping (critters), Worship (souls), damage/kills, class EXP, Atom Collider cost [C18]. Fed by Poppy fish (Crossover), Pet Arena Crucible (W4 Breeding), and in W5+ Upgrade Vault 65, Sushi, Jelly, Fountain [C6][C28][C29].

### Mechanics catalogue

Fields: raw save `Bubba` = [0] scalars, [1] upgrade levels, [2] prestige, [3] Charisma levels, [4] dice, [5] smoked meats; parsed under `account.bubba` (Toolbox `parsers/clickers/bubba.ts`) [C27].

1. **Meat production.** Per-second rate formula, three flat producers (1st/2nd/3rd Slice +1/+6/+50 per level) times additive percent upgrades (Good/Great/Best Meat +2/+8/+25 per level, Crossover +5 per POW-10 fish) times happiness, dice, smoke, Hustle, Megaflesh 1, Beeg Slice passive, Spare Coins and W4+ multipliers [C5][C6]. Field `account.bubba.meatsliceRate` (per minute), `meatSlices` ([0][0]).
2. **Upgrade unlock gating.** Types open by meat produced this run, MeatProdREQ(t); cap 28 [C2]. Field `totalUpgTypesAvailable`, `progress` ([0][4]), `progressReq`.
3. **Upgrade cost / levels.** Cost formula, caps (999; Fun Gifts 6, More Dice 6, More Sides 9), RealLV = level + prestige [C1][C3][C4]. Field `upgrades[i].level/prestige/cost`.
4. **Discounts.** Bargain (idx4, 1%/lvl), Cost Saver (idx18, 2%/lvl), Perma Sale (idx26, 4%/lvl), Rizz, Minehead opponent 5; each divides cost by (1+x/100). Menagerie cheapener (W7) affects only 1st Slice [C3][C4].
5. **Megaflesh (prestige reset).** Cost ~125x per level (3.16e5 first, 3.68e28 twelfth), 12 rows of rewards, Chromatic stacks beyond 11; resets everything except Boon, Megaflesh, Fun Gifts [C11][C12][C14][C15]. Field `megafleshOwned` = Bubba[1][8], `megaFlesh[]`.
6. **Bubba Boon.** Cost ~10x per level, never resets, seven rotating bonuses (10,5,5,15,1,4,3 % per stack) times Megaflesh multiplier, W6 Companion 51 multiplier [C16][C17]. Field `bonuses.*.bonus`, `upgrades[3].level`.
7. **Boon consumers.** Which Toolbox/game systems read each bonus; Critter Gain is flat critters in game versus percent on wiki (dispute) [C18][C19].
8. **Happiness and pats.** Happi Boi pats/hour, per-pat happiness, decay, hourly timer, Buyer Grin and Real Love feed it, Megaflesh 8 gives 50 free pats [C7][C23]. Fields Bubba[0][1], [0][5], [0][13].
9. **Open Gift and gifts.** Six gifts, Fun Gifts picks (two from MF3), passive and trigger table, Uber Gifts multiplier capped at 5x [C22][C30]. Fields Bubba[0][2], [0][3].
10. **Dice.** Frozen Dice product formula, Dice Roll hourly price, More Dice/More Sides, Loaded Dice luck, Courage, Red Die, MF5 reroll protection [C8]. Field Bubba[4].
11. **Smoker.** Unlock at 16 types; smoked meat tier roll with 16h10m55s quality plateau; five meat tiers multiply 2,3,4,6,10%; 2X Smoke, Slabcakes, MF9 free-roll [C9][C10][C13]. Fields Bubba[0][14], Bubba[0][7], Bubba[5]; `smokerSeconds`.
12. **Charisma Chart.** Six attributes with per-level effects, training by selection, XP requirement, cap 120, Super Chart, emulsify (MF6) 3x [C20][C21]. Fields Bubba[3], [0][6], [0][8], [0][15].
13. **Spare Coins.** Four coin types multiplying meat, Big Ol Coin / Savvy / MF11 raise odds [C24]. Fields Bubba[0][9..12].
14. **Away time.** Added on login at happiness 0, uncapped, per-character door gate [C26].
15. **W4 extension.** Pet Arena Crucible bonus 10 multiplies meat (1+2x) [C5][C28].
16. **W5+ extensions (one line each).** Fountain "Bubba Forever" (W5 Caverns) and Upgrade Vault 65 "Go Go Bubba" (max 500) multiply meat; Sushi 39 and Jelly 58 (W7) multiply meat; Companion 51 gives 3x/5x clicker bonuses; Menagerie Cheapener cheapens 1st Slice; Tome row 108 counts Megaflesh; Equinox "10 Megaflesh" challenge feeds Drop Rate; Button counter 56 reads meat [C28][C29].

17. **Cost-discount stack.** Bargain (4, 1%/lvl, x2 1.12), Cost Saver (18, 2%/lvl, x2 1.23) and Perma Sale (26, 4%/lvl, x2 1.30) each divide cost by (1+x/100) and multiply together with Rizz and Minehead opponent 5 (a W7 account-wide dependency) [C3][C40] (confidence H).
18. **Happiness feeders.** Real Love (20, all happiness x(1+level/100)) and Buyer Grin (5, per-purchase happiness, x1 1.3) feed the per-pat and per-purchase happiness that drive the HappinessBonus multiplier [C23][C41] (confidence H). The displayed rate in game is 60x the per-second figure (Toolbox exposes per minute) [C5][C45] (M).
19. **Coins and dice luck upgrades.** Spare Coins (21) and Big Ol Coin (27) set coin odds; coin tiers Nickel/Quarter/Dollar/(top) are worth +1/+5/+25/+100 percent. Loaded Dice (22) and the Courage attribute raise dice luck; Toolbox does not model luck, only the counted dice values (values above 6 count at 1/2.5) [C24][C42][C43] (H for tiers and counting, L for exact luck effect).
20. **2X Smoke.** Upgrade 25 gives a chance to double smoked meat when curing; timers Bubba[0][14] plus pending [0][7] [C9][C10][C44] (H for existence, L for exact probability).
21. **Chromatic detail.** Row 12 value = 2+(MF-12)/5 and its count = owned-11, stacking per copy past 11 [C14][C46] (H).
22. **Meat-slice hooks.** Meat Slice count Bubba[0][0] and progress Bubba[0][4] drive unlocks; Button Machine challenge 56 reads meat slices and the Equinox 10-Megaflesh challenge are W7 hooks [C29][C45] (H).

### Per-session actions

Check: is 1st Slice above 0; can the next Megaflesh be bought (spend leftover on Boon first); Smoker past 58,254 s; Charisma selection not on a maxed attribute; hourly pats then Open Gift if Beeg Slice is picked [C15][C10][C21][C36].

### AFK Setup implications

None per character; just log in on a character that has opened the door so away time is credited [C26].

### Min-max by stage

Bubba is World 3 content, so W1 and W2 have nothing to do except reaching the door.

- **W1 early / mid / late and W2 (all):** locked. Goal: open YumYum Grotto's door at the end of W2 [C25].
- **W3 early (Megaflesh 0-3, meat rate up to about 10M/min):** buy 1st Slice, then Bargain and Buyer Grin first, then meat production [C34]; Boon to the first levels as it becomes cheap; Megaflesh as soon as affordable; target 10M/min by MF3 [C31]; gifts 1 and 2 [C32]; Hustle and Rizz both about 45 and Joy 15 at MF3 (Hustle-first vs Rizz-first contested, confidence L; the "Rizz 45" reading is confirmed) [C31][C35][C38].
- **W3 mid (MF4-5, 500M to 30B/min):** gifts 1 and 3 (the switch to gift 5 comes at MF7); Hustle and Rizz to max, Joy 27-50; More Dice at MF4, Smoker and Super Chart at MF5 [C31][C32][C34]; roll dice hourly [C34]; Smoker claims every 7-16 h, but the quality plateau is 16h11m [C33][C10].
- **W3 late (MF6-8, 600B to 3Q/min):** Mindful first (agreed, confidence M), emulsify Mindful [C35][C31]; target Hustle 80/Rizz 100/Joy 30/Mindful 120 [C31]; gifts 1 and 5 from MF8, Uber Gifts and the 3-to-5 gift switch at MF7 [C32]; hourly pat-then-Open-Gift (Pulse) or save pats for the final push (GriffyBit) [C36]; mid-run Boon farming when Boon is far below Megaflesh cost [C36].
- **W4 and beyond (MF9+):** add Savvy 10-20, MF9 free Smoker rolls, MF10 x1.80; keep Boon past 7 climbing, the stacks continue the 7-cycle [C31][C13][C17]; Pet Arena Crucible and later W5-W7 multipliers [C28][C29].
- **Priority order within a run (ours, from the existing KB):** restart 1st Slice, Megaflesh, Boon, production, openers, discounts if they pay back, rest. This is our own rule, not a published source order [C39].

### Common mistakes

Training a maxed Charisma attribute (XP banks then is wiped by Megaflesh) [C21][C15]; claiming Smoker before a Megaflesh (smoked meat is cleared) [C15]; forgetting 1st Slice after reset [C26]; believing Charisma gives account bonuses (it only boosts Bubba) [C20]; logging in on a character without the door [C26]; trusting guides older than 2025-11-29 on costs [C25].
