## Refinery

Verified 2026-10-19 against Toolbox @6c37e3d (game 2.3.540). T = source tier (1 game data/Toolbox code, 2 wiki, 3 Toolbox logic/dashboard, 4 community). Paths are relative to `packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/` unless noted.

- **What it is:** a W3 building (Frostbite Tundra, left of the Construction Table) that turns W1-W3 materials into chained "salts" (nine catalogue entries; Anionic is a placeholder that `liveEntries` filters, so the parsed Refinery has eight live salts) (H). Each salt makes Power every cycle; a full Power bar is Refined to rank the salt up. Salts pay for Construction builds, the Salt Lick, vials and more. [C1][C2]
- **Unlocks:** W3. Salt 1 (Redox) is available; salt N+1 unlocks by paying a count of salt N from the storage chest (not Refinery storage): 40, 150, 300, 500, 750, 10K, 100K, 1M for salts 2-9 (salt 9 Anionic is a placeholder, not live in parsed data). (M: tier-1 label rests on authored KB file citing N.js, not Toolbox) Salts 7-9 sit in the Polymerization tab, gated by Research grid square 49. [C3][C4][C5]
- **Scope:** account-wide (one Refinery). Only Squire/Divine Knight characters add per-character effects (Refinery Throttle cooldown). [C6]
- **Inputs -> outputs:** inputs are per-cycle materials plus the previous salt; outputs are Power (becomes salt stock on Refine) and rank. Salt stock feeds buildings, Salt Lick, vials, Death Note. [C7][C8]
- **Goals and Milestones:**

| Milestone | Reward | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Refine any salt (task "Dying To Refine", breakpoint 1) | task credit (H) | [C9] |
| "Why so Salty?" total salts refined 25 / 150 / 600 / 2500 / 7000 / 25000 / 100000 / 200000 / 750000 | task rewards (H) | [C9] |
| W3 Taskboard merit `tasks[2][2][6]` | salts at index <= level cost rank^1.3 not rank^1.5 in the Refinery-salt component (H); merit has 5 levels at 2 merits each, +1 salt type per purchase | [C10] |
| Rank 22+ | PowerCAP reaches 1,000,000 (list value, cap index min(rank,24)); rank 14 = 200K (H) | [C11] |
| Power per cycle hits 250,000 (rank ~14,200 w/o companion; ceil(250000^(1/1.3)); earlier draft said ~3,740, corrected: 3,740^1.3 is only ~44,130) (H) | output stops growing; further ranks only raise cost | [C12] |
| Total salt ranks (first 6 salts) | Tome quantity; Squire Precision Power damage per rank (H) | [C13][C14] |

- **Feeds / fed by:** fed by printer samples (printer should print salt-cost materials), Trapping (Scorpie, Mousey, Tuttle), Worship souls (Dune, Frigid, Breezy, Deepsea), fish, bugs, bars, lumber. Feeds Construction (build costs in salts; Redox Rates talent), Salt Lick, Alchemy vials (Red Malt etc.), Death Note (Explosive Salts). [C7][C15]
- **Per-session actions:** collect Power and Refine full bars by hand; check fuel runway (Toolbox `getSaltMatsTimeLeft`); switch on idle unlocked salts; unlock next salt when stock allows; use Squire Refinery Throttle when off cooldown. Idle state: unlocked salt with `active=0`, or fuel exhausted. Capped state: bar full with Auto Refine off. [C16][C17]
- **AFK Setup implications:** the Refinery runs offline regardless of AFK target, so before a long stretch top up fuel and refine full bars; bar fill is bounded by remaining room, so a refined empty bar maximises offline gain. Fuel depletion halts all production. [C16][C18]
- **Min-max by stage:** see section below.
- **Common mistakes:** Auto Refine left on (KB V1 says it never ranks up; **Disputed**, see mechanic 8, L); salts left off; fuel runs out overnight; ranking a salt past what the previous salt/printer can supply; unlocking the next salt with stock sitting in Refinery storage (unlock draws from chest only). [C17][C19][C20]
- **Save and Toolbox fields:** `account.refinery.{salts[],refinerySaltTaskLevel,timePastCombustion,timePastSynthesis,timePastPolymerize,totalLevels,refineryStorage}`; Toolbox-computed `getRefineryCycleBonuses`, `getRefineryCycles`, `getSaltsBalance`, `getSaltMatsTimeLeft`, `calcTimeToRankUp`. ActiveOn: `account.refinery` (`depth-types.ts RefinerySalt`), KB `knowledge/systems/refinery.yaml` + `refinery.facts.yaml`, detectors `refinery/{fuel-runs-out,salt-off,auto-refine,full-bar,unlock-next}`. [C21]

### Mechanics catalogue

1. **Raw save layout.** `Refinery[0]=[unlockedSaltCount,timePastCombustion,timePastSynthesis,timePastPolymerize]`; `Refinery[1]`/`[2]` = names/amounts of salts parked in Refinery storage ("Blank" = empty); `Refinery[3+i]=[refined,rank,_,active,autoRefinePercentage]` for salt i (0-based). Parser: `parsers/world-3/refinery.ts` `parseRefinery`. Refinery storage slots count as owned salt for Salt Lick and Refinery costs. `refineryTabsOwned` = 2 + Research square 49. (H) [C22]
2. **Salt chain and recipes (data `refinery`, website-data/index.js; shared-data).** Redox: 10 Spore Cap + 5 Copper Ore. Explosive: 10 Bullfrog Horn, 5 Forest Fibres, 2 Redox. Spontaneity: 50 Pocket Sand, 30 Goldfish, 40 Fly, 2 Explosive. Dioxide: 10 Floof Ploof, 2 Dune Soul, 1 Scorpie, 1 Spontaneity. Purple: 25 Cracked Glass, 5 Bloach, 5 Sentient Cereal, 5 Mousey, 2 Dioxide. Nullo: 50 Contact Lense, 5 Void Bar, 5 Wispy Lumber, 5 Flycicle, 5 Frigid Soul, 2 Purple. Crosslink (W5): 10 Light Lantern, 2 Breezy Soul, 1 Tuttle, 1 Nullo. Propagated (W6/7): 25 Equinox Flesh, 5 Caulifish, 5 Litterbug, 5 Prehistrium Bar, 2 Crosslink. Anionic (W7 placeholder, "Not Yet" filler): 50 Magmaspike, 3x5 FillerMaterial, 5 Deepsea Soul, 2 Propagated. Quantities are multiplied per cycle by the cost formula. Salt names: Dioxide_Synthesis, Purple_Salt, Nullo_Salt. (H) [C23]
3. **Per-cycle cost.** `floor(rank^e) * quantity`, e = 1.3 for a Refinery-salt component when salt index <= `refinerySaltTaskLevel`, else 1.5. `calcCost`, `hasMissingMats`. Wiki describes the salt merit as a power-per-cycle multiplier; **Disputed**, code view (cost exponent) wins at tier 1; wiki view recorded, confidence M on code. Merit text: 'Refinery Salt Costs don't scale beyond power output, +1 Salt Type Per Purchase'. [C10]
4. **Power per cycle.** `floor(min(250000, rank^1.3 * (1 + companion35)))`; companion 35 bonus only if active. `getPowerPerCycle`. Max useful rank = ceil((250000/(1+c))^(1/1.3)) (`getMaxUsefulRank`; ~14,200 with no companion). Wiki puts the floor on Rank^1.3 alone; minor. (H) [C12]
5. **PowerCAP.** `randomList[18][min(rank,len-2)]`, floored at 25: 50,50,200,800,3000,8000,14000,20000,30000,40000,50000,65000,80000,100000,200000 at rank 14 then +100K steps ..900000, 1,000,000 from rank 22 on. Refine at power >= cap ranks up (game N.js @7640085, per KB note). `getPowerCap`. (H for list; M for N.js rank-up claim, unverifiable here) [C11][C17]
6. **Base cycle times.** Combustion (salts 1-3, index<=2) 900 s; Synthesis (salts 4-6, index<=5) 3600 s; Polymerize (salts 7-9) 14400*25 = 360,000 s (100 h). Time = base / ((1+speed%/100) * labTubing * (1+legend%/100)); polymerize additionally / (1+(Materials Science + Tasty Treat)/100); ceil to whole seconds (matters at high speed). (H) `REFINERY_BASE_CYCLE_TIMES`, `computeRefineryCycleTimes`. [C24]
7. **Speed sources (additive percent).** Red Malt vial (`Refinery_Cycle_Speed`, stat RefSpd), Salt Lick upgrade index 2 (2% per level, max 100 levels = 200%), Family bonus (Divine Knight, amplified by best Family Guy), Pipe Gauge sigil, Refinery stamp ("Faster refinery cycles", Stamp C22, needs Cheesy Crumbs), shiny pet "Faster Refinery Speed", Construction Mastery (floor(totalBuildingLevels/10)% from Rift 40), Arcade "Refinery Speed", Vote bonus 33, Research square 49 "Polymer Refinery" (+5% per level, max 2). Multipliers: Lab jewel "Gilded Cyclical Tubing" x3 when active; Legend talent 19. Polymerize only: Research square 48 "Materials Science" (25% per level, max 4), meal "Tasty Treat" (PolyRefSpd). `getRefineryCycleBonuses`. [C25]
8. **Auto Refine.** `autoRefinePercentage` slot 4. Per KB game-code notes: the auto pass sets Power to 0 (turns it into salt) and never ranks up; only a manual Refine at a full bar ranks up. **Disputed:** KB game-code view (auto pass never ranks up; L, N.js not accessible to verifiers) vs wiki Changelog v2.41 (2025-07-06): Auto Refine became an ON/OFF toggle that refines whenever there is any Power (M; pre-patch tension, KB notes may predate it). The save slot is still named `autoRefinePercentage` and Toolbox does not describe its semantics. Correction: do not assert 'never ranks up' as fact. [C17][C26]
9. **Active toggle.** slot 3; an inactive salt consumes and makes nothing. Production only continues while fuel stock lasts. [C16]
10. **Offline fill.** Away time fills at most the room left in the bar (KB note citing game code). [C18]
11. **Salt balance model.** Each salt is fuelled by its predecessor; `getSaltsBalance` compares output/h vs next salt's consumption/h and printer supply to compute `maxSafeRank`. `solveMaxRank`. Also `calcResourceToRankUp` = (cap - refined)/powerPerCycle * itemCost, the materials needed to finish a rank (dashboard). (M: formula not re-checked in detail) [C27]
12. **Time to rank up.** cyclesLeft = round((cap - refined)/powerPerCycle); cycles/day = 86400/cycleTime + Squire throttle cycles. `calcTimeToRankUp`. (M: round() formula not re-checked) [C28]
13. **Squire Refinery Throttle (talent 130, "REFINERY_THROTTLE").** Triggers extra Refinery cycles (cycles = 3 + floor(talentLevel/8): x1=3, x2=8, intervalAdd; cooldown 72000; earlier draft omitted the base 3) (H); cooldown read from `cooldowns[130]`; still costs materials. Divine Knight inherits. Throttle cycles sum across all Squire/Divine Knight characters; remaining cooldown = saved `cooldowns[130]` minus time since AFK start. (H) [C6][C29]
14. **Squire Redox Rates (talent 131).** Construction build speed multiplier = 1 + talentBonus*(Helium atom 'Talent Power Stacker' + lavaLog(Redox Salts in storage + Refinery storage))/100. Cross-system. `character.ts#L3681-3685`. (H; helium atom term added) [C30]
15. **Precision Power (talent 125).** +% damage per total Refinery rank (first 6 salts) when accuracy is high enough. `damage.ts#L706`. [C14]
16. **Salt Lick (Construction building; spends salts).** 11 upgrades in data (index: cost salt, effect, base cost, increment, base bonus, max level): 0 Redox: printer samples +0.5%/lv (20); 1 Redox: obol slots (8); 2 Explosive: +2% Refinery speed/lv (100); 3 Explosive: +0.2% class/skill EXP (100); 4 Spontaneity: +2 talent book max level (10); 5 Spontaneity: +1% liquid rate/capacity (100); 6 Dioxide: +5% worship TD points (10); 7 Dioxide: +0.4% move speed (25); 8 Purple: +3% multikill (10); 9 Purple: +0.1% total damage (250); 10 Nullo: +1% cooking mastery EXP (100). Each Salt Lick building level reveals one more upgrade (`TowerInfo[towers.Salt_Lick.index]` = unlockedCount; upgrade i is available when i < unlockedCount; level capped at maxLevel); owned salts for Salt Lick costs include the logged-in character's inventory plus storage plus Refinery storage. (H) Slot 0 gives printer sample size; slot 1 gives obol slots. Parser `world-3/saltLick.ts`, data `shared-data.json saltLicks`. [C31]
17. **Salt unlock costs.** From storage chest: Refinery2 40 Redox, 3: 150 Explosive, 4: 300 Spontaneity, 5: 500 Dioxide, 6: 750 Purple, 7: 10K Nullo, 8: 100K Crosslink, 9: 1M Propagated. [C3][C4]
18. **Printer interaction.** Salt-cost materials are excluded from printer atom alerts; printed output is a rank limiter in `getSaltsBalance`. `printer.ts#L204-217`; only printing slots count in `getSaltsBalance`. (H) [C27]
19. **Tome / achievement hooks.** `refinery.totalLevels` is a Tome quantity (`tome.ts#L332`). [C13]
20. **Merit.** W3 Taskboard "Salt" merit lowers cost exponent for Refinery-salt components (see 3). `tasks[2][2][6]`. [C10]
21. **W5+ extensions (one line each).** Polymerization tab (salts 7-9) via Research square 49; Crosslink/Propagated recipes use W5-W6 materials; Anionic (salt 9) is a placeholder in current data; Research squares 48/49 and the Tasty Treat meal speed polymerize; Rift/Construction mastery speed bonus needs Rift 40; Legend talent 19 and Lab tubing multiply all cycles. [C5][C24][C25]

### Min-max by stage

- **W1 early/mid/late:** nothing to do; Refinery does not exist. Stockpile Copper Ore and Spore Caps for Redox. (inferred) [C32]
- **W2:** nothing in the Refinery itself; Alchemy vial Red Malt needs Redox, so finishing W2 vials is a reason to get salts early. [C25]
- **W3 early:** build Refinery access, run Redox and Explosive ASAP (builds and the Death Note first level are paid in them); keep Auto Refine OFF and manually Refine full bars; switch on every unlocked salt. Wiki: "There is no benefit to not Refining the Salt". (H) [C1][C17][C33]
- **W3 mid:** unlock Spontaneity then Dioxide (300 Spontaneity); Wiki: efficiency drops sharply after rank 2 (rank 3 = 5x cost for 4x power), so do not chase rank on early salts; put points in Salt Lick Refinery speed (Explosive) first. Hunter: trap Scorpie, Mousey, Tuttle for Dioxide/Purple/Crosslink. Printer: print salt materials. (M; priority order is inferred from efficiency math, no community upgrade-order source found) [C34][C31][C27]
- **W3 late:** Purple and Nullo; max Salt Lick Refinery speed; Squire with Refinery Throttle; do not rank a salt beyond `saltMaxSafeRank` (output of the previous salt). Rank 22+: PowerCAP saturates at 1M (H); wiki's 'ranking gets faster' wording was not seen in fetched text (L). [C27][C35]
- **W4:** stack speed: Red Malt vial, Refinery stamp, Pipe Gauge sigil, Arcade, shinies, Construction Mastery (Rift 40), Divine Knight family bonus, Lab Gilded Cyclical Tubing (x3, big). Nullo feeds Salt Lick cooking-mastery upgrade (Rift 61 feature). (M) [C25][C31]
- **W5+ outline:** Polymerization via Research square 49; Materials Science and Tasty Treat for the 100 h cycle. [C24]
- **Per class:** Squire/Divine Knight only (Throttle, Redox Rates). [C6]

Community meta caveat: searches found no public upgrade-order guide specific to the Refinery; tier-4 coverage is thin. [C36]

### Disagreements recorded

- Wiki lists Dioxide, Purple, Nullo, Crosslink and Propagated under Synthesis (1 h); Toolbox puts salts at index > 2 on Synthesis only up to index 5 (Dioxide, Purple, Nullo) and index 6+ (Crosslink onward) on the Polymerize (100 h base) timer. Tier 1 wins; confidence M that wiki text is stale. [C24][C2]
- Wiki says "FLOOR(Rank^1.3) x (1 + Panda_Pet_Owned)"; Toolbox applies companion 35 inside the floor. Same quantity, H. [C12]
- Third-party Salt Lick table (DigitalTQ) names non-salt costs for some upgrades (Froge, Dune Soul, Mousey, Frigid Soul, Pingy) whereas Toolbox data keys all upgrades by a salt `rawName`. Likely the data's rawName is only the first/display cost; unresolved (L). Verifier note: DigitalTQ lists 10 upgrades, omitting the cooking-mastery one Toolbox has. [C31][C37]

- **Disputed (auto refine):** see mechanic 8; KB game-code note vs wiki v2.41 toggle. Confidence L on both.
- Wiki Salt Merit (power-per-cycle multiplier) vs Toolbox cost-exponent model: code view used. M.
