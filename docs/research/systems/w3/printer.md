## 3D Printer

Verified 2026-10-19 against Toolbox @6c37e3d (game 2.3.540) and the live game code N.js (2.3.540). T = source tier (1 game data/code, 2 wiki, 3 Toolbox logic/spec, 4 community). Paths relative to `packages/toolbox-parsers/vendor/` unless stated. Each [Cn] resolves in the Claims register at the end. Revised after two adversarial verdict sets: every claim now carries a confidence (H/M/L); items marked **Disputed** carry both views. Community (tier 4) coverage is thin; see Open questions.

- **What it is:** a W3 Construction building plus a Star Talent. A character uses Printer Sampling to record a fraction of what it is AFKing; the printer then produces that amount per hour, on top of the character's normal AFK, with account-wide and per-character multipliers. It is a Parallel Slot: it runs next to the AFK Target. [C1][C7][C8][C26]
- **Unlocks:** the first Construction space (3 Redox Salts, one Player Slot per building level, data maxLevel 11, wiki says 10). Printer Sampling comes from the Hoggindaz quest line (book from Hoggindaz3, which needs the printer built) and Frostbite Towndra. Gem Shop: Crystal 3D Printer (875 gems) and More Sample Spaces (250 +40 each, 6 max). [C1][C2][C6][C13][C14]
- **Scope:** per-character samples and slots (only characters whose slot is below the building level print), account-wide multiplier, gem-bought slots and day counters. [C4][C5][C19]
- **Inputs -> outputs:** input is a sample (a resource the character was AFKing, size = its normal hourly gain x sample %). Output is that item per hour per active slot x printer multi x Wired In / Harriep, delivered to the Storage Chest, plus Atom Collider fuel in Toolbox's model. [C8][C10][C19][C26][C35]
- **Goals and Milestones:**

- **Feeds / fed by:** fed by AFK gain rate, kill rate, drop rarity (fighting), Skill Mastery, Divine Knight Remembrance Orb kills, Sailing Gold Relic, Vote, Winter event, Companion, Compass, Lab Wired In, Divinity Harriep/Arctis. Feeds Refinery salt costs, Construction, Smithing/Anvil bars, Death Note and Walkthrough Demand, Atom Collider, Tome, Equinox challenges, W7 Button task. [C19][C26][C35][C36][C37][C42]
- **Per-session actions:** check for Blank active/stored sample slots; swap stored samples to match Demand (resets nothing); re-sample only when the new size is worth losing the day-counter bonus; use Printer Go Brr (Maestro) when off cooldown; confirm the sample talent is on the attack bar before AFKing. Idle state: an active slot is Blank; capped state: item stock at or above Atom threshold with nothing needed. [C15][C16][C27]
- **AFK Setup implications:** a sample is taken from the character's current AFK Target, so the AFK Target at sample time defines the item and size (bigger when kills/h and AFK gains are high). A character AFK in the Laboratory (or with Arctis) prints 2x and with Harriep 3x/6x, so Lab parking plus printer slots compound. Printer Sampling must be on the bar when the sample is taken but is not an attack. [C8][C26]
- **Min-max by stage:** see below.
- **Common mistakes:** sampling often and wiping Gold Relic/Winter/Legend day counters; sampling with low AFK gains or wrong AFK Target; leaving a stored/active slot Blank; sample not retaken after big level, gear or bubble gains; equipping The Royal Sampler without knowing the EXP curse; printing materials already in surplus (or atom-excluded items) instead of Demand; assuming Toolbox samplePct equals the game's (it ignores the 90% cap). [C10][C12][C15][C16][C31][C35][C38]
- **Save and Toolbox fields:** see Mechanics catalogue. ActiveOn: `account.printer` (`PrinterState`), KB `printer.check.slot-plan`, gem-shop printer checks. [C41]

### Mechanics catalogue

1. **Building and Player Slots.** `towers.3D_Printer`: cost 3 Redox x costInc [1,30], maxLevel 11, +1 Player Slot per level. Player i prints only if i < building level (UI paginates 6 characters per page). Field: `account.towers.data["3D_Printer"]` / `account.printer.level`; raw `TowerInfo[0]`. [C1][C2][C4]
2. **Stored sample slots.** 4 + GemItemsPurchased[112] (max 10). Fields: `Print` (first 5 pairs/char) and `PrinterXtra` (10 elements/char, extra pairs). Full slots reject new samples. [C5][C7][C15]
3. **Active (printing) slots.** 1 + GemItemsPurchased[111] (max 2). Last 4 elements of the character's list; Toolbox sets `active: true` there. [C5][C7]
4. **Gem Shop purchases.** Crystal 3D Printer 875 gems (idx 111, max 1); More Sample Spaces 250 gems +40/purchase, max 6 (idx 112): total 250+290+330+370+410+450 = 2,100 gems for all six. Field: `account.gemShopPurchases[111,112]`. [C6]
5. **Sampling action.** Printer Sampling (talent 635) on the attack bar: 2 s cooldown, 1 mana. Talent bonus 10 + 0.075/level (bigBase), max level 100. Field: `characters[].flatStarTalents` `PRINTER_SAMPLING`. [C13]
6. **Sample item and base size.** By AFK type (fighting drop-row rule, mining/choppin/catching node, fishing random pool, spelunking). Base size = hourly gain of that activity. [C8][C9]
7. **Sample size bonus (percent, cap 90%).** Sum of the 13 terms in C11, divided by 100, min 0.9; applied to base size. Toolbox: `characters[].printerSample` (`getPrinterSampleRate`, no cap). [C10][C11][C12]
8. **Sample size sources, formulas.** Printer Sampling talent 10+0.075L; Salt Lick Redox 0.5%/level (max 20); gear stat 60; Sample It 12L/(40+L); Super Samples 9L/(75+L); achievement 158 +1; Snow Slurry +0.5/level; Royal Sampler +13.5+1.5/level (level max 20, EXP curse); Stample 4L/(30+L); Amplestample 5L/(30+L); Merit +0.5 per buy (cap 5); Maestro family 6L/(100+L) (cap 5); Arcade 4L/(100+L); Utilitarian Capsule 5L/(200+L). [C11][C28]-[C33]
9. **Printer multiplier.** Product formula in C19. Toolbox: `getPrinterMulti(account, characters)` returns `{value, params, breakdown}`; per-sample `boostedValue = value x multi x WiredIn x Harriep`. Field: `account.printer[c][].boostedValue`. [C19]
10. **Day-counter growth.** accountOptions[125] (Gold Relic days), [323] (winter event days), [354] (Companion days), [364] (Compass days), [479] (Legend talent days, not applied), [138] (Remembrance Orb kills). Sampling resets [125], [323], [479]; [354] and [364] are not reset in the sampler code. Gold Relic +2%/day (more with forms; Toolbox applies no day cap to opt 125, the game's 40-160 day cap per form is only in description text, confidence M); Companion 1-2%/day; Moon of Print 0.02%/level/day. [C16][C19][C20][C24][C25]
11. **Per-character multipliers.** Wired In 2x (Lab Mainframe index 1, requires the bonus active and the character AFK in Laboratory OR counting as Arctis-major active), Harriep major 3x, both 6x. Harriep's major can reach everyone via King Doot, a pocket divinity or Coral Kid (divinity.ts:363-373, 392); a pocket Arctis puts everyone in the lab. Omniphau's major (AFK claims also progress 3D Printer by AFK time) can advance printer progress (shared-data.json:11476, T1, M; the accrued quantity is not specified). `account.lab.labBonuses` Wired_In. [C26][C39][C44]
12. **Skill Mastery and charm.** Shared helper `getSkillMasteryBonusByIndex(...,3)` starts its sum at 7 (reduce init in misc.ts) and adds 1 per skill (18) whose Rift mastery tier is unlocked, plus Lolly Flower pristine charm 25 (Sneaking W6, NjTrP15, held down in the Lobby to add to the collection), additive inside one factor. Whether the +7 base is a real printer bonus or a helper constant is weakly supported (confidence L). Skill Mastery unlock is a Rift bonus; the per-skill threshold is level 750 per wiki (T2, M). [C22]
13. **Vote and Event.** Vote index 11 (1.2x per wiki, unverified, L); Winter-event shop bonus 4 'Supreme Wiring' +2% output per day, counter accountOptions[323], factor is 1 + 2 x opt323 x eventShop(4)/100 (the 2 is in the formula); how the event shop bonus is bought/earned and how opt 323 accrues are not in the data (L). [C23]
14. **King of the Remembered and Orb of Remembrance.** 1 + L x log10(orb kills)/100 (Divine Knight talent 178, x1=5 x2=150 decay). The counter is accountOptions[138] (kills shown above the Orb); Orb of Remembrance (Divine Knight talent 168, 15 mana, 160 s cooldown, +1% EXP/drop per kill shown) is presumed to feed it, but the accrual rule is not in Toolbox or data (confidence L). [C21][C43]
15. **Printer Go Brr.** Maestro-only talent 32 (5 mana, 0.45 s cast per talents.json): instant x hours of samples, no-CD chance, cooldown 82000 s; Toolbox `relevantTalents[32]`. [C27]
16. **Blank slots and the Royal Sampler lock.** A sample goes into the first Blank stored slot; with none free it fails and resets nothing. The Royal Sampler prayer cannot be unequipped while any sample exists (curse text 'Remove all samples to Unequip'), so clear samples first. [C15][C31]
16b. **Re-sample rule and reset.** Keep larger value; always reset counters on success; Hoggindaz5 progress ticks on first sample. [C16][C17]
17. **Largest-sample records.** accountOptions[211-215]: Oak Log, Copper, Spore Cap, Goldfish, Fly; Tome quantities. [C18]
18. **Atoms and exclusions.** Toolbox `calcTotals`/`calcAtoms`; thresholds 15M/25M/100M/250M/1.05B by accountOptions[133]; `getPrinterExclusions`. [C35]
19. **Taskboard and Equinox hooks.** Printer go Brrrr task; 1M/1B/Red Frisbee equinox challenges. [C34][C36]
20. **Prayer: The Royal Sampler.** Equipped prayer, cursed with EXP loss and cannot be unequipped while samples exist. [C31]
21. **W5+ extensions (one line each).** W5: Gold Relic (Sailing artifact forms), Harriep/Arctis (Divinity), Biggole Mole companion; W6: Lolly Flower charm and Bonus Ballot vote; W7: Moon of Print (Compass), Supreme Wiring event, Spelunking samples, Legend talent Yet Another Printer Multi (display only), Button Task for a Copper sample, Menagerie reads the Copper sample; Minehead uses Silicon atom. [C20][C22]-[C25][C37]

### Min-max by stage

No community upgrade order for the printer was found with the tools available (search budget exhausted, Reddit blocked). The orders below are tier-1/2-derived: they rank each lever by size per cost and are flagged inferred. Confidence M unless noted.

- **W1 early/mid/late:** the printer does not exist; keep skilling and AFK gains high because sample size scales off hourly gain at sample time. Bank Gem Shop gems toward 875 for the Crystal 3D Printer. [C8][C40]
- **W2 (late):** vials/bubbles worth leveling for later: Sample It (Kazam), Snow Slurry vial, Stample/Amplestample stamps as they appear. (inferred) [C29][C30]
- **W3 early:** build the 3D Printer (3 Redox), finish Hoggindaz to get Printer Sampling, buy Crystal 3D Printer first (community "agreed buy", H), then More Sample Spaces. Sample your highest-gain AFK target on each character that has a slot; print the materials the Walkthrough Demand needs (the existing M14 plan). Do the first sample on a high-AFK-gain character. [C1][C6][C14][C40][C41]
- **W3 mid:** raise building level to cover all characters; add Salt Lick Redox (cheap, 20 levels) and Stample stamps; buy Merit +0.5% to cap 10 purchases; re-sample after major AFK-gain jumps. Print refinery salt materials (Spore Cap, Copper, Fly, Goldfish, etc.) for the Refinery. (inferred) [C28][C29][C33][C42]
- **W3 late:** push sample size toward the 90% cap (stamps, Sample It, Super Samples on a Squire, Maestro family, Arcade, Utilitarian Capsule, achievement 158); do not exceed 90 because it is wasted. Buy remaining sample spaces. [C10][C11]
- **W4:** Lab Wired In and parking characters in the Laboratory doubles their output; Skill Mastery (+1% each at 750 via Rift) and the Arcade slot; Death Note kills feed monster-material samples; use Voidwalker speedrun for monster samples (wiki). [C22][C26][C38]
- **W5+ outline:** Gold Relic/Remembrance Orb/Harriep/Arctis multipliers; avoid needless re-samples because Gold Relic, Winter, Legend counters reset. Take big samples when counters are low anyway. [C16][C20][C21][C26]
- **Per class:** Maestro (Printer Go Brr, family sample size), Squire (Super Samples), Divine Knight (King of the Remembered), anyone with Printer Sampling. [C21][C27][C31]
